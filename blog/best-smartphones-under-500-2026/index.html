Best Smartphones Under $500 in 2026

Tech Editor July 14, 2026 10 min read
GoogleSamsungNothingBuying Guide

You no longer need to spend flagship money to get a genuinely good phone. This guide ranks the best smartphones under $500 in 2026, phones with strong cameras, smooth displays, and dependable performance that borrow real flagship features without the flagship price.

The under-$500 category has improved more than almost any other price bracket over the past couple of years. Chipsets that used to be reserved for $700-plus phones now show up in $350 devices, camera processing software has trickled down from flagship phones, and even budget-focused brands are now shipping 120Hz displays and 5G as standard rather than a premium extra.

Best Overall: Google Pixel 10a

The Pixel 10a is our top pick under $500 for a simple reason: Google continues to bring flagship-adjacent camera processing down to a genuinely affordable price. Starting at roughly $499, it delivers dependable, natural-looking daylight and low-light photos that punch well above what you'd expect at this price, alongside Google's clean, fast version of Android.

Its AI feature set doesn't fully match the Pixel 10 Pro's Gemini-powered tools, and its chipset is a step down from flagship silicon, but for buyers who want long-term software support and a dependable everyday camera without paying flagship prices, it remains one of the safest picks in this entire guide.

Best for Long-Term Support: Samsung Galaxy A56 5G

The Galaxy A56 5G brings Samsung's camera processing and, crucially, Samsung's software update commitment down to a more accessible price. At $449, it delivers reliable, natural-looking daylight photos and benefits from Samsung's typically long update policy, making it a dependable choice for buyers who plan to keep their phone for several years without falling behind on security patches.

Best Distinctive Design: Nothing Phone (3a)

The Nothing Phone (3a) continues to stand out from an increasingly similar-looking crowd of budget Android phones. Its transparent design and signature Glyph lighting pair with genuinely solid specs for its roughly $379 starting price, including a large 5,000mAh battery and a bright 120Hz OLED display. For buyers who want a phone that doesn't blend in, it remains one of the more interesting picks under $500.

Best Tight Budget with 5G: Realme 13 5G

The Realme 13 5G sits well under the $500 ceiling, at around $150, while still offering a genuinely usable MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset, a smooth 120Hz display, and a 50MP stabilized main camera. For buyers whose budget is tighter than $500 but who still want confirmed, reliable 5G connectivity rather than a phone that only lists 5G on paper, it remains one of the strongest options available.

Best Smooth Display on a Budget: Infinix GT 20 Pro

The Infinix GT 20 Pro remains one of the sharpest budget picks on the market. Its 144Hz AMOLED display is unusual at this price point, and its Dimensity 8200 chipset comfortably handles everyday tasks and casual gaming. At $299, it delivers more display and performance for the money than almost anything else in this range.

Best Battery Life Under $500: Moto G Power (2026)

The Moto G Power (2026) rounds out this guide as the strongest pick specifically for battery life. Its 6,720mAh battery lasted over 27 hours in independent Wi-Fi testing, comfortably covering two full days of normal use, at a price around $300. For buyers whose top priority is never running out of charge, it's difficult to beat at this price.

Editor's Note At this price point, software update length matters just as much as day-one specs. A phone that stops receiving security patches after two years can end up costing more over time than a slightly pricier phone with longer support.

How to Choose the Right Phone for You

If camera quality is your top priority, the Google Pixel 10a is the clearest choice under $500. If you want the longest software update commitment, the Samsung Galaxy A56 5G is the safer long-term pick. If you want a phone that looks and feels different from everything else on the market, the Nothing Phone (3a) is worth a look, and if your budget is closer to $150 to $300, the Realme 13 5G and Infinix GT 20 Pro both prove that a genuinely capable phone doesn't require spending anywhere close to $500.

For a broader look at flagship options if your budget stretches further, our best Android phones of 2026 ranking and best battery life smartphones of 2026 guide both cover higher price tiers in more depth.

People Also Ask

What is the best smartphone under $500 in 2026?
The Google Pixel 10a is the best overall smartphone under $500 in 2026, offering Google's camera processing and clean software at roughly $499. The Samsung Galaxy A56 5G and Nothing Phone (3a) are also strong alternatives depending on your priorities.
Can you get a 5G phone for under $500 in 2026?
Yes. Nearly every phone recommended in this guide, including the Pixel 10a, Galaxy A56 5G, and Nothing Phone (3a), supports 5G as standard, since 5G is no longer limited to expensive flagships.
Do phones under $500 have good cameras in 2026?
Many do. Phones like the Google Pixel 10a benefit from flagship-adjacent camera processing software, and the Infinix GT 20 Pro and Realme 13 5G both produce sharp daylight photos well above what a similar price got you just a couple of years ago.
What should I prioritize when buying a phone under $500?
Software update length, battery life, and camera quality generally matter more day to day than raw benchmark performance at this price point, since nearly every phone under $500 in 2026 handles everyday tasks smoothly.
Is the Nothing Phone (3a) a good alternative to the Pixel 10a?
Yes, particularly for buyers who value distinctive design and a lower starting price. It offers a large battery and a bright 120Hz display for around $379, though its camera and AI features trail the Pixel 10a slightly.
