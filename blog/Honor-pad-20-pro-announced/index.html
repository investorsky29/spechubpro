Honor Pad 20 Pro Announced With Snapdragon 8s Gen 4, 165Hz Display and 10,100mAh Battery

Tech Editor August 18, 2026 8 min read
HonorTabletsSnapdragonLatest News

Honor has officially unveiled the Pad 20 Pro, a productivity-focused Android tablet built around a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset, a 12.1-inch 165Hz display, and a 10,100mAh battery. The tablet steps up clearly from the standard Pad 20 launched earlier this year, and Honor is also offering a Paperlike Edition aimed at note-taking and sketching.

The Pad 20 Pro arrives just a few months after the standard Honor Pad 20, and the upgrade path is immediately clear: a faster chipset, the same sharp 12.1-inch panel pushed to a smoother refresh rate, and a battery large enough to comfortably outlast most laptops. Honor is positioning the tablet less as a media-consumption device and more as a genuine productivity tool, with a PC-style desktop mode and support for a keyboard case and stylus.

Display: 12.1-inch 3K Panel at 165Hz

The centerpiece of the Pad 20 Pro is its 12.1-inch IPS LCD panel, running at a 3K resolution of 3,000 x 1,872 pixels (292 PPI) in a 16:10 aspect ratio. The display supports a 165Hz refresh rate, alongside 144Hz, 120Hz, 90Hz, and 60Hz modes, and reaches up to 700 nits of peak brightness. It covers the DCI-P3 color gamut and supports 1.07 billion colors, with IMAX Enhanced certification and global DC dimming for reduced flicker.

Honor is also offering a Paperlike Edition of the tablet, which swaps the standard glossy finish for an anti-glare, micro-textured coating designed to diffuse reflections and mimic the tactile resistance of writing on paper. Both versions support the optional Honor Magic-Pencil 4s stylus for note-taking and sketching, and Honor has built in several eye-care features, including hardware-level low blue light filtering, anti-flicker technology, and AI-driven eye comfort adjustments.

Performance: Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 Chipset

The biggest change from the standard Pad 20 is the chipset. Where the base model uses a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3, the Pad 20 Pro moves up to a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4, built on a 4nm process. The octa-core chip pairs a single Cortex-X4 prime core running up to 3.2GHz with three Cortex-A720 performance cores and four Cortex-A720 efficiency cores, backed by an Adreno 825 GPU.

According to Honor, the new chipset delivers a 31 percent increase in CPU performance and a 49 percent increase in GPU performance over the Snapdragon 7 Gen 3, adding up to more than double the overall performance of its predecessor. The tablet ships with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage, though the storage is not expandable via microSD.

Battery and Charging

The Pad 20 Pro packs a 10,100mAh battery, a substantial capacity for a tablet, paired with 66W wired fast charging, an upgrade over the charging speed on the standard Pad 20. Honor claims the battery is good for up to 12 hours of continuous video playback or around 7 hours of gaming on a single charge, figures that reflect Honor's own internal testing rather than independent lab results.

Despite the large battery, Honor kept the design relatively slim, with an aluminum unibody measuring 6.29mm thick and weighing 537 grams.

Software and Productivity Features

The Pad 20 Pro runs MagicOS 10 based on Android 16, and Honor is leaning heavily into productivity for this release. The tablet includes a PC Mode with resizable, desktop-style windows for multitasking, along with cross-platform data sharing and multi-screen collaboration with compatible Android, iOS, and PC devices.

One of the more notable software additions is AI Memo, a feature that can record meetings, transcribe audio to text, generate summaries, and produce mind maps automatically. Honor also offers an optional Smart Bluetooth Keyboard case for the tablet, though it does not include a built-in trackpad, according to reports.

Cameras

Camera hardware is modest, as is typical for a productivity tablet. The Pad 20 Pro includes an 8-megapixel front-facing camera for video calls and a 13-megapixel rear camera, positioning the tablet's cameras as a secondary feature behind its display, performance, and battery life.

Connectivity and Audio

The tablet supports Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0, both current-generation standards that clearly separate the Pad 20 Pro from Honor's entry-level tablets. On the audio side, Honor fitted a six-speaker setup tuned with DTS Ultra and spatial audio support, aimed at making the tablet more usable for media playback and video calls without external speakers.

Editor's Note Battery life and performance figures quoted by manufacturers, including Honor's video playback and gaming claims here, reflect internal testing under specific conditions. Real-world results can vary depending on brightness, refresh rate settings, and usage patterns.

Price and Availability

Honor has confirmed a Malaysia launch for the Pad 20 Pro on August 24, 2026, with full pricing and memory configuration details expected on that date. As of this writing, Honor has not confirmed availability or pricing for the US, UK, or other global markets, and the tablet is currently listed only on Honor's Malaysia website.

For context, the standard Honor Pad 20 launched at $299 on Amazon, and if Honor follows a similar pattern for the Pro model, a wider global rollout is plausible, though this has not been officially confirmed. Buyers outside Malaysia should treat any pricing figures they see elsewhere as unofficial until Honor makes a formal announcement for their region.

Frequently Asked Questions

What chipset does the Honor Pad 20 Pro use?
The Honor Pad 20 Pro uses a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset, an octa-core design with one Cortex-X4 prime core up to 3.2GHz, three Cortex-A720 performance cores, and four Cortex-A720 efficiency cores, paired with an Adreno 825 GPU.
How big is the Honor Pad 20 Pro's battery?
The Honor Pad 20 Pro has a 10,100mAh battery with 66W wired fast charging. Honor claims up to 12 hours of continuous video playback and around 7 hours of gaming on a single charge.
Is the Honor Pad 20 Pro available in the US or UK?
Not yet. The Honor Pad 20 Pro has only been confirmed for Malaysia so far, with a local launch date of August 24, 2026. Honor has not announced pricing or availability for the US, UK, or other global markets.
What is the Honor Pad 20 Pro Paperlike Edition?
The Paperlike Edition is a variant of the Honor Pad 20 Pro with an anti-glare, micro-textured screen coating designed to diffuse reflections and mimic the tactile feel of writing on paper when used with the optional Honor Magic-Pencil 4s stylus.
Does the Honor Pad 20 Pro support a keyboard and stylus?
Yes. Honor offers an optional Smart Bluetooth Keyboard case, though it does not include a built-in trackpad according to reports, and the tablet supports the Honor Magic-Pencil 4s stylus for note-taking and sketching.
How does the Honor Pad 20 Pro compare to the standard Honor Pad 20?
The Pad 20 Pro upgrades the chipset from a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 to a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4, delivering more than double the performance according to Honor, and boosts charging speed. The display size stays the same, but the Pro model adds the Paperlike Edition option and a six-speaker audio setup.
