MediaTek Dimensity 9600M Is Official: Specs, Features and Phones

Global News Desk September 17, 2026 13 min read
MediaTekDimensityChipsetsLatest News

MediaTek has officially introduced the Dimensity 9600M processor, adding a new chip to its premium Dimensity 9600 family. The new processor was announced on September 15, 2026, alongside the flagship Dimensity 9600 Pro, but the two chips are not aimed at the same phones. While the Dimensity 9600 Pro is built for the very top of the market, the Dimensity 9600M is designed to bring many of the same premium features to a wider range of high-end smartphones.

The Dimensity 9600M combines an all-big-core CPU, Arm Mali-G1 Ultra graphics, MediaTek's NPU 990, LPDDR5X memory, UFS 4.1 storage, advanced 5G connectivity, and new AI features. MediaTek says the first smartphones using the Dimensity 9600M are expected to arrive in the fourth quarter of 2026, and the standard vivo X500, launching September 21, 2026, is already confirmed to use it, with the Oppo Find X10 also reported as an early adopter ahead of its own September 22 launch.

What Is the Dimensity 9600M Processor?

The Dimensity 9600M is a premium smartphone system-on-chip from MediaTek, designed around an eight-core, all-big-core CPU architecture. Instead of combining large performance cores with small efficiency cores the way most chips do, it uses three different classes of Arm C1 cores: one Arm C1-Ultra core clocked up to 4.21GHz, three Arm C1-Premium cores up to 3.5GHz, and four Arm C1-Pro cores up to 2.7GHz, backed by 16MB of L3 cache and 10MB of system-level cache.

MediaTek also gives the chip Armv9.3 architecture support and SME2 instructions, useful for machine learning and AI workloads, alongside support for LPDDR5X memory and four-lane UFS 4.1 storage. CPU performance is only one part of the story, though; the 9600M also focuses heavily on AI, gaming, imaging, and connectivity.

Dimensity 9600M CPU: Eight Big Cores Explained

One of the most interesting parts of the Dimensity 9600M is its all-big-core CPU design, built from three types of Arm C1 cores.

Arm C1-Ultra

There is one Arm C1-Ultra core, clocked up to 4.21GHz with 2MB of L2 cache. This is the most powerful CPU core in the chip, intended for demanding tasks such as heavy applications, gaming workloads, and other performance-intensive operations.

Three Arm C1-Premium Cores

The processor also has three C1-Premium cores, clocked up to 3.5GHz with 1MB of L2 cache each, helping handle demanding workloads without requiring the Ultra core to shoulder everything alone.

Four Arm C1-Pro Cores

Finally, there are four C1-Pro cores, clocked up to 2.7GHz with 512KB of L2 cache each. Although positioned below the Ultra and Premium cores, they're still part of the all-big-core design rather than small efficiency cores.

This approach can give manufacturers more performance headroom for demanding apps while the scheduling system decides which cores should handle which tasks. MediaTek also includes its Dimensity Scheduling Engine 3.0, an upgrade over the second-generation version used on the Dimensity 9500, designed to improve responsiveness and better manage CPU, GPU, and memory resources.

Dimensity 9600M GPU and Gaming Features

Gaming is another major part of the new processor. The Dimensity 9600M uses the Arm Mali-G1 Ultra MC12 GPU and inherits MediaTek's Neural Graphics Architecture and HyperEngine gaming technologies. MediaTek says supported gaming scenarios can reach up to 185 frames per second, though this figure shouldn't be treated as a universal gaming speed since actual FPS depends on the game, graphics settings, display, thermal system, and software optimization of the phone.

The chip also supports advanced ray-tracing features, and MediaTek says the platform can deliver true 120fps ray-traced scenes in supported scenarios. Its MediaTek Rendering and Frame Creation technology, or MRFC 3.0, can also help transform 60fps game content into a smoother 120fps experience. That means the Dimensity 9600M isn't only focused on raw GPU power; it also leans on AI and frame-generation technologies to improve perceived gaming smoothness, which could be particularly useful on phones with high-refresh-rate displays.

Dimensity 9600M AI Features

AI is one of the biggest changes in this generation. The Dimensity 9600M comes with the MediaTek NPU 990, which handles demanding on-device generative and agentic AI tasks, alongside an ultra-efficient NPU with a compute-in-memory architecture for lower-power, always-on AI tasks. That gives the chip two different approaches to AI: the powerful NPU handles demanding workloads, while the efficient NPU deals with background or always-on features without unnecessarily draining the battery.

The processor supports technologies including Gemini Nano 4, Qwen 3.5 Omni 4B, 4K text-to-image generation, personalized AI experiences, proactive AI features, AI camera assistance, and broader agentic AI functions delivered through MediaTek's Agentic AI Engine. The camera agent is particularly interesting because it can use AI to provide smarter photography suggestions, understanding what the user is trying to capture and helping with the shooting process rather than simply improving image processing after a photo is taken.

Dimensity 9600M Camera and Video Features

The Dimensity 9600M is also designed for premium smartphone cameras. MediaTek lists support for camera sensors of up to 320MP, though again, that doesn't mean every phone using the chip will actually have a 320MP camera; the real hardware is selected by each phone manufacturer.

The chipset also supports up to 8K 60fps 10-bit video capture across formats including HEVC, AVC, VP9, and AV1, 4K 120fps video with EIS, 4K 60fps cinematic video, AI super zoom, AI composition assistance, AI anti-shake, and horizon correction. The 8K60 support is particularly notable because recording at this resolution and frame rate requires substantial processing power, while 4K 120fps support can be useful for slow-motion video and high-quality capture.

Display and Storage Support

The Dimensity 9600M can support displays with resolutions up to WQHD+ at 180Hz, a combination useful for premium phones since a high resolution provides sharper images while a high refresh rate makes scrolling and supported games feel smoother. The processor also supports LPDDR5X memory at up to 10,667Mbps, and for storage, MediaTek includes four-lane UFS 4.1 support. Fast storage is increasingly important as modern smartphones load larger applications, games, and AI models, and UFS 4.1 can help reduce loading times and improve data transfer performance.

Dimensity 9600M 5G and Connectivity

The 9600M is built for modern 5G networks, supporting 3GPP Release 17, Sub-6GHz 5G, carrier aggregation, and both standalone and non-standalone 5G networks. MediaTek lists support for up to 7.4Gbps download speeds under supported conditions, and the chip also supports 5G and 4G Dual SIM Dual Active, meaning compatible phones can maintain active connections on two SIMs.

For wireless connectivity, the processor supports Wi-Fi 7 with speeds of up to 7.3Gbps, and Bluetooth 6.0 with speeds of up to 12Mbps. These maximum speeds are theoretical platform capabilities; real-world results depend on the network, router, carrier, signal quality, and the phone's antenna design.

Dimensity 9600M vs Dimensity 9500

One of the most interesting discussions around the new chip is its relationship with the Dimensity 9500, and independent testing and reporting since launch has largely settled the question: the Dimensity 9600M is, in hardware terms, essentially the Dimensity 9500 repackaged under a new name. Multiple outlets that compared MediaTek's own spec sheets for both chips side by side found the CPU configuration and clock speeds, the Mali-G1 Ultra MC12 GPU, and the APU 990 NPU are identical between the two, right down to camera support capping out at 320MP and 8K60/4K120 video on both.

That doesn't mean nothing changed. MediaTek is bringing newer platform technologies to the 9600M, including the upgraded Dimensity Scheduling Engine 3.0 (up from the second-generation engine on the 9500), the newer Agentic AI Engine, and support for newer AI models like Gemini Nano 4 and Qwen 3.5 Omni 4B.

Dimensity 9600M vs Dimensity 9600 Pro

The Dimensity 9600M and Dimensity 9600 Pro belong to the same generation but target different levels of the smartphone market, and the gap between them is considerably wider than the similar names suggest. The Dimensity 9600 Pro is the true flagship chip, built on a 2nm process with a different "2+3+3" CPU layout using newer Arm C2-Ultra cores clocked up to 4.55GHz, a different Mali G2-Ultra NX GPU with a third-generation ray tracing engine, and a more powerful primary NPU 1090. MediaTek also gives the 9600 Pro next-generation LPDDR6 memory alongside LPDDR5X, plus UFS 5.0 storage.

The 9600M, by contrast, stays on TSMC's 3nm N3P process with the older Arm C1 cores, the Mali-G1 Ultra MC12 GPU, and the NPU 990, paired with LPDDR5X and UFS 4.1. In simple terms, the 9600 Pro is a genuine next-generation flagship chip, while the 9600M is a refreshed version of last year's flagship platform repositioned to bring some of the 9600 Pro's newer software and AI features to a broader range of high-end devices at a lower cost for manufacturers.

Is the Dimensity 9600M Good for Gaming?

On paper, the Dimensity 9600M is built for demanding mobile gaming. It combines an all-big-core CPU with the Mali-G1 Ultra MC12 GPU, NPU 990, Neural Graphics Architecture, and MediaTek HyperEngine, and it supports high-refresh-rate displays along with technologies designed to improve frame rates and smoothness. MediaTek's claim of up to 185fps applies to supported scenarios rather than every game, so the real gaming experience will depend heavily on the phone's cooling system, software optimization, screen, and game settings. Still, the hardware platform gives manufacturers plenty of performance features to build gaming-focused or premium flagship phones.

Is the Dimensity 9600M Good for AI?

AI is arguably one of the main reasons the 9600M exists as part of the newer 9600 family. Its NPU 990 is designed for generative and agentic AI, while the additional efficient NPU is intended for lower-power AI tasks, and support for Gemini Nano 4 and Qwen 3.5 Omni 4B gives manufacturers access to newer on-device AI capabilities. That said, the actual AI experience will depend on how each phone maker implements the hardware; a phone may share the same processor as another device but offer different AI features because of differences in software.

Editor's Note It's worth being upfront about naming here: a chip called "Dimensity 9600M" sitting alongside a "Dimensity 9600 Pro" naturally suggests two close variants of the same new platform. In reality, independent comparisons of MediaTek's own spec sheets show the 9600M's core silicon is essentially unchanged from last year's Dimensity 9500, while the 9600 Pro is the genuine new flagship. If you're comparing phones by chipset name alone, it's worth checking which specific Dimensity 9600 variant a phone actually uses.

Dimensity 9600M Phones: Which Phones Will Use It?

The list of confirmed Dimensity 9600M phones is still developing, since MediaTek only announced the processor in September 2026, but several upcoming smartphones have already been linked to the chip.

vivo X500

The standard vivo X500 is one of the strongest candidates and now appears confirmed: multiple reports indicate the standard X500 will use the Dimensity 9600M, while the X500 Pro and X500 Pro Max are set to use the higher-end Dimensity 9600 Pro. The vivo X500 series is scheduled for launch in China on September 21, 2026.

Oppo Find X10

The Oppo Find X10 is another phone linked to the Dimensity 9600M. Reports indicate the Find X10 Pro Max will use the Dimensity 9600 Pro, which by extension makes the standard Find X10 a likely candidate for the 9600M, though Oppo has not yet confirmed the chipset for each specific model in the series. The Find X10 lineup is reported to launch on September 22, 2026. Until Oppo confirms the exact chipset per model, it's better to describe the Dimensity 9600M connection as reported rather than fully confirmed.

More Phones Are Coming

MediaTek says the first Dimensity 9600M smartphones are expected to launch in Q4 2026, so more brands and models should appear as manufacturers announce their upcoming phones.

Dimensity 9600M Release Date

The Dimensity 9600M release date as a chipset announcement was September 15, 2026. MediaTek announced it alongside the Dimensity 9600 Pro, and the company says the first smartphones powered by the new 9600M are expected to launch during the fourth quarter of 2026, with the vivo X500 arriving first on September 21.

What Makes the Dimensity 9600M Different?

The Dimensity 9600M sits between a traditional generation-to-generation processor upgrade and a broader platform refresh. Its CPU and GPU hardware are effectively unchanged from the Dimensity 9500, but MediaTek is adding newer AI capabilities, scheduling improvements, graphics technologies, and support for newer AI models on top of that familiar silicon. That makes the processor more than just a number on a spec sheet, even if it's less than a full new chip: for manufacturers, it's a way to build high-end smartphones with strong CPU and GPU performance without using the more expensive 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro platform. For users, the biggest differences will likely show up in AI features, gaming optimization, camera processing, and overall phone software rather than raw benchmark scores.

Conclusion

The Dimensity 9600M processor is MediaTek's new premium chip for high-end smartphones that don't necessarily need the company's top 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro platform. Its biggest strengths are the all-big-core CPU, Mali-G1 Ultra graphics, NPU 990, advanced AI support, fast LPDDR5X memory, UFS 4.1 storage, high-refresh-rate display support, and modern 5G connectivity.

At the same time, the chip is particularly interesting because its core hardware is, by MediaTek's own published specifications, essentially identical to the Dimensity 9500. Rather than a completely new CPU generation, MediaTek is using the 9600M to bring newer AI, scheduling, gaming, and platform technologies to another tier of premium smartphones. The first Dimensity 9600M phones are expected in Q4 2026, starting with the vivo X500 on September 21 and likely the Oppo Find X10 shortly after. As more phones launch, real-world gaming tests, battery results, and benchmark comparisons will give a much clearer picture of what the Dimensity 9600M can actually deliver.
