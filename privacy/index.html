Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SpecHub (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at spechubpro.com.

At SpecHub, accessible from https://spechubpro.com/, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information that may be collected when you use our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

SpecHub may collect limited information when you use our website. This may include general usage information such as pages visited, browser type, device information, approximate location, referring pages, and time spent on the website.

We may also receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as information submitted through a contact form or newsletter subscription, if those features are available on the website.

SpecHub may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, improve website functionality, analyse website usage, and support advertising services.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through SpecHub may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website
  • Improve our content, features, and user experience
  • Understand how visitors use the website
  • Respond to questions or messages submitted through our contact features
  • Provide newsletters or other communications when you have voluntarily subscribed
  • Display and measure relevant advertising
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical, security, or fraudulent activity

3. Third-Party Advertising

SpecHub may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide, personalise, measure, and improve advertising.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website and other websites.

For more information about how Google uses information from sites and applications that use its services, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

You can manage your advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings.

4. Cookies

SpecHub uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember certain preferences, analyse website traffic, and support advertising services.

Some cookies may be necessary for certain website features to function, while other cookies may be used for analytics or advertising purposes.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or availability of some features of the website.

5. Data Sharing

SpecHub does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers that help us operate the website, such as hosting providers, analytics services, email services, security services, and advertising providers, where applicable.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to help protect information processed through SpecHub. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

If you would like to make a privacy-related request, please contact us at support@spechubpro.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SpecHub is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to SpecHub, please contact us so that we can review the information and take appropriate action where required.

9. Third-Party Websites

SpecHub may contain links to third-party websites, including manufacturers, retailers, social media platforms, and other external services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any external website you visit.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of SpecHub after changes are posted constitutes your acknowledgement of the updated Privacy Policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can Contact Us or email hello@spechubpro.com.
