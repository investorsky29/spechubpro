Expert Review

The Honor Magic V5 is one of the thinnest foldable smartphones available. It combines a large 7.95-inch LTPO OLED inner display with a bright 6.43-inch cover display and the Snapdragon 8 Elite processor for flagship performance. The 50MP triple-camera system with a 64MP periscope lens delivers excellent photography, while the 6100mAh silicon-carbon battery provides exceptional endurance with fast wired and wireless charging. Its lightweight design, premium hinge, and IP58/IP59 protection make it one of the best premium foldables of 2025.
