Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 6a is the lower-priced companion to the Pixel 6, announced on May 11, 2022 at $449 in the US. It brought Google's first-generation Tensor chip to a compact 6.1-inch phone. It suits buyers who want Pixel software and a flagship-class chip at a modest price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the first-generation Google Tensor (5 nm) with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage, plus the Titan M2 security chip. The Pixel 7a that followed uses the Tensor G2 and 8GB of RAM. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.1-inch OLED display has 1080 x 2400 pixels and a refresh rate of up to 60Hz, which is lower than the 90Hz offered on the Pixel 7a. An optical fingerprint sensor sits under the screen.

Camera Experience

The Pixel 6a has a dual rear camera with a main and an ultrawide lens. Resolution, aperture and front-camera details are omitted because we could not verify them for this page. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4410 mAh with fast wired charging. Wireless charging is not listed, unlike the Pixel 7a. The charging wattage could not be verified, so none is listed. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 12 and has stereo speakers and a USB-C port. It is rated IP67 and has a metal frame.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a small, inexpensive phone with Pixel software and a good security chip will find it a fit, especially if they can find one at a discount.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want wireless charging, a 90Hz screen or more RAM should look at the Pixel 7a. Those who want the largest battery and a telephoto lens should consider a Pro model.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 6a is a compact, affordable phone with a capable chip and a good security chip. Its main limits are the 60Hz display and the lack of wireless charging.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel 6a and the Pixel 7a?
The Pixel 7a has a Tensor G2 chip, 8GB of RAM, a 4385 mAh battery, a display of up to 90Hz and wireless charging. The Pixel 6a has the first-generation Tensor, 6GB of RAM, a 4410 mAh battery, a 60Hz display and no wireless charging.
Is the Pixel 6a water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP67.
Does the Pixel 6a support wireless charging?
No wireless charging is listed for the Pixel 6a.
How much RAM and storage does it have?
It has 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage.
Which Android version did the Pixel 6a launch with?
It launched with Android 12.
How much did the Pixel 6a cost at launch?
In the US, it launched at $449. Prices elsewhere differ, and current prices vary by retailer.
