Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Spark 30 Pro is a 4G mid-range phone from 2024 with a 6.78-inch 120Hz AMOLED display, a MediaTek Helio G100 chipset, a 108MP main camera and a 5,000mAh battery with 33W charging. It is the higher model of the Spark 30 family and a Limited Edition version also exists.

It has not been released in India, so most listings are based on its global launch.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G100 with two 2.2GHz Cortex-A76 and six 2.0GHz Cortex-A55 cores, with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage that can be expanded up to 1TB. The GPU and memory type were not confirmed and are not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch AMOLED at Full HD+ resolution with a 120Hz refresh rate and a punch-hole front camera. Brightness and protection glass were not confirmed and are not listed.

Camera Experience

The rear system is a dual-camera setup led by a 108MP wide main sensor, and the front camera is 13MP. Rear video goes up to 1440p at 30fps, and front video to 1080p at 30fps. The second rear camera was not confirmed and is not listed.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,000mAh with 33W wired charging through USB Type-C. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 14 with HiOS and does not support 5G. NFC, water resistance, fingerprint sensor type and the software-update period were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want an AMOLED screen, a high-resolution main camera, expandable storage and a big battery without paying for 5G will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, faster charging, an ultrawide or telephoto camera should look at the Camon 40 series or another model. Buyers in India cannot buy it there.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Spark 30 Pro is a feature-rich 4G phone for its price, with a 120Hz AMOLED screen, a 108MP camera and expandable storage, held back by 33W charging and limited information about some features. It suits budget buyers who prioritise screen and camera resolution.
