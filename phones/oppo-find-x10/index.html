Expert Review

The OPPO Find X10 is a flagship smartphone launched in September 2026 with a 6.59-inch LTPO AMOLED display supporting up to 144Hz and a MediaTek Dimensity 9600M chipset. Its camera system combines a 200MP OIS main camera, a 200MP periscope telephoto camera with 2.8x optical zoom and a 50MP ultrawide camera. The phone also features an 8000mAh silicon-carbon battery with 80W wired and 50W wireless charging. It runs Android 17 with ColorOS 17 and includes IP66, IP68 and IP69 protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the OPPO Find X10?
The OPPO Find X10 is priced at $799, depending on the RAM and storage configuration chosen.
What chipset does the OPPO Find X10 use?
The OPPO Find X10 is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9600M chipset, built on a 3nm process, paired with an ARM G1-Ultra GPU.
Does the OPPO Find X10 have a telephoto camera?
Yes, the OPPO Find X10 has a 200MP periscope telephoto camera with OIS and 2.8x optical zoom, alongside its 200MP main camera and 50MP ultrawide camera.
Does the OPPO Find X10 support wireless charging?
Yes, the OPPO Find X10 supports 50W wireless charging in addition to 80W SuperVOOC wired charging.
Is the OPPO Find X10 water resistant?
Yes, the OPPO Find X10 carries IP66, IP68 and IP69 ratings, offering strong protection against dust, submersion and high-pressure water jets.
Does the OPPO Find X10 support microSD storage expansion?
No, the OPPO Find X10 does not have a microSD card slot. Storage is fixed at 256GB, 512GB, or 1TB depending on the model chosen.
