Expert Review

The iPhone XS Max was Apple's largest flagship iPhone of 2018, featuring a 6.5-inch Super Retina OLED display and the A12 Bionic processor. Its dual 12MP rear cameras support optical image stabilization and 2x optical zoom, while Face ID provides secure biometric authentication. With IP68 protection, wireless charging, and up to 512GB of storage, the XS Max offered a premium feature set for its generation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the iPhone XS Max?
The iPhone XS Max is priced at around $190 today as a used or refurbished device. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the iPhone XS Max use?
The iPhone XS Max is powered by Apple's A12 Bionic chip, the same hexa-core processor used in the standard iPhone XS, paired with 4GB of RAM.
What is the difference between the iPhone XS and iPhone XS Max?
The iPhone XS Max has a larger 6.5-inch display compared to the standard XS's 5.8-inch screen and a bigger 3174mAh battery, while sharing the same A12 Bionic chip and dual-camera system.
When was the iPhone XS Max released?
The iPhone XS Max was released on September 21, 2018, alongside the standard iPhone XS.
Does the iPhone XS Max support 5G?
No, the iPhone XS Max does not support 5G connectivity. Apple's first 5G-capable iPhones arrived with the iPhone 12 series in 2020.
Is the iPhone XS Max worth buying today?
As an older flagship, the iPhone XS Max lacks 5G, USB-C, and modern iOS features, but its large OLED display and capable A12 Bionic chip still make it a reasonable low-cost option for buyers who want a bigger classic iPhone.
