Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The vivo V30 Pro was announced in Indonesia on February 28, 2024 and launched in India on March 7, 2024, with sales starting on March 14. It is the higher model of the V30 series and the first V-series phone with ZEISS optics, combining a 7.5 mm body with a 6.78-inch curved AMOLED display, a Dimensity 8200 chipset and a 5,000mAh battery.

It sits in the mid-range and is a different type of phone to vivo's X-series flagships. The standard vivo V30 uses a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 and a simpler camera setup without the 50MP portrait camera.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 8200 (4 nm), with one 3.1GHz and three 3.0GHz Cortex-A78 cores and four 2.0GHz Cortex-A55 cores, plus a Mali-G610 MC6 GPU. RAM is LPDDR5X in 8GB or 12GB sizes, and storage is UFS 3.1 in 256GB or 512GB sizes with no card slot.

SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch curved AMOLED at 1260 x 2800 pixels (about 453 pixels per inch) with a 120Hz refresh rate and HDR10+. A peak brightness of 2,800 nits is advertised, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

The front glass is Schott α, and the frame is plastic. The phone is rated IP54, so it is dust protected and resistant to splashes but not to immersion.

Camera Experience

The rear system has three 50MP cameras with ZEISS optics: a Sony IMX920 main (f/1.88, 1/1.49-inch sensor, optical stabilisation), a 50MP portrait camera with a 50mm-equivalent lens (2x zoom) and a 50MP ultrawide with a 119 degree field of view. An Aura Light ring flash supports portraits.

The 50MP front camera has an f/2.0 lens, a 92 degree field of view and autofocus. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,000mAh with 80W wired charging, listed as reaching 100% in about 43 to 48 minutes depending on the figure. No wireless charging is listed. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and Funtouch OS 14. The listed update promise is up to 2 major Android upgrades and 3 years of security updates. It has an in-display fingerprint sensor and dual Nano-SIM support. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth versions were not confirmed and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a slim, light phone with a curved screen, a good selfie camera, ZEISS portrait features and quick charging without paying for a flagship will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a long-range zoom, wireless charging, stronger water resistance or a longer update promise should look at a flagship such as the vivo X200 or a phone with a higher IP rating.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the V30 Pro is a slim, camera-focused mid-ranger with a triple 50MP system, a 50MP autofocus selfie camera and 80W charging, with IP54 and a short update promise as its main limits. It suits people who value design, portraits and selfies.

The V30 Pro has a Dimensity 8200 and a triple 50MP ZEISS camera including a 50MP portrait lens. The standard V30 uses a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 and a 50MP main with a 50MP ultrawide and a 2MP bokeh lens.

The battery is 5,000mAh with 80W wired charging. No wireless charging is listed.

A 50MP main, a 50MP 2x portrait camera and a 50MP ultrawide on the back, plus a 50MP autofocus selfie camera, co-engineered with ZEISS.

How long does it get software updates?
