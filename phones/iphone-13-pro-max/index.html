Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 13 Pro Max is the largest model of Apple's 2021 lineup, released on September 24, 2021 at $1,099 in the US. It shares its chip, cameras and ProMotion display with the iPhone 13 Pro, but adds a 6.7-inch screen and a much larger reported battery. It was aimed at buyers who wanted the longest battery life and biggest screen. It is now an older phone.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A15 Bionic is a 5 nm chip with a six-core CPU and a five-core GPU. Independent sources report 6GB of RAM, which Apple does not publish. Storage is 128GB, 256GB, 512GB or 1TB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED display has 1284 x 2778 pixels (about 458 ppi) and an adaptive refresh rate from 10Hz to 120Hz. Apple rates it for 1,000 nits typical and up to 1,200 nits peak in HDR. The larger panel suits video, gaming and reading.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 12MP main (f/1.5, sensor-shift stabilization), a 12MP telephoto (f/2.8, 3x optical zoom) and a 12MP ultrawide (f/1.8, autofocus) with macro capability, plus a LiDAR scanner. The front camera is 12MP. Video goes up to 4K at 60 fps with ProRes support. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 4352 mAh, which is about 665 mAh more than the 3687 mAh reported for the iPhone 12 Pro Max. Apple states about 50% in 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter, which is not included. Wireless charging supports MagSafe at up to 15W and Qi at up to 7.5W. We have no endurance tests, and an older battery may hold less charge today.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 15 and supports 5G, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0, NFC and Ultra Wideband. It has Face ID, a LiDAR scanner, MagSafe and an IP68 rating up to 6 metres for 30 minutes.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Heavy video watchers, travellers and anyone who wants the largest screen and longest battery of the iPhone 13 family will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a lighter phone should choose the iPhone 13 Pro or the iPhone 13. Those who want USB-C or newer features should look at a recent iPhone.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 13 Pro Max combined ProMotion, a big OLED screen and a very large reported battery. Its weight and Lightning port are the main limits today.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the iPhone 13 Pro Max and the iPhone 13 Pro?
They share the A15 Bionic chip, 6GB of reported RAM and the camera set. The 13 Pro Max has a 6.7-inch display, a reported 4352 mAh battery and weighs 240 g. The 13 Pro has a 6.1-inch display, a reported 3095 mAh battery and weighs 204 g.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 13 Pro Max?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 4352 mAh.
Does the iPhone 13 Pro Max have a 120Hz display?
Yes. It uses an adaptive ProMotion display that varies between 10Hz and 120Hz.
Does it come in 1TB?
Yes. Storage options are 128GB, 256GB, 512GB and 1TB.
Does it support 5G?
Yes. It supports 5G on sub-6 GHz networks, and US models also support mmWave.
How much did the iPhone 13 Pro Max cost at launch?
In the US, the launch price was $1,099 for 128GB. Prices elsewhere differ, and Apple no longer sells this model as a current product.
