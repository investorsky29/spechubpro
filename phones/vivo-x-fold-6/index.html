Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The vivo X Fold6 was announced in China on June 26, 2026 and released there on July 1, then launched globally on October 1, 2026. It is a book-style foldable with an 8.02-inch inner display and a 6.51-inch cover screen, powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super chipset and led by a 200MP ZEISS main camera.

It follows the vivo X Fold5. Its main selling points are its 4.4 mm unfolded thickness and its very large battery for a foldable. The global version differs from the China version in battery capacity (6,900mAh vs 7,000mAh) and software (Android 17 with OriginOS 7 Fold vs Android 16 with OriginOS 6 Fold).

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super (3 nm), with one 4.21GHz C1-Ultra core, three 3.5GHz C1-Premium cores and four 2.7GHz C1-Pro cores, plus a Mali-G1 Ultra MC12 GPU. RAM is 12GB or 16GB with 256GB, 512GB or 1TB of storage and no card slot. Memory and storage standards were not confirmed and are not listed.

SpecHub has not run benchmarks or thermal tests, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The inner display is an 8.02-inch foldable LTPO AMOLED at 2312 x 2504 pixels (about 424 pixels per inch) with a 120Hz refresh rate. It supports HDR10+, HDR Vivid and Dolby Vision. The 6.51-inch cover screen is also an LTPO AMOLED running at up to 120Hz at 1120 x 2528 pixels.

vivo advertises a peak brightness of 5,000 nits on both displays, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement. Both screens are larger than the 7.5 to 7.6-inch inner displays and 5.4 to 5.5-inch cover screens on some rival foldables.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is led by a 200MP ZEISS main camera with optical stabilisation, and video recording goes up to 8K. Two 20MP selfie cameras are advertised, one on each screen.

The telephoto and ultrawide camera specifications were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The global X Fold6 has a 6,900mAh battery, made up of two cells that vivo describes as equivalent to a single 6,900mAh battery, while the China version has 7,000mAh. Wired charging is 80W, wireless charging is 40W, and reverse wireless (5W) and reverse wired charging are also supported. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests.

SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The global version runs Android 17 with OriginOS 7 Fold, and the China version runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6 Fold. vivo positions the global version around productivity. The fingerprint sensor is side-mounted.

A water-resistance rating, a software-update period and some camera details were not confirmed and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a thin, light foldable with a very large battery, two large high-brightness displays and a 200MP main camera will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a confirmed water-resistance rating, a published update policy, a stylus or a known global price may prefer a more established foldable. Buyers who prefer a wider, more compact foldable may look at the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the X Fold6 is a thin, large-battery foldable with a flagship chipset and a 200MP main camera, held back by several unconfirmed details such as its water-resistance rating and global price. It suits people who prioritise battery size and thinness in a foldable.
