Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor 400 Pro is the higher-end model of Honor's 2025 pair, released on May 22, 2025. It combines a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chip, a 200MP main camera, a 3x telephoto lens and IP68/IP69 protection. It suits buyers who want a camera-focused phone with fast charging and a strong durability rating.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 (4 nm) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. The Honor 200 Pro used the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch AMOLED display has 1280 x 2800 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate. It is slightly larger than the 6.55-inch screen of the standard Honor 400.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 200MP main (f/1.9, 1/1.4-inch sensor, optical stabilisation), a 50MP telephoto (f/2.4, 3x optical zoom, optical stabilisation) and a 12MP ultrawide (f/2.2, 112 degrees). The front camera is 50MP with a 2MP depth sensor. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5300 mAh with 100W wired charging, 50W wireless charging and 5W reverse charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15 and MagicOS 9.0. It is rated IP68 and IP69, has dual Nano-SIM, an in-screen fingerprint sensor and an infrared blaster.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a flagship-class chip, a telephoto lens, fast wired and wireless charging and strong dust and water protection will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want the longest battery life should check the larger-battery models in Honor's range. Those on a tighter budget may prefer the Honor 400.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor 400 Pro is a well-rounded camera phone with fast charging and a top durability rating. Its weight and moderate battery capacity are the main trade-offs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Honor 400 Pro and the Honor 400?
The Honor 400 Pro has a 6.7-inch display, a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, 100W wired and 50W wireless charging, a 3x telephoto camera and IP68/IP69. The Honor 400 has a 6.55-inch display, a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3, 66W wired charging, no telephoto lens and IP65.
How fast does the Honor 400 Pro charge?
Wired charging is 100W and wireless charging is 50W, with 5W reverse charging.
Is the Honor 400 Pro water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68 and IP69.
Does it have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 50MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom.
What is the battery capacity?
The international model is listed at 5300 mAh.
