Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Camon 40 Pro 5G was unveiled on March 3, 2025 at MWC 2025 with the rest of the Camon 40 series and reached markets such as Indonesia and the Philippines from May 2025. It combines a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, a MediaTek Dimensity 7300 chipset, a 50MP OIS main camera and a 50MP autofocus selfie camera.

This page covers the 5G model. The Camon 40 Pro (4G) is the same design with a Helio G100 chipset and a 120Hz display, while the Camon 40 Premier 5G is a more powerful model with a different camera setup.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 7300 Ultimate (4 nm), with four 2.5GHz Cortex-A78 and four 2.0GHz Cortex-A55 cores and a Mali-G615 MC2 GPU. RAM is 8GB or 12GB with a single 256GB storage option, and some markets also add virtual RAM. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch Full HD+ AMOLED at 1080 x 2436 pixels (about 393 pixels per inch) with a 144Hz refresh rate. An in-display fingerprint scanner is built in. Brightness and protection glass were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

The 50MP selfie camera has autofocus, and the phone has stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos support.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main camera with a Sony LYT-700C sensor and optical stabilisation, and an 8MP ultrawide. There is no telephoto lens. The front camera is a 50MP sensor with autofocus.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,200mAh with 45W wired charging. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 15 with HiOS 15, and up to 3 major Android upgrades are listed. A water-resistance rating was not confirmed from more than one source and is not listed. It supports dual SIM (dual standby) and 5G.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a slim, light 5G phone with a fast 144Hz AMOLED screen, a stabilised main camera and a good selfie camera at a mid-range price will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a telephoto camera, faster charging or a more powerful chipset should look at the Camon 40 Premier 5G. Buyers who do not need 5G could look at the 4G Camon 40 or Camon 40 Pro.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Camon 40 Pro 5G is a slim, lightweight mid-range 5G phone with a standout 144Hz display and selfie camera, held back by a modest chipset and a single storage size. It suits people who prioritise screen, selfies and design.
