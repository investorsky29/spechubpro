Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy S9 is a compact flagship smartphone from 2018 that remains notable for its premium design, excellent Super AMOLED display, and capable 12MP camera with optical image stabilization. It features the Snapdragon 845 or Exynos 9810 processor depending on the market, along with expandable storage, wireless charging, IP68 protection, and a 3.5mm headphone jack. Its 3000mAh battery and older hardware are its main limitations today.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy S9?
The Samsung Galaxy S9 is priced at around $50 - $95 today as a used or refurbished device. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the Samsung Galaxy S9 use?
The Samsung Galaxy S9 uses either a Qualcomm Snapdragon 845 or a Samsung Exynos 9810 chipset depending on the region, both built on a 10nm process.
Does the Samsung Galaxy S9 have an ultrawide camera?
No, the Galaxy S9 has a single 12MP rear camera with optical image stabilization. Samsung did not add a multi-lens camera system until the Galaxy S10 the following year.
When was the Samsung Galaxy S9 released?
The Samsung Galaxy S9 was released on March 16, 2018.
Does the Galaxy S9 support 5G?
No, the Samsung Galaxy S9 does not support 5G. 5G connectivity did not arrive on Samsung's Galaxy S lineup until the Galaxy S10 5G and Galaxy S20 series.
Is the Samsung Galaxy S9 worth buying today?
As an older flagship, the Galaxy S9 lacks 5G, a multi-camera system, and modern processing power, but its display quality and premium design still make it a low-cost option for buyers who mainly want a compact, reliable Android phone.
