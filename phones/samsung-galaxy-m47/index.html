Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy M47 5G is a battery-focused mid-range smartphone launched in 2026. It combines a 6.7-inch 120Hz Super AMOLED display with a Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 processor and a large 6000mAh battery. The 50MP OIS main camera is joined by ultrawide and macro cameras, while 45W charging and bypass charging make the phone particularly useful for users who spend long hours gaming or consuming media. Samsung also provides long-term software and security support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy M47 5G?
The Samsung Galaxy M47 5G is priced at $249. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the Samsung Galaxy M47 5G use?
The Samsung Galaxy M47 5G is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 with an octa-core CPU and Adreno 710 GPU.
What is the battery capacity?
The Samsung Galaxy M47 5G has a 6000 mAh battery with 45W wired fast charging and bypass charging support.
When was the Samsung Galaxy M47 5G released?
The Samsung Galaxy M47 5G was released on June 29, 2026.
Does the Samsung Galaxy M47 5G support 5G?
Yes, the Samsung Galaxy M47 5G supports 5G connectivity.
What is bypass charging on the Galaxy M47?
Bypass charging lets the phone draw power directly from the charger while it's plugged in, instead of routing it through the battery first. This helps reduce battery wear and heat during long gaming or streaming sessions while charging.
