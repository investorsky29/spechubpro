Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Galaxy S25+ is the larger of Samsung's two standard S25 models, with a 6.7-inch QHD+ display and a 4900 mAh battery. It sits between the compact Galaxy S25 and the Galaxy S25 Ultra. It suits buyers who want a big screen and battery without paying for the Ultra's extras such as a stylus.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy, a 3 nm chip with an octa-core CPU at up to 4.47 GHz and an Adreno 830 GPU. RAM is 12GB and storage is 256GB or 512GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch AMOLED display has 1440 x 3120 pixels (about 513 ppi) and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, with HDR10+ support. That is a higher resolution than the 1080p panels on the Galaxy S25 FE and A-series phones, which makes text and fine detail sharper.

Camera Experience

The rear setup includes a 50MP main camera (f/1.8, 1/1.56-inch sensor, optical stabilisation) and a 10MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom. Other rear and front cameras are omitted because we could not verify them for this page. SpecHub has not tested image quality, so no results are claimed.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4900 mAh with 45W wired charging, 15W wireless charging and 4.5W reverse wireless charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15 and One UI 7. Samsung promises seven years of OS and security updates, which is among the longest in the industry. The USB-C port runs at USB 3.2 speeds, and the phone is rated IP68.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a large, sharp screen and a big battery in a flagship Samsung, and who plan to keep the phone for many years, are the main audience.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want the fastest wired charging, a stylus or the longest zoom should look at the Galaxy S25 Ultra. Those who prefer a smaller phone may like the Galaxy S25.

Editorial Verdict

The Galaxy S25+ delivers a sharp QHD+ display, a flagship chip and a seven-year update promise. Its main limits are the 45W wired charging speed and the lack of a card slot.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Galaxy S25+ and the Galaxy S25?
The S25+ has a 6.7-inch 1440 x 3120 display and a 4900 mAh battery. The S25 has a 6.2-inch 1080 x 2340 display and a 4000 mAh battery. Both use the Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy.
How long does the Galaxy S25+ get updates?
Samsung promises seven years of OS and security updates.
How fast does the Galaxy S25+ charge?
Wired charging is listed at 45W, wireless charging at 15W and reverse wireless charging at 4.5W.
Does the Galaxy S25+ have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 10MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom.
Is the Galaxy S25+ water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68.
How much did the Galaxy S25+ cost at launch?
In the US, the 12GB + 256GB model launched at $999. Prices vary by country, configuration and promotions.
