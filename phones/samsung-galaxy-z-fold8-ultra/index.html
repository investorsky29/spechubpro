Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is Samsung's flagship foldable for 2026, combining an expansive 8.0-inch QXGA+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X main display with a 6.5-inch 120Hz cover screen. It is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy and offers up to 16GB of RAM with 1TB of storage. Its rear camera system features a 200MP OIS main camera, 50MP ultrawide and 10MP 3x optical telephoto camera. The 5000mAh dual battery supports 45W wired charging and 20W wireless charging, while Android 17 and One UI 9 power the software experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra?
The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is priced at $2,099, depending on the RAM and storage configuration chosen.
What chipset does the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra use?
The Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy chipset paired with an Adreno GPU.
Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra water resistant?
The Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra carries an IP48 rating, which protects against dust and immersion but offers less protection than the IP68 rating found on many conventional (non-foldable) flagships.
Does the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra support wireless charging?
Yes, the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra supports 20W fast wireless charging in addition to 45W wired charging, plus Wireless PowerShare for charging other devices.
Does the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra have a telephoto camera?
Yes, the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra has a 10MP telephoto camera with OIS and 3x optical zoom, alongside its 200MP main camera and 50MP ultrawide camera.
Does the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra support microSD storage expansion?
No, the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra does not have a microSD card slot. Storage is fixed at 256GB, 512GB, or 1TB depending on the model chosen.
