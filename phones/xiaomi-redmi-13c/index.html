Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Xiaomi Redmi 13C was announced and released on November 10, 2023. It is an entry-level 4G phone with a 6.74-inch 90Hz IPS LCD, the MediaTek Helio G85 chipset, a 50MP main camera and a 5,000mAh battery.

This page covers the 4G Redmi 13C. A separate Redmi 13C 5G exists with different hardware, so make sure you are comparing the right version.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G85 (MT6769Z, 12 nm), with two 2.0GHz Cortex-A75 and six 1.8GHz Cortex-A55 cores and a Mali-G52 MC2 GPU. Xiaomi lists 4GB + 128GB, 6GB + 128GB and 8GB + 256GB versions with LPDDR4X RAM and eMMC 5.1 storage, though options differ by region.

A dedicated microSDXC slot is available. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.74-inch IPS LCD at 720 x 1600 pixels (about 260 pixels per inch) with up to 90Hz refresh, listed at 450 nits typical and 600 nits in high-brightness mode. Xiaomi lists Corning Gorilla Glass, TÜV Rheinland low blue light and flicker-free certifications (software solution), and DC dimming.

The 8MP front camera (f/2.0) records 1080p video at 30fps.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 50MP main (f/1.8, PDAF) with a 2MP macro camera and an auxiliary lens. Video is limited to 1080p at 30fps. There is no ultrawide or telephoto camera.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,000mAh (typical) with 18W PD charging support. Xiaomi's regional pages show a 10W adapter in the box in some markets and no adapter in others, so the charging speed you get may depend on the charger you use. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 13 and MIUI 14. Connectivity includes 4G LTE, dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.3, GPS with GLONASS, GALILEO and BDS, and a USB Type-C port. It also keeps a 3.5mm headphone jack and FM radio, and NFC is listed as market dependent. The fingerprint sensor is side-mounted.

A software-update period for this model was not confirmed from the sources used and is not listed. No water-resistance rating is listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a low-cost phone with a large battery, expandable storage, a headphone jack and FM radio will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, a sharper or AMOLED screen, a faster chipset, 4K video or an ultrawide camera should look at a more expensive phone. Buyers who want the 5G model should look at the Redmi 13C 5G.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Redmi 13C is a basic, practical budget phone: a big battery, a 90Hz screen, a microSD slot and a headphone jack, with a modest chipset and HD+ resolution as the trade-offs. It suits first-time buyers, secondary phones and users who prioritise battery and expandable storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Redmi 13C a 5G phone?
No. The Redmi 13C covered here is a 4G LTE phone with the MediaTek Helio G85. A separate Redmi 13C 5G exists with different specifications.
Which chipset does the Redmi 13C use?
It uses the MediaTek Helio G85 (MT6769Z, 12 nm) with a Mali-G52 MC2 GPU.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 5,000mAh with 18W PD charging supported. Xiaomi lists a 10W adapter in the box in some markets and none in others.
Does it have a headphone jack, microSD slot and FM radio?
Yes to all three. GSMArena and Xiaomi list a 3.5mm jack and a dedicated microSDXC slot, and Xiaomi lists FM radio support.
What RAM and storage options are there?
Xiaomi lists 4GB + 128GB, 6GB + 128GB and 8GB + 256GB, with LPDDR4X RAM and eMMC 5.1 storage. Available options vary by region.
What cameras does it have?
A 50MP main camera (f/1.8), a 2MP macro camera and an auxiliary lens on the back, plus an 8MP front camera. Video is recorded at up to 1080p and 30fps.
What is the display like?
A 6.74-inch IPS LCD at 720 x 1600 pixels with up to 90Hz refresh rate, 450 nits typical and 600 nits in high-brightness mode.
