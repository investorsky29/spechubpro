Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Galaxy Z Fold7 is Samsung's 2025 book-style foldable. It opens to an 8.0-inch display and runs the same Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy chip as the Galaxy S25+. It was released on July 25, 2025 at about $2,000 in the US. It is aimed at buyers who want a tablet-size screen that folds into a phone.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy (3 nm) with 12GB of RAM. Storage is 256GB, 512GB or 1TB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone, and details such as CPU clock and GPU are omitted because we could not verify them for this model.

Display Experience

The inner display is an 8.0-inch foldable AMOLED at 1968 x 2184 pixels, a nearly square shape that suits reading, multitasking and video. The cover display details are not listed here because they could not be verified.

Camera Experience

A 10MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom, f/2.4 and optical stabilisation is listed. Other rear and front cameras are omitted because we could not verify them for this page. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4400 mAh. Charging speeds are not listed here because they could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 16, and Samsung promises seven years of OS and security updates. The USB-C port runs at USB 3.2 speeds.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Heavy multitaskers, readers and video watchers who want an 8.0-inch screen that folds to phone size, and who accept the price, will benefit most.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want the lowest price or the largest battery should consider a non-folding phone such as the Galaxy S25+. Those who want a smaller foldable should look at the Galaxy Z Flip7.

Editorial Verdict

The Galaxy Z Fold7 combines a flagship chip with an 8.0-inch inner display and strong update support. Its price and modest 4400 mAh battery are the main trade-offs.
