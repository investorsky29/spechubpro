Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 9 Pro Fold is Google's second book-style foldable, released on September 4, 2024 at $1,799 in the US. It has an 8.0-inch inner display and a 6.3-inch cover display, and it uses the same Tensor G4 chip as the rest of the Pixel 9 line. It is aimed at buyers who want a tablet-size screen that folds into a phone.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Google Tensor G4 with the Titan M2 security chip, 16GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. Google promises seven years of OS and security updates. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The inner display is an 8.0-inch LTPO OLED at 2076 x 2152 pixels with a 1-120Hz refresh rate. The cover display is a 6.3-inch OLED at 1080 x 2424 pixels with 60-120Hz. A near-square inner screen suits reading, multitasking and video.

Camera Experience

The rear setup is a triple camera, and there are two 10MP front cameras, one for each display. Rear lens details are omitted because we could not verify them for this page. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4650 mAh with wired charging of up to 45W and Qi wireless charging. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14. It has a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, face unlock and an IPX8 rating. IPX8 covers water immersion but not dust.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a large foldable screen in a Pixel, and do not mind the weight, will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want the lowest price, a lighter phone or a dust-resistance rating should consider a non-folding Pixel such as the Pixel 9 Pro.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 9 Pro Fold combines Tensor G4, 16GB of RAM and a large inner display with long software support. Its weight, thickness and price are the main trade-offs.
