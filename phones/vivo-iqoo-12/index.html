Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iQOO 12 launched in China in November 2023 and in India on December 12, 2023, with sales starting on December 14. It was the first phone in India with the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset and the first non-Pixel phone there to ship with Android 14. It is a gaming-oriented flagship with a 6.78-inch 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display, a 5,000mAh battery and 120W charging.

This page covers the iQOO 12 sold in India and Southeast Asia, where it runs Funtouch OS 14. China received the iQOO 12 with OriginOS 4 and also a separate iQOO 12 Pro, which has different hardware and is not covered here.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 (4 nm) with an Adreno 750 GPU, backed by iQOO's own Q1 chip for gaming. RAM is LPDDR5X and storage is UFS 4.0. India offered 12GB + 256GB and 16GB + 512GB versions, while Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia received only the 16GB + 512GB version.

A 6,010 mm² stainless steel vapour chamber handles cooling. SpecHub has not run benchmarks or thermal tests, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch flat 1.5K LTPO AMOLED at 1260 x 2800 pixels with a variable refresh rate up to 144Hz, HDR10+ and 2160Hz PWM dimming. iQOO advertises up to 1,400 nits in high-brightness mode and 3,000 nits peak brightness, which are stated figures and not SpecHub measurements.

The 16MP front camera sits in a punch-hole cut-out.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main (1/1.3-inch OmniVision OV50H, f/1.68, optical stabilisation), a 50MP ultrawide and a 64MP periscope telephoto (f/2.57, optical stabilisation, 3x optical zoom, up to 100x digital zoom). A V3 imaging chip supports the camera pipeline, and video recording goes up to 8K.

The ultrawide field of view varies between sources and is not listed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,000mAh with 120W wired charging, and a charger is included in the box. No wireless charging is listed for this model. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and Funtouch OS 14 in India and Southeast Asia. Connectivity includes 5G, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4 and NFC, and an optical in-display fingerprint sensor handles biometrics. India offered the Legend and Alpha colour versions.

A software-update period and a water-resistance rating were not confirmed and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a gaming-focused flagship with a fast 144Hz screen, a big battery, a bundled 120W charger and a capable periscope telephoto will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want wireless charging, a confirmed water-resistance rating or a newer chipset should look at a more recent flagship. Buyers who want stronger camera zoom should look at the vivo X100 Pro or later X-series models.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the iQOO 12 is a performance-first flagship with a 144Hz display, Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, a 120W charger in the box and a useful 3x periscope, and it lacks wireless charging and a confirmed IP rating. It suits gamers and power users who want flagship speed at an aggressive price.
