Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The original Google Pixel Fold was Google's first foldable phone, announced on May 10, 2023 and released on June 27, 2023 at $1,799 in the US. It has a 7.6-inch inner display and a 5.8-inch cover screen. It is now an older foldable that has been replaced by the Pixel 9 Pro Fold and Pixel 10 Pro Fold.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Google Tensor G2 (5 nm) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. There is no memory card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The inner display is a 7.6-inch foldable OLED at 1840 x 2208 pixels with a 120Hz refresh rate and HDR10+. The cover display is a 5.8-inch OLED at 1080 x 2092 pixels, also at 120Hz, with Gorilla Glass Victus protection.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 48MP main (f/1.7, optical stabilisation), a 10.8MP ultrawide and a 10.8MP telephoto with 5x optical zoom. The front camera is 10MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4821 mAh with 21W wired charging and 8W wireless charging. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 13. It is rated IPX8, which covers water but not dust, and supports a Nano-SIM and an eSIM.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want an inexpensive entry into foldables and are comfortable with an older chip and shorter remaining support may find it appealing at a secondhand price.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want newer chips, faster charging, a lighter body or dust resistance should look at the Pixel 9 Pro Fold or Pixel 10 Pro Fold.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel Fold introduced Google's book-style foldable with a wide cover screen and a telephoto camera. Its weight, slow charging and age are the main limits today.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel Fold and the Pixel 9 Pro Fold?
The Pixel Fold has a Tensor G2, a 7.6-inch inner display, a 4821 mAh battery and weighs 283 g. The Pixel 9 Pro Fold has a Tensor G4, an 8.0-inch inner display, a 4650 mAh battery and weighs 257 g.
How big are the Pixel Fold's displays?
The inner display is 7.6 inches and the cover display is 5.8 inches.
Is the Pixel Fold water resistant?
It is rated IPX8 for water. There is no dust-resistance rating.
How fast does the Pixel Fold charge?
Wired charging is 21W and wireless charging is 8W.
How much RAM and storage does it have?
It has 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage.
How much did the Pixel Fold cost at launch?
In the US, it launched at $1,799. Prices elsewhere differ, and it is no longer sold new by Google.
