Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Galaxy S26 Ultra is the top model of Samsung's 2026 S-series, announced on February 25, 2026. It has a 6.9-inch QHD+ display, a built-in S Pen, a four-camera rear system with a 200MP main sensor, and a Privacy Display feature. It is aimed at buyers who want the most capable Galaxy, including note-taking and long zoom.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy in all markets, unlike the S26 and S26+, which also use the Exynos 2600 in some regions. RAM is 12GB, or 16GB on the 1TB version, and storage is 256GB, 512GB or 1TB. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.9-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display has 1440 x 3120 pixels and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz. Samsung's Privacy Display feature limits how well the screen can be seen from the side. Samsung has acknowledged that the screen can look slightly dimmer than the Galaxy S25 Ultra's, especially at maximum brightness when viewed at an angle.

Camera Experience

The rear system has a 200MP wide camera, a 50MP ultrawide, a 50MP telephoto and a 10MP telephoto. Two telephoto lenses give more zoom options than the S26 and S26+, which have one. Aperture, zoom ratio and front-camera details are omitted because we could not verify them. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh. Wired charging goes up to 60W, which Samsung says reaches about 75% in 30 minutes with a 60W adapter, and wireless charging is 25W. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone ships with Android 16 and Samsung's One UI, and includes a built-in S Pen. SIM options vary by market, with dual Nano-SIM and eSIM listed. Update-policy and water-resistance ratings are not listed here because they were not verified for this model.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Power users who want a stylus, long zoom, fast charging and a big QHD+ display will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who find 214 g and a 6.9-inch screen too large may prefer the Galaxy S26 or S26+. Buyers on a budget should consider those models.

Editorial Verdict

The Galaxy S26 Ultra is the most feature-packed Galaxy of 2026, with an S Pen, a four-camera setup and the fastest charging in the series. Its size, weight and price are the trade-offs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Galaxy S26 Ultra and the S26+?
The Ultra has a 6.9-inch display, a built-in S Pen, a 200MP main camera with two telephoto lenses, a 5000 mAh battery and 60W charging. The S26+ has a 6.7-inch display, a 50MP main camera with one telephoto lens, a 4900 mAh battery and 45W charging.
Which chipset does the Galaxy S26 Ultra use?
It uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy in all markets.
How fast does the Galaxy S26 Ultra charge?
Wired charging goes up to 60W, which Samsung says reaches about 75% in 30 minutes with a 60W adapter. Wireless charging is 25W.
Does the Galaxy S26 Ultra have an S Pen?
Yes. It has a built-in S Pen.
How much RAM and storage does it have?
It has 12GB of RAM, or 16GB on the 1TB model. Storage options are 256GB, 512GB and 1TB.
How much does the Galaxy S26 Ultra cost?
In the US, the 256GB model started at $1,299, and European pricing started at €1,469. Prices vary by country, storage and promotions.
