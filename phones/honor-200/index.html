Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor 200 is the standard model of Honor's 2024 line, launched in China in May 2024. It combines a slim 7.7 mm body with a portrait-focused camera set that includes a telephoto lens. It suits buyers who want a mid-range phone with fast charging and a flexible camera.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 (4 nm) with 8GB or 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. The Honor 200 Pro uses the more powerful Snapdragon 8s Gen 3. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch OLED display has 1200 x 2664 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate, and Honor rates its peak brightness at up to 4,000 nits. It is flat rather than curved at the edges.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main (f/2.0, 1/1.56-inch sensor, optical stabilisation), a 50MP telephoto (f/2.4, 2.5x optical zoom, optical stabilisation) and a 12MP ultrawide (f/2.2, 112 degrees). The front camera is 50MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5200 mAh with 100W wired charging and 5W reverse wired charging. Wireless charging is not supported, unlike the Honor 200 Pro, which supports 66W wireless charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and MagicOS 8.0, Honor's own interface. It has dual Nano-SIM and an in-screen fingerprint sensor. A water-resistance rating is not listed for this phone.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who care about portraits and zoom, and want fast charging in a slim body at a mid-range price, will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want wireless charging, a faster chip or an IP rating should look at the Honor 200 Pro or the Honor 400 series.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor 200 delivers an unusually flexible camera set and very fast charging for its class. The lack of wireless charging and a modest chip are the main limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Honor 200 and the Honor 200 Pro?
The Honor 200 has a 6.7-inch display, a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3, 100W wired charging and no wireless charging. The Honor 200 Pro has a 6.78-inch display, a Snapdragon 8s Gen 3, 100W wired charging and 66W wireless charging.
How fast does the Honor 200 charge?
Wired charging is 100W with a 5200 mAh battery. Wireless charging is not supported.
Does the Honor 200 have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 50MP telephoto camera with 2.5x optical zoom.
Which chip does the Honor 200 use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3.
Which software does the Honor 200 run?
It launched with Android 14 and MagicOS 8.0.
How much does the Honor 200 cost?
