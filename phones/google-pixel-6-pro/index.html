Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 6 Pro was Google's 2021 flagship, released on October 28, 2021 at $899 in the US. It introduced the first-generation Google Tensor chip and a big redesign with a camera bar. It suits buyers who want a large display, 12GB of RAM and a triple camera in a Pixel.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the first-generation Google Tensor (5 nm) with 12GB of RAM and storage of 128GB, 256GB or 512GB, plus the Titan M2 security chip. The Pixel 7 Pro that followed moved to the Tensor G2. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch LTPO OLED display has 1440 x 3120 pixels and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, protected by Gorilla Glass Victus. An optical fingerprint sensor sits under the screen.

Camera Experience

The Pixel 6 Pro has a triple rear camera. Resolution, zoom and front-camera details are omitted because we could not verify them for this page. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5003 mAh with fast wired charging, fast wireless charging and reverse wireless charging. Wattage figures are not listed because they could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 12. It has stereo speakers, a USB-C port and IP68 water and dust resistance.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a large, smooth display, 12GB of RAM and wireless charging in a Pixel will find it a good match, particularly at a secondhand price.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want the newer Tensor G2 chip or a longer software-support horizon should look at the Pixel 7 Pro or later. Those who prefer a smaller phone may like the Pixel 6a.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 6 Pro offers a big LTPO display, 12GB of RAM and a large battery. Its main limits are its size and an older chip generation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel 6 Pro and the Pixel 7 Pro?
The Pixel 7 Pro uses the Tensor G2 and has a 5000 mAh battery. The Pixel 6 Pro uses the first-generation Tensor and has a 5003 mAh battery. Both have 12GB of RAM and a 6.7-inch 1440 x 3120 display.
Does the Pixel 6 Pro have a 120Hz display?
Yes. It supports a refresh rate of up to 120Hz.
Is the Pixel 6 Pro water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68.
Does it support wireless charging?
Yes. It supports fast wireless charging and reverse wireless charging.
How much RAM and storage does the Pixel 6 Pro have?
It has 12GB of RAM and storage of 128GB, 256GB or 512GB.
How much did the Pixel 6 Pro cost at launch?
In the US, the 12GB + 128GB model launched at $899. Prices elsewhere differ, and current prices vary by retailer.
