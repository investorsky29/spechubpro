Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Galaxy S25 FE is the lower-priced Fan Edition of the S25 series, released on September 4, 2025. It has a 6.7-inch 120Hz display and a 4900 mAh battery, but uses the Exynos 2400 chip rather than the Snapdragon 8 Elite of the S25+. It suits buyers who want a large, water-resistant Samsung phone without paying flagship prices.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Samsung Exynos 2400 with a ten-core CPU at up to 3.2 GHz and an Xclipse 940 GPU. RAM is 8GB and storage is 128GB, 256GB or 512GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch AMOLED display has 1080 x 2340 pixels (about 385 ppi) and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, with HDR10+. It is less sharp than the 1440 x 3120 panel on the Galaxy S25+.

Camera Experience

A 50MP main camera with optical stabilisation is listed. Samsung also fits an ultrawide and a telephoto camera, but we could not verify their details, so they are omitted. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4900 mAh, the same capacity as the Galaxy S25+. Charging speeds are not listed here because they could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 16. It has an in-screen optical fingerprint sensor, a USB-C 3.2 port and IP68 water and dust resistance.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a big screen, a large battery and water resistance in a Samsung phone at a lower price than the S25+ will find it a fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a sharper QHD+ display or the Snapdragon 8 Elite chip should look at the Galaxy S25+. Those who want a compact phone may prefer the Galaxy S25.

Editorial Verdict

The Galaxy S25 FE offers the S25 series' big-battery formula with older silicon and a 1080p display at a lower price. It is a value pick rather than a flagship.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Galaxy S25 FE and the Galaxy S25+?
Both have a 6.7-inch 120Hz AMOLED display and a 4900 mAh battery. The S25 FE has a 1080 x 2340 display, an Exynos 2400 and 8GB of RAM. The S25+ has a 1440 x 3120 display, a Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy and 12GB of RAM.
What chipset does the Galaxy S25 FE use?
It uses the Samsung Exynos 2400 with an Xclipse 940 GPU.
Is the Galaxy S25 FE water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68.
Which Android version does it run?
It launched with Android 16.
Does it have a memory card slot?
No. Storage options are 128GB, 256GB and 512GB.
How much did the Galaxy S25 FE cost at launch?
In the US, the 8GB + 128GB model launched at about $650. Prices vary by country, configuration and promotions.
