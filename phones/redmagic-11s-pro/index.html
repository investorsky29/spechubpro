Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results.

Phone Overview

The RedMagic 11S Pro is the newest phone in this group, listed with a June 2026 release. SpecHub's listed price of $1,299 could not be confirmed against an official source, so check the manufacturer's store for the current price. It builds on the 11 Pro with a higher-clocked Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 Leading Version, a cooling system called AquaCore, and a specification table that lists 80W wireless charging.

It is offered in only two colours, Subzero and Nightfreeze, and with a narrower memory range than the 11 Pro. It is aimed at buyers who want the most capable RedMagic specification in this group.

Performance and Processor

The chip is the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 Leading Version, with the CPU listed at up to 4.74GHz, compared with 4.6GHz for the 11 Pro. The GPU is listed only as Adreno, with no model number, so the table does not tell you which generation it is. RAM is 12GB or 16GB and storage is 256GB or 512GB, which is less than the 11 Pro's 24GB and 1TB options.

The AquaCore system combines liquid cooling with a fan listed at 24,000 RPM, compared with 23,000 RPM on the 10S Pro and 10S Pro+. SpecHub has not tested sustained performance, so we cannot say how much these differences help in real games.

Display Experience

The 6.85-inch AMOLED screen is listed as a BOE panel at 1216 x 2688 pixels (about 430 pixels per inch) with a refresh rate of up to 144Hz. The resolution matches the other RedMagic pages here, so the display itself is not the main upgrade.

The under-display 16MP selfie camera keeps the screen free of cut-outs during games and video. As with other under-display designs, selfie quality may be lower than a traditional front camera.

Camera Experience

This is the only page in the group that lists aperture values: f/1.88 on the 50MP main camera with OIS and f/2.0 on the 50MP ultrawide. A lower f-number means a wider opening, which lets in more light, but the table does not give sensor sizes, so we cannot judge low-light results from it. A 2MP macro and a 16MP under-display front camera complete the list, and no telephoto lens is listed.

SpecHub has not tested image quality, so we make no claims about how the camera compares with other flagship phones.

Battery and Charging

The 7500mAh battery matches the 11 Pro. Wired charging is 80W, and the specification table lists 80W wireless charging as well. Launch coverage of the global model describes both wired and wireless charging, and reports that the China-market version has no wireless charging, so check which version you are buying.

We have no endurance or charge-time results for this phone.

Software and Features

The table lists Android 16 but does not name the RedMagic OS version. It supports 5G on Sub-6GHz bands, Bluetooth 5.4, NFC and USB-C 3.2 Gen 2, with dual Nano-SIM. The Wi-Fi entry says Wi-Fi 6, but launch coverage and retailer listings we found describe Wi-Fi 7 support, so this table entry is unconfirmed. Check it if Wi-Fi 7 matters to you.

RedMagic's product page states IPX8 water resistance, and its gaming package includes shoulder triggers and RGB lighting. The specification table has no IP rating row.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

It suits buyers who want the highest-clocked RedMagic chip, the largest listed cooling fan and the option of wireless charging, and who see price as a secondary concern. It may also appeal to people who want a documented aperture on the main camera.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Anyone watching their budget should look at the 11 Pro, which lists the same battery capacity and screen size at a lower price. Buyers who need 24GB of RAM or 1TB of storage will not find them in this table. People who want Wi-Fi 7 should check this phone's actual spec first, and photography-led buyers will find more focused options elsewhere.

Editorial Verdict

The 11S Pro offers the highest listed clock speed, the strongest cooling hardware and listed wireless charging. The case for it rests on those extras, since the screen, battery capacity and camera list are largely the same as the 11 Pro. It costs more than the 11 Pro, so it suits enthusiasts who want the extras. Several specification entries on this page need verification, so double-check the details that matter to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the 11S Pro different from the 11 Pro?
The differences on paper are a higher-clocked chip (up to 4.74GHz against 4.6GHz), AquaCore cooling with a 24,000 RPM fan, 80W wireless charging listed in the table, and aperture values on the main and ultrawide cameras. The screen size, resolution and battery capacity are the same. The 11 Pro also offers more RAM and storage options, so compare them against your own needs.
Does the RedMagic 11S Pro support wireless charging?
The specification table lists 80W wireless charging, and launch coverage of the global model describes wired and wireless charging. The China-market version was reported to have no wireless charging, so confirm which version you are buying.
Does it have Wi-Fi 6 or Wi-Fi 7?
The table on this page lists Wi-Fi 6. Launch coverage and retailer listings we found describe Wi-Fi 7, so this entry is unconfirmed. Check the official specification if it matters to you.
Is the RedMagic 11S Pro water resistant?
RedMagic's product page states IPX8 water resistance. The specification table on this page has no IP rating row, so check the manufacturer's current specification for your region.
What memory options are available?
The table lists 12GB or 16GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage, with no memory card slot. The 11 Pro lists up to 24GB and 1TB.
What do the camera aperture values mean?
The main camera is listed at f/1.88 and the ultrawide at f/2.0. A lower f-number means a wider aperture, which lets in more light. Sensor size also affects low-light results and is not listed.
What are the listed price and release date?
SpecHub lists the RedMagic 11S Pro at $1,299 and a June 2026 release. We could not confirm the $1,299 figure, and launch coverage reported a lower global starting price. Prices vary by region, configuration and retailer, so check the official store.
