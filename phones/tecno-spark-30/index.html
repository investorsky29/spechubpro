Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Spark 30 is a 4G entry-level phone that was first launched in Ghana in 2024 with a 6.78-inch 90Hz display, a MediaTek Helio G91 chipset, a 64MP main camera and a 5,000mAh battery with 18W charging.

It sits between the cheaper Spark 30C and the Spark 30 Pro, which has a Helio G100 chipset and an AMOLED display.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G91 (12 nm) with a Mali-G52 MP2 GPU. It comes with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is 6.78 inches with a 90Hz refresh rate and a stated 800-nit peak brightness, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement. Its density of about 396 pixels per inch indicates a Full HD+ class resolution. The panel type and protection glass were not confirmed and are not listed.

Camera Experience

The main camera is a 64MP Sony IMX682 sensor in a circular camera island, and the front camera is 13MP in a centred punch-hole. Other rear cameras and video modes were not confirmed and are not listed.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,000mAh with 18W charging. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 14. Water resistance, connectivity versions and the software-update period were not confirmed and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a large screen, a big battery and a high-resolution main camera at a very low price will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, an AMOLED display or faster charging should look at the Spark 30 Pro or a more expensive phone.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Spark 30 is a basic 4G phone with a big screen and battery and a 64MP camera, with slow charging and an entry-level chip as its main limits. It suits budget buyers who prioritise screen size and battery.
