Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Smart 8 is an entry-level phone launched in December 2023, successor to the Smart 7. It has a 6.6-inch 90Hz screen, a Unisoc T606 chip and a 5000 mAh battery. It is aimed at first-time smartphone buyers and anyone who needs a basic, long-lasting phone.

Performance and Processor

The global model uses the Unisoc T606 chipset with 3GB of RAM and 64GB of storage, expandable by microSD. Some markets, including India, sell versions with a different chip, so check your region's listing. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.6-inch IPS LCD has 1612 x 720 pixels (about 267 ppi) and a 90Hz refresh rate. A Dynamic Bar feature uses the punch-hole area to show status updates and charging information.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 13MP main (f/1.8) with a VGA depth sensor. The front camera was not verified for this page. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh with 10W wired charging, so a full charge takes a long time. Wireless charging is not supported. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 13 and XOS 13. It weighs 188.5 g and comes in four colours.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a very cheap phone for calls, messaging and basic apps, with a long-lasting battery, will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want faster charging, more RAM or a better camera should look at the Hot 40 series or the Smart 9.

Editorial Verdict

The Infinix Smart 8 covers the basics at a very low price, with a large battery and expandable storage. Its performance and charging speed are modest.
