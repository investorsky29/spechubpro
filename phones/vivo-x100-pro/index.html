Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The vivo X100 Pro was announced in China on November 13, 2023 and released there on November 21, then reached global markets from December 2023. It is vivo's camera-focused flagship of that generation, with a MediaTek Dimensity 9300 chipset, a 6.78-inch 1.5K LTPO AMOLED display and a triple 50MP ZEISS camera system.

It was the first phone with a ZEISS APO-certified telephoto lens. The X100 Pro was followed by the vivo X200 Pro and then the vivo X300 Pro, and the standard vivo X100 is a separate model with a 5,000mAh battery and 120W charging.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 9300 (4 nm), a design in which all eight cores are performance-class: one 3.25GHz Cortex-X4, three 2.85GHz Cortex-X4 and four 2.0GHz Cortex-A720 cores, with an Immortalis-G720 MC12 GPU. A separate vivo V3 imaging chip handles camera processing.

Memory is LPDDR5X (LPDDR5T on the 16GB + 1TB China version) with UFS 4.0 storage. Configurations differ by market, and there is no card slot. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch curved 1.5K LTPO AMOLED at 1260 x 2800 pixels with a 1 to 120Hz variable refresh rate and HDR10+. A peak brightness of 3,000 nits is advertised, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

The phone is rated IP68, so it is dust tight and can be immersed up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes. Water resistance is not permanent.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP 1-inch type main camera (Sony IMX989, f/1.75, optical stabilisation), a 50MP ultrawide (f/2.0, floating lens design, autofocus) and a 50MP periscope telephoto (f/2.5, 100mm equivalent, 4.3x optical zoom). The telephoto is ZEISS APO certified and has a macro mode, and digital zoom goes up to 100x.

Sources differ on the exact telephoto sensor size, so it is not listed. The selfie camera was not confirmed and is not listed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,400mAh. Wired charging is 100W, advertised as reaching 50% in roughly 12.5 to 14 minutes depending on the source, and the adapter in the box is rated at 120W even though the phone supports up to 100W. Wireless charging is 50W, and reverse wired charging is supported. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests.

SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14. Global versions run Funtouch OS and Chinese versions run OriginOS. A software-update period for the global version was not confirmed and is not listed. It has dual-SIM options (Nano-SIM plus eSIM, or two Nano-SIMs) and different network bands for International and China versions.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a 1-inch main sensor, a long-range APO telephoto and fast wired and wireless charging, and who are comfortable with a phone from the previous generation, will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a newer chipset, longer update support or a 200MP telephoto should look at the vivo X200 Pro or X300 Pro. Buyers who want a memory card slot or a lighter phone need a different device.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the X100 Pro is a camera-first flagship with a 1-inch main sensor, a 4.3x APO telephoto, a large battery and fast charging, all in an IP68 body. Its main limits are age, weight and the lack of a card slot. It suits people who prioritise camera hardware over having the newest chip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chipset does the vivo X100 Pro use?
It uses the MediaTek Dimensity 9300 (4 nm) with an Immortalis-G720 MC12 GPU and vivo's V3 imaging chip.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 5,400mAh. Charging is 100W wired and 50W wireless, with reverse wired charging supported.
What cameras does the vivo X100 Pro have?
A 50MP 1-inch main camera, a 50MP ultrawide and a 50MP ZEISS APO periscope telephoto with 4.3x optical zoom.
Is the vivo X100 Pro waterproof?
It is rated IP68, dust tight and immersible up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes. Water resistance is not permanent.
What RAM and storage options are there?
Configurations include 12GB + 256GB, 16GB + 512GB and 16GB + 1TB in China. The global launch offered a single 16GB + 512GB version. There is no memory card slot.
How much did the vivo X100 Pro cost at launch?
In China it started at ¥4,999 for 12GB + 256GB, with ¥5,499 for 16GB + 512GB and ¥5,999 for 16GB + 1TB. A verified global launch price was not confirmed, and prices vary by country, retailer and storage size.
What is the difference between the X100 and X100 Pro?
The X100 Pro has a 5,400mAh battery, 100W wired and 50W wireless charging, a 1-inch main sensor and a 50MP APO telephoto. The standard X100 has a 5,000mAh battery with 120W charging and a 64MP telephoto.
Does it run the same software worldwide?
No. Global versions run Funtouch OS, while Chinese versions run OriginOS.
