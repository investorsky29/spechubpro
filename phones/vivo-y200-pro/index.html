Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The vivo Y200 Pro 5G was announced and launched in India on May 21, 2024, at ₹24,999 for a single 8GB + 128GB version. It is a mid-range 5G phone built around a 6.78-inch curved Full HD+ AMOLED display, a Snapdragon 695 chipset, a 64MP main camera with optical stabilisation and a 5,000mAh battery.

vivo described it as the first phone in its Y series with a 3D curved display. This page covers the India model of the Y200 Pro; other Y200-series phones, such as the Y200 GT with a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3, are different devices.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 695 5G (6 nm) with an Adreno 619 GPU, a mid-range chip. It is paired with 8GB of LPDDR4X RAM, which can be extended virtually by up to 8GB, and 128GB of UFS 2.2 storage.

Memory card support was reported differently by different sources, so it is not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch curved AMOLED at 1080 x 2400 pixels with a 120Hz refresh rate and a 2.3 mm bezel. A peak brightness of 1,300 nits is advertised, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

The 16MP selfie camera (f/2.45) sits in a punch-hole notch, and an optical fingerprint sensor is built into the display. The phone is rated IP54, so it is dust protected and splash resistant but not meant for immersion.

Camera Experience

The rear setup is a 64MP main camera (f/1.79, PDAF, optical stabilisation) and a 2MP depth sensor (f/2.4). There is no ultrawide or macro camera. Software features include Aura Light flash, Super Night Mode and a Bokeh Flare Portrait mode.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,000mAh with 44W wired charging, listed as reaching 50% in 28 minutes, and a 44W charger is included in the box. No wireless charging is listed. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and Funtouch OS 14. It has a Silk Style glass back that resists fingerprints, dual SIM support and a built-in optical fingerprint sensor. A software-update period was not confirmed and is not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a slim, light phone with a curved AMOLED screen, a stabilised main camera and a charger in the box at a mid-range price will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want stronger performance, an ultrawide camera, a larger battery or faster charging should look at a phone with a newer chipset.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Y200 Pro is a stylish, lightweight mid-range phone with a bright curved AMOLED screen and a stabilised 64MP camera, held back by an older chipset and a limited camera set. It suits people who value design and screen quality over raw speed.
