Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Xiaomi 17 was announced in China on September 25, 2025 and released on September 27, 2025. It reached global markets in early 2026 together with the Xiaomi 17 Ultra, while the Xiaomi 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max stayed China-only. Xiaomi skipped the "16" name entirely, so the 17 follows the Xiaomi 15.

It is the compact model of the series: a 6.3-inch screen, a 191 g body, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and a triple 50MP camera system. The main regional difference is the battery, listed at 6,330mAh for the global model and 7,000mAh in a China listing.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 (SM8850-AC, 3 nm), with two Oryon V3 Phoenix L cores at up to 4.6GHz and six Phoenix M cores at 3.62GHz, plus an Adreno 840 GPU. RAM is 12GB or 16GB, storage is UFS 4.1, and there is no card slot.

Global versions are listed with 256GB and 512GB of storage; a 1TB option appears for China. SpecHub has not run benchmarks on this phone, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.3-inch LTPO AMOLED panel at 1220 x 2656 pixels (about 460 pixels per inch) with a 120Hz refresh rate and 2160Hz PWM dimming. It supports Dolby Vision, HDR Vivid and HDR10+, and a peak brightness of 3,500 nits is listed, which is a listed figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

Global models use Xiaomi Shield Glass, while China models use Dragon Crystal Glass. The 50MP selfie camera (f/2.2) records 4K video at up to 60fps.

Camera Experience

The three rear cameras are all 50MP: a main (f/1.7), a telephoto (f/2.0, about 2.6x optical zoom) and an ultrawide (f/2.4, 102 degrees), with Leica optics. Laser autofocus and a colour spectrum sensor are also listed.

Video goes up to 8K at 30fps, with 10-bit Dolby Vision and LOG recording at 4K. These are listed capabilities; SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The global Xiaomi 17 has a 6,330mAh battery and the China model 7,000mAh. Wired charging is 100W and wireless is 50W. The European retail package does not include the 100W charger, which Xiaomi sells separately.

SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 16 with HyperOS 3. Connectivity includes 5G (SA and NSA), Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4 with aptX HD, aptX Adaptive and LHDC 5, NFC, an infrared port and a USB Type-C 3.2 port with DisplayPort. It has an ultrasonic under-display fingerprint sensor and IP68 dust and water resistance.

There is no 3.5mm jack and no FM radio. A software-update period for the global Xiaomi 17 is not verified on this page.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a compact flagship with a flagship chipset, a 3x-class telephoto, wireless charging and a flat 6.3-inch screen will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want the longest zoom or a 1-inch main sensor should look at the Xiaomi 17 Ultra. Buyers who want a headphone jack, a card slot or the larger China-model battery need a different phone or version.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Xiaomi 17 is a compact all-rounder: flagship chip, bright LTPO display, three 50MP cameras and fast wired and wireless charging, with the global battery as its main regional compromise. It suits people who want a small, well-equipped Android flagship.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a Xiaomi 16?
No. Xiaomi skipped the "16" name and moved from the Xiaomi 15 series to the Xiaomi 17 series and others.
Which chipset does the Xiaomi 17 use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 (SM8850-AC, 3 nm) with an Adreno 840 GPU.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The global model has a 6,330mAh battery and the China model 7,000mAh. Charging is 100W wired and 50W wireless.
Are the Xiaomi 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max sold globally?
No. The global launch covered the Xiaomi 17 and 17 Ultra, while the Pro and Pro Max stayed China-only.
Is the Xiaomi 17 waterproof?
It is listed as IP68 dust tight and water resistant, immersible up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes. Water resistance is not permanent.
Does it have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 50MP telephoto (f/2.0, about 2.6x optical zoom) alongside a 50MP main and a 50MP ultrawide.
How much does the Xiaomi 17 cost?
The global launch price was €999 for 12GB + 256GB, and ₱53,999 in the Philippines. Prices vary by country, retailer, storage size, taxes and promotions, so check a local seller.
Does it have a headphone jack?
No. The listings show no 3.5mm jack and no FM radio.
