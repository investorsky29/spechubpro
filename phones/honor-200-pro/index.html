Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor 200 Pro is the higher-end model of Honor's 2024 pair, released on May 31, 2024 at €799 in Europe. It has a curved 6.78-inch display, a Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chip and wireless charging. It suits buyers who want a camera-led phone with very fast charging.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 (4 nm) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. The standard Honor 200 uses a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.78-inch OLED display has 1224 x 2700 pixels, dual curved edges and a 120Hz refresh rate. Honor rates the peak brightness at up to 4,000 nits.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main (f/1.9, 1/1.3-inch sensor, optical stabilisation), a 50MP telephoto (f/2.4, 2.5x optical zoom, optical stabilisation) and a 12MP ultrawide (f/2.2, 112 degrees). The front camera is 50MP with a 2MP depth sensor. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5200 mAh with 100W wired charging, 66W wireless charging and 5W reverse charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and MagicOS 8.0. It has dual Nano-SIM and an in-screen fingerprint sensor. Sources disagree on its dust and splash rating, so none is listed here.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want fast wired and wireless charging, a telephoto lens and a high-end chip in a mid-priced phone will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a confirmed water-resistance rating or newer software should look at the Honor 400 Pro. Those who prefer a flat screen may prefer the Honor 200.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor 200 Pro stands out for its 100W and 66W charging and flexible camera set. The unclear durability rating and older software are the main caveats.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Honor 200 Pro and the Honor 400 Pro?
The Honor 200 Pro has a Snapdragon 8s Gen 3, a 5200 mAh battery and 66W wireless charging. The Honor 400 Pro has a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, a 5300 mAh battery, 50W wireless charging and a 200MP main camera.
How fast does the Honor 200 Pro charge?
Wired charging is 100W and wireless charging is 66W, with 5W reverse charging.
Does the Honor 200 Pro have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 50MP telephoto camera with 2.5x optical zoom.
Is the Honor 200 Pro water resistant?
Sources disagree on the exact dust and splash rating, so check the manufacturer's specification before relying on it.
Which chip does it use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8s Gen 3.
How much did the Honor 200 Pro cost at launch?
In Europe, the 12GB + 256GB model launched at €799. Prices elsewhere differ.
