Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Camon 50 is a 4G mid-range phone announced in February 2026, shown at MWC 2026 on March 3 and on sale from March 2026. It is the base model of the Camon 50 series, with a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, the MediaTek Helio G200 Ultimate chipset and a very large battery.

It sits below the Camon 50 Pro models, which add a curved display and a 3x telephoto camera, and the Camon 50 Ultra 5G, which uses a Dimensity chipset. Six colours are listed: Moonlight Black, Malachite Green, Nebula Titanium, Fir Green, Lavender Mist and Mint Cream.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G200 Ultimate (6 nm), paired with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage. CPU and GPU details were not confirmed for this base model and are not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch AMOLED at 1208 x 2644 pixels (about 429 pixels per inch) with a 144Hz refresh rate. Brightness and protection glass were not confirmed and are not listed. Stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos support come with it.

Camera Experience

All Camon 50 models share a 50MP Sony LYT-700C main camera with optical stabilisation using a closed-loop motor. The base Camon 50 is not listed with a telephoto camera; that is added on the Camon 50 Pro models. Details of the secondary rear cameras and the selfie camera were not confirmed and are not listed.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6,150mAh in most listed markets and 6,500mAh in Pakistan, with 45W wired charging. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 16 with HiOS 16 and lists up to 3 major Android upgrades. It is rated IP68, IP69 and IP69K and meets MIL-STD-810 testing, and it has a One-Tap AI key with Tecno's AI suite.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a large battery, a high-refresh AMOLED screen and a high dust and water protection rating at the lower end of the Camon 50 range will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, a telephoto camera or a curved display should look at the Camon 50 Pro 5G or the Camon 50 Ultra 5G.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Camon 50 is a durable 4G phone with a big battery and a smooth 144Hz AMOLED screen, with a mid-range chipset and a limited camera set as its trade-offs. It suits people who prioritise battery life and ruggedness over speed.
