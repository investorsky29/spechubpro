Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor Magic7 Pro is the flagship of Honor's 2024 Magic line, announced in China on October 30, 2024 and launched in Europe on January 15, 2025 at €1,299. It combines a Snapdragon 8 Elite chip, a 200MP periscope telephoto camera and very fast wired and wireless charging. It is aimed at buyers who want a camera-led flagship.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite (3 nm) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.8-inch curved LTPO OLED display has 1280 x 2800 pixels and an adaptive 1-120Hz refresh rate. Honor rates it for up to 5,000 nits of peak brightness, and an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor sits under the glass.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main with optical stabilisation, a 200MP periscope telephoto (f/2.6, 69 mm, 1/1.4-inch sensor, 3x optical zoom) and a 50MP ultrawide. The front camera is 50MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The global model has a 5270 mAh battery, compared with 5850 mAh for the China version. Wired charging is 100W and wireless charging is 80W, with reverse wired and wireless charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15 and MagicOS 9.0. It is rated IP68 and IP69 and supports dual Nano-SIM plus an eSIM. Honor lists AI features such as Magic Portal and Gemini integration.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Photographers who want a long zoom lens with a large sensor, and buyers who value very fast charging, will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want the largest battery should note the smaller 5270 mAh global capacity. Buyers on a tighter budget may prefer the Honor Magic7 Lite or a mid-range model.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor Magic7 Pro is a strong camera and charging flagship. The reduced battery size outside China and the high launch price are the main caveats.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Honor Magic7 Pro's battery capacity?
