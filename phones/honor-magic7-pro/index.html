Expert Review

The Honor Magic7 Pro is Honor's premium flagship smartphone, combining the Snapdragon 8 Elite processor with a stunning 6.8-inch LTPO OLED display and a versatile triple-camera system. Its 200MP periscope telephoto camera delivers exceptional zoom performance, while the 5850mAh silicon-carbon battery provides outstanding endurance with 100W wired and 80W wireless charging. Combined with IP68/IP69 protection, Wi-Fi 7, advanced AI features, and long-lasting performance, the Magic7 Pro stands among the best Android flagships of 2025.
