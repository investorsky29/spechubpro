Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy A57 5G is a 2026 premium mid-range smartphone featuring a slim 6.7-inch 120Hz Super AMOLED display and Samsung's Exynos 1680 processor. Its 50MP OIS main camera is joined by a 12MP ultrawide and 5MP macro camera, while the 5000mAh battery supports 45W fast charging. IP68 protection, Galaxy AI features, and Samsung's long-term software support make the Galaxy A57 a strong choice for users looking for a premium Galaxy experience without moving to the flagship S series.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy A57 5G?
The Samsung Galaxy A57 5G is priced at $549. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the Samsung Galaxy A57 5G use?
The Samsung Galaxy A57 5G is powered by Samsung's own Exynos 1680 chipset with an octa-core CPU and Xclipse GPU.
What is the battery capacity?
The Samsung Galaxy A57 5G has a 5000 mAh battery with 45W wired fast charging.
When was the Samsung Galaxy A57 5G released?
The Samsung Galaxy A57 5G was released on March 25, 2026.
Does the Samsung Galaxy A57 5G support Galaxy AI?
Yes, the Samsung Galaxy A57 5G includes Galaxy AI features as part of its One UI 8.5 software on top of Android 16.
How is the Galaxy A57 different from the Galaxy A37?
The Galaxy A57 sits a tier above the Galaxy A37, using a faster Exynos 1680 chipset instead of the A37's Exynos 1480, a slimmer 6.9mm body, and a higher $549 price compared to the A37's $449.
