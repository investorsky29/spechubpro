Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Xiaomi 17 Ultra launched in China on December 25, 2025, and reached global markets in early 2026 alongside the Xiaomi 17. It is Xiaomi's camera-focused flagship, with a 1-inch 50MP main sensor, a 200MP periscope telephoto with continuous optical zoom, a 6.9-inch screen and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5.

Xiaomi skipped the "16" name, so this phone follows the Xiaomi 15 Ultra. In global markets the 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max are not offered, which makes the Ultra the top model there. Some specifications, including exact global dimensions and weight, were not verified for this page and are left out rather than guessed.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 (3 nm). The model sold in the Philippines has 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage. CPU and GPU details and the storage standard were not verified from sources for this specific model, so they are not listed here. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.9-inch LTPO AMOLED at 1200 x 2608 pixels (about 416 pixels per inch) with a 120Hz refresh rate and 2160Hz PWM dimming. It supports HDR10+, Dolby Vision and HDR Vivid, and a 3,500-nit peak brightness is listed, which is a listed figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

The front glass is Xiaomi Shield Glass 3.0, which Xiaomi claims is 30% more drop resistant than the Xiaomi 15 Ultra's, a manufacturer claim that SpecHub has not tested.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 1-inch 50MP main (Light Fusion 1050L, f/1.7, optical stabilisation), a 200MP periscope telephoto (1/1.4-inch, f/2.4 to f/3.0, optical stabilisation) and a 50MP ultrawide, with Leica co-engineered optics. The periscope offers continuous optical zoom of roughly 3.2x to 4.3x (75 to 100mm equivalent).

The 50MP selfie camera sits in a punch-hole. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The global model lists a 6,000mAh battery with 90W wired and 50W wireless charging. The China version uses a larger 6,800mAh cell.

SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 16 with HyperOS 3, and the Philippines model is listed with 4 years of OS upgrades and 6 years of security patches. Connectivity includes 5G, Wi-Fi 7, NFC and an ultrasonic under-display fingerprint sensor. The water and dust rating is not listed because only one source gave it.

Global versions also add Google services and eSIM support compared with the China version.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who prioritise camera hardware, especially a 1-inch main sensor and a long continuous-zoom periscope, and who want a large 6.9-inch Android flagship will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a compact phone, a lower price or the larger China-model battery may prefer the Xiaomi 17 or another phone. Buyers who need verified dimensions, weight or water-resistance details should check Xiaomi's regional page before buying.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the 17 Ultra is a camera-first flagship with a 1-inch main sensor, a 200MP continuous-zoom periscope, a large 120Hz screen and fast wired and wireless charging. Its premium price and the smaller global battery are the main trade-offs. It suits people who choose a phone for its cameras.
