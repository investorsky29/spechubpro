Expert Review

The Xiaomi 17 Ultra is Xiaomi's 2026 ultra-premium camera flagship, combining a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor with a 6.9-inch HyperRGB OLED display and a Leica camera system. Its cameras include a 50MP 1-inch Light Fusion 1050L main sensor, a 200MP Leica APO telephoto with 75-100mm mechanical optical zoom, and a 50MP Leica ultrawide camera. The phone also features a 6000mAh battery, 90W wired charging, 50W wireless charging, IP68 protection and 4K 120fps Dolby Vision video recording.
