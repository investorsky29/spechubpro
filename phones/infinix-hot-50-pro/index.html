Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Hot 50 Pro is a budget phone released in November 2024 with a 6.78-inch 120Hz AMOLED display and a 5000 mAh battery. It is thicker and heavier than the Hot 50 Pro+ but keeps the headphone jack and NFC. It is aimed at buyers who want an AMOLED screen and practical extras at a low price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Helio G100 (6 nm) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage, expandable by microSD up to 2TB. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.78-inch flat AMOLED display has 1080 x 2436 pixels (about 392 ppi) and a 120Hz refresh rate. GSMArena lists a peak brightness of up to 1,800 nits, higher than the 1,300 nits of the Hot 50 Pro+.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 50MP main (f/1.6, 1/2.8-inch sensor), and the front camera is 8MP, lower than the 13MP selfie camera on the Hot 50 Pro+. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and secondary lenses are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh with 33W wired charging, which Infinix says reaches about 50% in 27 minutes. Reverse wired charging is supported and wireless charging is not. We have no charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and XOS 14.5. It has dual speakers, NFC, a 3.5mm headphone jack and IP54 splash resistance, and supports 4G only.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want an AMOLED screen, a headphone jack and NFC at a low price will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a slimmer, lighter body or a better selfie camera should look at the Hot 50 Pro+. Those who want 5G should look at a higher-tier phone.

Editorial Verdict

The Hot 50 Pro is a practical budget phone with a bright AMOLED screen and useful extras. Its 8MP selfie camera and 4G-only chip are the main compromises.

Frequently Asked Questions

What chipset does the Infinix Hot 50 Pro use?
It uses the MediaTek Helio G100 (6 nm) with 8GB of RAM.
What is the difference between the Hot 50 Pro and the Hot 50 Pro+?
The Hot 50 Pro is 7.4 mm thick and weighs 190 g, with an 8MP selfie camera and up to 1,800 nits brightness. The Hot 50 Pro+ is 6.8 mm thick and weighs 162 g, with a 13MP selfie camera and a curved display.
Does the Hot 50 Pro have a headphone jack?
Yes. It has a 3.5mm jack and NFC.
How fast does it charge?
Wired charging is 33W with a 5000 mAh battery.

Yes. It supports microSD cards up to 2TB.
