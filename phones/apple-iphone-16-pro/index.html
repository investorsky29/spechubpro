Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 16 Pro is the compact Pro model of Apple's 2024 lineup. It has a titanium frame, a slightly larger 6.3-inch screen than the iPhone 15 Pro, a 5x telephoto lens and a Camera Control button. It is aimed at people who want Pro features in a pocketable size.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Apple A18 Pro (3 nm) with a six-core CPU and a six-core GPU. Independent sources report 8GB of RAM. Storage ranges from 128GB to 1TB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.3-inch LTPO OLED display has 1206 x 2622 pixels and an adaptive 1-120Hz refresh rate, with an Always-On mode. Apple rates it for 1,000 nits typical, 1,600 nits peak in HDR and up to 2,000 nits outdoors.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 48MP main, a 48MP ultrawide and a 12MP telephoto with 5x optical zoom, a step up from the 3x lens on the iPhone 15 Pro. The front camera is 12MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and further lens figures are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

Independent sources report a 3582 mAh battery; Apple does not publish this figure. Charging speeds are not listed because they could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 18 and supports Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC and USB-C with USB 3 speeds. It has Face ID, the Action button and Camera Control.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a compact phone with a 120Hz screen, a long telephoto lens and a titanium build will find it a strong fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a larger screen and the biggest battery should consider the iPhone 16 Pro Max. Those who do not need a telephoto lens or 120Hz can save money with the standard iPhone 16.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 16 Pro offers a long telephoto lens, a ProMotion display and a titanium body in a compact size. Its main limits are a modest reported battery and the lack of a charger in the box.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the iPhone 16 Pro and the iPhone 16 Pro Max?
Both use the A18 Pro chip. The 16 Pro has a 6.3-inch display, a 3582 mAh reported battery and weighs 199 g. The 16 Pro Max has a 6.9-inch display, a 4685 mAh reported battery and weighs 227 g.
Does the iPhone 16 Pro have a 120Hz display?
Yes. It uses an adaptive ProMotion display from 1Hz to 120Hz.
Does it have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 12MP telephoto camera with 5x optical zoom.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 16 Pro?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3582 mAh.
How much RAM does the iPhone 16 Pro have?
Apple does not publish RAM. Independent sources report 8GB.
How much did the iPhone 16 Pro cost at launch?
In the US, launch prices were $999 for 128GB, $1,099 for 256GB, $1,299 for 512GB and $1,499 for 1TB. Prices elsewhere and current retail prices differ.
