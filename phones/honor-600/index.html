Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor 600 is the standard model of Honor's 2026 line, announced on April 22, 2026 with the Honor 600 Pro. It has a 6.57-inch AMOLED display, a large battery and a 200MP main camera. It suits buyers who want a long-lasting mid-range phone with fast wired charging.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 (4 nm) with 8GB of RAM and storage that starts at 128GB. The Honor 600 Pro uses the faster Snapdragon 8 Elite. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.57-inch AMOLED display has 1264 x 2728 pixels (about 458 ppi) and a 120Hz refresh rate. Honor rates its peak brightness at up to 8,000 nits, which mainly helps with HDR content and outdoor visibility.

Camera Experience

A 200MP main camera and a 50MP front camera are listed. Other lenses and camera details could not be verified for this page, so they are not listed. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 7000 mAh in international versions and 6400 mAh in Europe, with 80W wired charging. Wireless charging is not supported, which the Honor 600 Pro adds. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10. It supports Bluetooth 6.0, Wi-Fi 6, NFC, an infrared blaster and eSIM, and has an in-screen fingerprint sensor.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a large battery, a bright screen and fast charging at a mid-range price will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want wireless charging, a telephoto camera or a flagship chip should look at the Honor 600 Pro. European buyers should note the smaller 6400 mAh battery.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor 600 is a battery-focused mid-range phone with a bright display and fast charging. The market-dependent battery size and missing wireless charging are the main caveats.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Honor 600 and the Honor 600 Pro?
The Honor 600 uses a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, has 8GB of RAM, no wireless charging and weighs 185 g. The Honor 600 Pro uses a Snapdragon 8 Elite, has 12GB of RAM, 50W wireless charging and a 3.5x telephoto camera, and weighs 200 g.
What is the Honor 600's battery capacity?
It is 7000 mAh in international versions and 6400 mAh in Europe, with 80W wired charging.
Does the Honor 600 support wireless charging?
No. Wireless charging is a Honor 600 Pro feature.
Which chip does it use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4.
Which software does it run?
It runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10.
