Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 6 was Google's standard 2021 flagship, released on October 28, 2021 at $599 in the US. It introduced the first-generation Tensor chip and the camera-bar design. It suits buyers who want a Pixel with a large screen and wireless charging at a lower cost than the Pixel 6 Pro.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the first-generation Google Tensor (5 nm) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage, plus the Titan M2 security chip. There is no memory card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.4-inch AMOLED display has 1080 x 2400 pixels and a 90Hz refresh rate, with Gorilla Glass Victus protection and HDR10+ support. The Pixel 6 Pro has a larger 6.7-inch screen that supports up to 120Hz.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main camera with optical stabilisation and a 12MP ultrawide. The front camera is 8MP. There is no telephoto lens, which the Pixel 6 Pro adds. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4614 mAh with 30W wired charging and 21W Qi wireless charging. Battery Share lets it charge other devices wirelessly. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 12. It has stereo speakers, Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.2, NFC and a USB-C 3.1 port, and it is rated IP68.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want an older Pixel with a large display, wireless charging and good water resistance, usually at a discount, are the main audience.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a telephoto lens or 120Hz should look at the Pixel 6 Pro. Those who want a newer chip and longer support should consider a later Pixel.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 6 is a capable first-generation Tensor phone with a large screen and wireless charging. Its age and lack of a telephoto lens are the main limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel 6 and the Pixel 6 Pro?
The Pixel 6 has a 6.4-inch 1080 x 2400 display at 90Hz, 8GB of RAM and a 4614 mAh battery. The Pixel 6 Pro has a 6.7-inch 1440 x 3120 display up to 120Hz, 12GB of RAM, a 5003 mAh battery and a telephoto camera.
Does the Pixel 6 support wireless charging?
Yes. It supports 21W Qi wireless charging and Battery Share reverse wireless charging.
Is the Pixel 6 water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68.
Which chip does the Pixel 6 use?
It uses the first-generation Google Tensor with the Titan M2 security chip.
How much RAM and storage does it have?
It has 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage.
How much did the Pixel 6 cost at launch?
In the US, the 128GB model launched at $599. Prices elsewhere differ, and current prices vary by retailer.
