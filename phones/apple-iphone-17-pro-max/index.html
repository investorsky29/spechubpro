Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is the largest phone in Apple's 2025 lineup. It shares the A19 Pro chip and three 48MP cameras with the iPhone 17 Pro, and adds a 6.9-inch screen, a larger reported battery and a 2TB storage option. It is aimed at buyers who want the biggest screen and battery.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Apple A19 Pro with 12GB of RAM reported by independent sources. Storage options run from 256GB to 2TB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone and does not list core counts because they were not verified.

Display Experience

The 6.9-inch OLED display has 1320 x 2868 pixels and an adaptive refresh rate of up to 120Hz, with Ceramic Shield 2 protection and the Dynamic Island.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are three 48MP units: main, ultrawide and telephoto. The front camera is an 18MP Center Stage unit. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and zoom ratios are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

Independent sources report a 5088 mAh battery, or 4832 mAh for the physical-SIM model; Apple does not publish capacity. Charging speeds are not listed because they could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 26 and supports Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, NFC and USB-C. It has Face ID, the Action button and Camera Control. The weight is 233 g, or 231 g for the international version.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Heavy video viewers, mobile photographers and anyone who wants the largest iPhone screen and reported battery will benefit most.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who prefer a lighter, smaller phone should choose the iPhone 17 Pro. Those who do not need three cameras may be well served by the standard iPhone 17.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is the capacity leader of the lineup, with the biggest screen, battery and storage options. Its size and weight are the main trade-offs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the iPhone 17 Pro Max and the iPhone 17 Pro?
They share the A19 Pro chip and camera set. The Pro Max has a 6.9-inch display, a 5088 mAh reported battery and weighs 233 g. The Pro has a 6.3-inch display, a 4252 mAh reported battery and weighs 206 g.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 17 Pro Max?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 5088 mAh, or 4832 mAh for the physical-SIM model.
Does it come with 2TB of storage?
Yes. Storage options are 256GB, 512GB, 1TB and 2TB.
How many rear cameras does it have?
Three, all 48MP: main, ultrawide and telephoto.
How much RAM does the iPhone 17 Pro Max have?
Apple does not publish RAM. Independent sources report 12GB.
How much did it cost at launch?
In the US, launch prices were $1,199 for 256GB, $1,399 for 512GB, $1,599 for 1TB and $1,999 for 2TB. Prices elsewhere and current retail prices differ.
