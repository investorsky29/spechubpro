Expert Review

The Apple iPhone Duo is Apple's first foldable iPhone, officially introduced in September 2026. It features a book-style foldable design with a 7.6-inch inner Super Retina XDR OLED display and a 5.4-inch outer display. Both screens support ProMotion up to 120Hz, Always-On display, Dynamic Island, HDR, and up to 3000 nits of outdoor peak brightness. The phone is powered by Apple's A20 Pro chip with a 6-core CPU, 7-core GPU, and dual 16-core Neural Engine. Its camera system includes a 48MP Fusion Main camera with sensor-shift OIS and a 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide camera, while the main camera also provides optical-quality 2x telephoto capability. The iPhone Duo has a titanium design, IP68 protection, Touch ID, dual eSIM support, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, USB-C, and support for Apple Pencil. It starts at $1,999 with 256GB of storage, while availability begins October 23, 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Apple iPhone Duo?
The Apple iPhone Duo starts at $1,999 for the 256GB model. Higher storage tiers up to 2TB are available at additional cost.
What chipset does the iPhone Duo use?
The iPhone Duo is powered by Apple's A20 Pro chip, featuring a 6-core CPU and a 7-core GPU with Neural Accelerators, the same platform found in the iPhone 18 Pro lineup.
Does the iPhone Duo have Face ID?
No, the iPhone Duo does not have Face ID. It uses Touch ID built into the side button instead, supporting multiple fingerprints.
When was the Apple iPhone Duo released?
The Apple iPhone Duo was officially introduced in September 2026, with availability beginning October 23, 2026.
Does the iPhone Duo support the Apple Pencil?
Yes, Apple Pencil support is confirmed for the iPhone Duo, though it arrives later in 2026 through a software update rather than at launch.
Does the iPhone Duo use a physical SIM card?
No, the iPhone Duo uses dual eSIM exclusively and does not support a physical SIM card.
