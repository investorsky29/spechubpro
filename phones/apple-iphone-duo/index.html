Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone Duo is Apple's first foldable iPhone, announced on September 9, 2026. It opens like a book to a 7.6-inch inner display and has a 5.4-inch cover display for use when closed. The phone goes on sale on October 23, 2026, so the details here come from Apple's announcement rather than long-term use. It is aimed at buyers who want a tablet-sized screen in a phone.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Apple A20 Pro with a six-core CPU (two performance and four efficiency cores) and a seven-core GPU. Apple does not publish RAM, and some retailer listings give figures that Apple has not confirmed, so none is listed here. Storage options are 256GB, 512GB, 1TB and 2TB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The inner display is a 7.6-inch foldable OLED at 1878 x 2670 pixels with a 4:3 aspect ratio, which suits reading and split-screen work. The 5.4-inch cover display has 1398 x 2034 pixels. Both support an adaptive refresh rate of 1-120Hz, and Apple rates the display for up to 3,000 nits of peak outdoor brightness. The phone also supports an Apple Pencil (USB-C).

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 48MP main and a 48MP ultrawide, with no telephoto lens listed. The front side has a 12MP camera plus an under-display camera for the inner screen. Apple's launch material does not give further lens figures in the sources we checked, so they are omitted. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Retail listings report a 5800 mAh battery; Apple does not publish capacity. Apple states about 50% in around 20 minutes with a 60W-capable USB-C adapter, which is not included. Wireless charging runs at up to 25W with MagSafe or Qi2. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone ships with iOS 27. It has a side-button fingerprint sensor, an Apple N1 wireless chip, an Apple C2 cellular modem and second-generation Ultra Wideband, plus Emergency SOS and Crash Detection. It is rated IP68, and the titanium frame is paired with Ceramic Shield 2 on the front.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Early adopters who want a large reading and multitasking screen that folds into a phone, and who already use Apple's ecosystem, are the main audience.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a lighter phone (254 g), a thinner folded body (11.3 mm) or a telephoto lens may prefer a Pro iPhone. Those on a tighter budget will find a $1,999 starting price high.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone Duo brings Apple's chip, a 7.6-inch foldable display and 25W wireless charging into a titanium foldable. Its limits are its weight, folded thickness, price and lack of a telephoto camera. Because it has not shipped yet, hands-on results are not available.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the iPhone Duo go on sale?
Apple announced it on September 9, 2026. Pre-orders start on October 16, 2026, and it is available from October 23, 2026.
How much does the iPhone Duo cost?
Launch price: $1,999 USD in the US for the 256GB model. Prices elsewhere differ and may change.
How big are the iPhone Duo's displays?
The inner display is 7.6 inches (1878 x 2670 pixels) and the cover display is 5.4 inches (1398 x 2034 pixels). Both support 1-120Hz.
How fast does the iPhone Duo charge?
Apple states about 50% in around 20 minutes with a 60W-capable USB-C adapter, and wireless charging up to 25W with MagSafe or Qi2. An adapter is not included in the box.
Is the iPhone Duo water resistant?
Yes. Launch listings give it an IP68 rating (up to 6 metres for 30 minutes).
How much RAM and what battery capacity does it have?
Apple does not publish RAM. Retail listings report a 5800 mAh battery, which Apple has not published.
