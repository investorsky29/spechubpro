Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The RedMagic 10S Pro+ is a gaming phone that launched in China on May 26, 2025, alongside the standard RedMagic 10S Pro. It is a minor update of the RedMagic 10 Pro family, with the same 6.85-inch screen and 229 g body, a faster Snapdragon 8 Elite Leading Version chip, and a 7,500mAh battery with 120W charging.

It is a China-market model. The global RedMagic 10S Pro launch in June 2025 covered the non-Plus phone only, and no Pro+ model was announced for global markets. That makes it different from the global 10S Pro (7,050mAh, 80W), the 2024 RedMagic 10 Pro+ (7,050mAh, 120W) and the RedMagic 11 series.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite (SM8750-AC, 3 nm) with two Oryon V2 Phoenix L cores at up to 4.47GHz and six Phoenix M cores at 3.53GHz, plus an Adreno 830 GPU at 1200MHz. RedMagic markets this as the Snapdragon 8 Elite Leading Version, with the top clock raised from 4.32GHz to 4.47GHz compared with the earlier RedMagic 10 Pro. The phone adds RedCore R3 Pro, RedMagic's own gaming chip.

Memory is 16GB or 24GB of RAM (LPDDR5T) with 512GB or 1TB of UFS 4.1 Pro storage, and there is no card slot. Cooling uses a built-in fan, listed at 23,000rpm, with liquid-metal heat transfer. SpecHub has not run benchmarks or thermal tests, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.85-inch AMOLED at 1216 x 2688 pixels (about 431 pixels per inch) with a 144Hz refresh rate and a listed peak brightness of 2,000 nits, which is a listed figure and not a SpecHub measurement. A BOE Q9+ panel with 2592Hz PWM dimming has been reported for the series.

The 16MP selfie camera sits under the display, so there is no cut-out. The usual trade-off applies: under-display cameras can give lower selfie quality than a conventional front camera. The screen protection is listed as Mohs level 4 without naming a glass type.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main (f/1.9, 1/1.55-inch sensor, optical stabilisation), a 50MP ultrawide (f/2.0) and a third lens described as a 2MP macro. No telephoto lens is listed, so zoom depends on cropping.

Video goes up to 8K at 30fps, with 4K at up to 60fps and 1080p at up to 240fps. These are listed capabilities. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so this page does not rate image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is a 7,500mAh silicon-carbon cell with 120W wired charging, which is listed as reaching 100% in 35 minutes. That is a stated figure and not a SpecHub test. The standard 10S Pro uses a 7,050mAh battery with 80W charging.

Wireless and reverse charging are not listed for this model. SpecHub has no battery-life results, so no endurance claim is made.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 15 with RedMagic OS 10.5. The software update period for the China-market Pro+ is not verified. RedMagic's global store states support periods for the global 10S Pro (three years in most regions, five in the EU and UK), which should not be assumed to apply to this model.

Connectivity includes 5G (SA and NSA), tri-band Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4, NFC, an infrared port and a USB Type-C 3.2 Gen 2 port with DisplayPort. It has a 3.5mm headphone jack, stereo speakers, an optical under-display fingerprint sensor and IP54 splash resistance. Individual network bands are not specified, so confirm band support before importing one.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Gamers in China, or buyers prepared to import a China-market phone, who want the Snapdragon 8 Elite Leading Version, active cooling, 120W charging and a headphone jack will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers outside China who want warranty, local network-band support and a clear update policy should look at the global RedMagic 10S Pro. Buyers who want wireless charging, a telephoto camera, a high water-resistance rating or a card slot need a different phone.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the 10S Pro+ is the higher-specification regular version of the RedMagic 10S Pro series, adding a bigger battery, faster charging and more RAM and storage options over the standard 10S Pro. Its main limits are that it is sold in China only, has IP54 protection and lists no wireless charging. It suits gamers who prioritise cooling, battery and charging speed over cameras and global support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the RedMagic 10S Pro+ the same as the RedMagic 10S Pro?
No. They share the 6.85-inch display, 229 g body and Snapdragon 8 Elite Leading Version, but the Pro+ has a 7,500mAh battery with 120W charging and 16GB or 24GB RAM options, while the 10S Pro has a 7,050mAh battery with 80W charging.
Was the RedMagic 10S Pro+ sold globally?
No Pro+ model was announced for the global 10S Pro launch, and the Pro+ is listed as a China model (NX789J). Check with a reseller before importing one.
How is it different from the RedMagic 10 Pro+ and RedMagic 11 Pro?
The 2024 RedMagic 10 Pro+ lists a 7,050mAh battery, 120W charging and an SM8750-AB chip with Android 15 and RedMagic OS 10. The 10S Pro+ has a 7,500mAh battery, the SM8750-AC Leading Version and RedMagic OS 10.5. The RedMagic 11 series is a later generation with its own page.
Which chipset does the RedMagic 10S Pro+ use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite (SM8750-AC, 3 nm) in the Leading Version, with CPU cores listed at up to 4.47GHz, an Adreno 830 GPU and the RedCore R3 Pro gaming chip.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 7,500mAh and wired charging is 120W, listed as 100% in 35 minutes. Wireless charging is not listed.
Does it have a headphone jack?
Yes. It has a 3.5mm jack.
Is the RedMagic 10S Pro+ waterproof?
It is listed as IP54, which means dust protected and splash resistant, not waterproof. Another listing shows IP53 for the related 10S Pro, so do not rely on the rating for water exposure.
How much does the RedMagic 10S Pro+ cost?
The China launch price was ¥7,499 for the 24GB + 1TB version. An estimate of about €730 has also been published without naming a configuration. SpecHub has not verified other prices, and prices vary by seller and region.
