Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Phantom X2 Pro was announced in December 2022 as the flagship of Tecno's Phantom X2 series, and it reached markets such as the Philippines in early 2023 at ₱42,999. Its defining feature is a retractable 50MP portrait telephoto lens that extends from the camera module, paired with a 6.8-inch curved 120Hz AMOLED display and the MediaTek Dimensity 9000 chipset.

It was Tecno's flagship phone of its generation, and the cheaper Phantom X2 uses a conventional 64MP OIS main camera instead of the retractable lens.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 9000 (4 nm), with one 3.05GHz Cortex-X2, three 2.85GHz Cortex-A710 and four 1.8GHz Cortex-A510 cores. It is paired with 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB of UFS 3.1 storage. Sources name the GPU differently, so it is not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.8-inch curved-edge AMOLED at 1080 x 2400 pixels with a 120Hz refresh rate, up from 90Hz on the original Phantom X, and a punch-hole front camera. An in-display fingerprint scanner is included. Brightness and protection glass were not confirmed and are not listed.

Camera Experience

The rear system has three cameras: a 50MP main camera (ISOCELL GNV, 1/1.3-inch, f/1.9), a 50MP retractable portrait telephoto (65mm equivalent, f/1.5, 2.5x optical zoom) and a 13MP ultrawide that also serves as a macro camera. Rear video goes up to 4K.

The retractable lens extends when the camera is opened and collapses when it is closed, which allows a larger sensor than a fixed lens of the same thickness. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,160mAh with 45W wired charging, advertised as reaching 100% in about 60 minutes, and a 45W charger is included in the box. There is no wireless charging. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 12 and HiOS 12 and supports 5G, dual-band Wi-Fi and NFC. Colours are Mars Orange (with an eco-friendly edition using a recycled-plastic back) and Stardust Grey. A software-update period was not confirmed and is not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want an unusual portrait camera and a large curved AMOLED screen, and who accept an older Android generation, will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want current software, faster charging, wireless charging or a stated water-resistance rating should look at a newer phone such as the Camon 50 Ultra 5G.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Phantom X2 Pro was a bold flagship with a retractable portrait lens and a top-tier chipset, held back today by its Android 12 launch software and a modest charging speed. It suits people who value its portrait camera concept and large display.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is special about the Phantom X2 Pro camera?
It has a retractable 50MP portrait telephoto lens (65mm equivalent, f/1.5, 2.5x optical zoom) that extends from the camera module, alongside a 50MP main camera and a 13MP ultrawide.
Which chipset does the Tecno Phantom X2 Pro use?
The MediaTek Dimensity 9000 (4 nm), with 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB of UFS 3.1 storage.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 5,160mAh with 45W wired charging, advertised as 100% in about 60 minutes. There is no wireless charging.
What is the difference between the Phantom X2 and X2 Pro?
The X2 Pro has a retractable portrait telephoto lens, a larger 6.8-inch display and 12GB of RAM. The standard X2 has a conventional 64MP OIS main camera and 8GB of RAM.
Does it support 5G?
Yes. It supports 5G as well as 4G LTE with VoLTE.
What software did it launch with?
Android 12 with HiOS 12.
How much did the Tecno Phantom X2 Pro cost at launch?
In the Philippines the official price was ₱42,999 for 12GB + 256GB. Prices vary by country, retailer and promotions.
What colours does it come in?
Mars Orange (including an eco-friendly edition) and Stardust Grey.
