Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Xiaomi 14 Ultra was announced and released in China on February 22, 2024 and reached global markets at MWC 2024. It is the camera-focused model of the Xiaomi 14 series, with a 6.73-inch LTPO AMOLED display, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset and a four-camera Leica system built around a 1-inch main sensor.

GSMArena lists two versions: a global model with a 5,000mAh battery and a China-only model with 5,300mAh. Network bands differ too, so check the exact version before buying. Xiaomi also offered a Titanium Special Edition in China with two-way satellite communication, according to 9to5Google.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 (SM8650-AB, 4 nm) with an Adreno 750 GPU. Chinese launch coverage lists up to 16GB of RAM and up to 1TB of storage, in 12GB + 256GB, 16GB + 512GB and 16GB + 1TB versions. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.73-inch LTPO AMOLED at 1440 x 3200 pixels (about 522 pixels per inch) with a 120Hz refresh rate, 1920Hz PWM dimming, Dolby Vision and HDR10+. GSMArena lists 1,000 nits typical and 3,000 nits peak brightness, which are listed figures and not SpecHub measurements.

The front uses Xiaomi Shield Glass on the global model, which Xiaomi says is 10 times more drop resistant according to 9to5Google, a manufacturer claim that SpecHub has not tested.

Camera Experience

The rear system has four 50MP cameras with Leica optics: a 1-inch type Sony LYT-900 main sensor with a variable aperture from f/1.63 to f/4.0, an ultrawide, a 3.2x telephoto and a 5x periscope telephoto. The 32MP front camera has an f/2.0 lens.

Other camera details, such as video modes and the exact apertures of the secondary cameras, were not confirmed from reliable sources and are not listed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The global model has a 5,000mAh battery and the China-only model 5,300mAh. Wired charging is 90W. Android Central lists 80W wireless charging for the China launch, while the global wireless figure was not confirmed. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and HyperOS, and GSMArena lists up to 4 major Android upgrades. It has IP68 dust and water resistance and dual Nano-SIM support. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth versions were reported only in pre-launch leaks and are not listed here.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a camera-focused flagship with a 1-inch variable-aperture main sensor and two telephoto lenses will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a newer chipset, the longest software support or the larger China battery may prefer the Xiaomi 15 Ultra, Xiaomi 17 Ultra or the China model. Buyers who want a smaller, lighter phone need a different device.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the 14 Ultra is a camera-first flagship with four 50MP cameras, a bright 2K LTPO screen and 90W charging, with a battery that is smaller on global models. It is best suited to people who value camera hardware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chipset does the Xiaomi 14 Ultra use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 (SM8650-AB, 4 nm) with an Adreno 750 GPU.
How big is the battery?
The global model has a 5,000mAh battery and the China-only model has 5,300mAh, according to GSMArena. Wired charging is 90W.
What cameras does it have?
Four 50MP Leica cameras: a 1-inch type main sensor with variable aperture (f/1.63 to f/4.0), an ultrawide, a 3.2x telephoto and a 5x periscope telephoto. The selfie camera is 32MP.
Is the Xiaomi 14 Ultra waterproof?
It is listed as IP68 dust tight and water resistant, immersible up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes. Water resistance is not permanent.
How many software updates does it get?
It launched with Android 14 and HyperOS, and GSMArena lists up to 4 major Android upgrades.
Is there a Xiaomi 16 Ultra successor?
No. Xiaomi skipped the "16" name. The Xiaomi 14 Ultra was followed by the Xiaomi 15 Ultra and then the Xiaomi 17 Ultra.
How much did the Xiaomi 14 Ultra cost at launch?
In China it started at ¥6,499 for 12GB + 256GB, ¥6,999 for 16GB + 512GB and ¥7,799 for 16GB + 1TB, according to Android Central. Global prices vary by country, retailer and storage size, so check a local seller.
Does it support wireless charging?
Android Central lists 80W wireless charging for the China launch. The global wireless figure was not confirmed from the sources used, so check Xiaomi's regional page.
