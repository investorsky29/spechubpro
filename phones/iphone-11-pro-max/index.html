Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 11 Pro Max was the largest iPhone of 2019, released on September 20, 2019 at $1,099 in the US. It shares its chip, cameras and stainless steel build with the smaller iPhone 11 Pro, but adds a 6.5-inch screen and a bigger battery. It was aimed at buyers who wanted maximum screen size and battery life. It is now an older phone.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A13 Bionic is a 7 nm chip with a six-core CPU and a four-core GPU. Independent sources report 4GB of RAM, which Apple does not publish. Storage is 64GB, 256GB or 512GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.5-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display has 1242 x 2688 pixels (about 458 ppi) and a 60Hz refresh rate. Apple rates it for 800 nits typical and up to 1,200 nits peak in HDR, with Dolby Vision support. The larger panel suits video and reading better than the 5.8-inch iPhone 11 Pro.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 12MP main (f/1.8, optical stabilisation), a 12MP telephoto (f/2.0, 2x optical zoom, optical stabilisation) and a 12MP ultrawide (f/2.4, 120 degrees). The front camera is 12MP. Video goes up to 4K at 60 fps. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3969 mAh, the largest of the iPhone 11 family, against 3046 mAh for the iPhone 11 Pro. Apple states about 50% in 30 minutes with an 18W or higher adapter, and an 18W adapter is included. Wireless charging uses Qi at up to 7.5W. We have no endurance tests, and an older battery may hold less charge today.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 13 and supports Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0, NFC and Ultra Wideband. It has Face ID and an IP68 rating up to 4 metres for 30 minutes. It uses a Lightning port and does not support 5G.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a large OLED screen and a long-lasting battery in an older iPhone, and who can accept the weight, may still find it a good match at a secondhand price.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, USB-C, a 120Hz display or a lighter phone should look at a newer iPhone. Those who prefer a smaller body can consider the iPhone 11 Pro.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 11 Pro Max combined a big OLED screen, three cameras and the largest battery of its generation. Its weight, Lightning port and lack of 5G show its age.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the iPhone 11 Pro Max and the iPhone 11 Pro?
They share the A13 Bionic chip and camera set. The 11 Pro Max has a 6.5-inch display, a reported 3969 mAh battery and weighs 226 g. The 11 Pro has a 5.8-inch display, a reported 3046 mAh battery and weighs 188 g.
Does the iPhone 11 Pro Max support 5G?
No. It supports LTE only.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 11 Pro Max?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3969 mAh.
Is the iPhone 11 Pro Max water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68 for up to 4 metres for 30 minutes.
Does it have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 12MP telephoto camera with 2x optical zoom.
How much did the iPhone 11 Pro Max cost at launch?
In the US, the launch price was $1,099 for 64GB. Prices elsewhere differ, and Apple no longer sells this model as a current product.
