Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Spark 40 Pro+ was announced on July 2, 2025 and released on July 7, 2025 as the top model of the Spark 40 series. It was the first phone to launch with the MediaTek Helio G200 chipset, and it pairs it with a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, a 5,200mAh battery and a body only 6.5 mm thick.

Unlike the Spark 40 Pro and the base Spark 40, it supports 30W wireless charging, which is rare at this price tier. It is a 4G phone with IP64 protection and Gorilla Glass 7i on the front.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G200 (6 nm), with two 2.2GHz Cortex-A76 and six 2.0GHz Cortex-A55 cores and a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU running at 1.1GHz. It comes with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage and has no card slot. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch AMOLED at 1224 x 2720 pixels (about 440 pixels per inch) with a 144Hz refresh rate. Brightness is advertised at 1,600 nits in high-brightness mode and 4,500 nits at peak, which are stated figures and not SpecHub measurements. Some regional listings describe the panel as curved. The fingerprint scanner is built into the display.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 50MP main sensor (f/1.6, 1/2.8-inch, PDAF) with an auxiliary lens, and the front camera is 13MP (f/2.2). Rear video goes up to 1440p at 30fps with gyro stabilisation, and front video to 1080p at 30fps. There is no ultrawide or telephoto camera.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,200mAh with 45W wired charging, 30W wireless charging and 5W reverse wireless charging, plus 10W reverse wired charging. One listing showed 35W wireless charging, but most list 30W, so that figure is used here. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 15 with HiOS 15, and up to 3 major Android upgrades are listed. It has IP64 dust and splash resistance, 1.5 m drop resistance and dual Nano-SIM support. Colours are Nebula Black, Aurora White, Moon Titanium and Tundra Green.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a slim, light budget phone with a bright 144Hz AMOLED screen and wireless charging will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, an ultrawide or telephoto camera, or full water resistance should look at a more expensive phone, such as the Camon 50 series. Buyers who want a cheaper option could look at the Spark 40 Pro.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Spark 40 Pro+ is a very slim and well-specified entry-level phone with an excellent display and wireless charging, limited by 4G and a simple camera set. It suits people who want design, screen and charging features at a low price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Spark 40 Pro and Spark 40 Pro+?
The Pro+ uses a Helio G200 and supports 30W wireless and 5W reverse wireless charging. The Pro uses a Helio G100 Ultimate and has wired charging only. Both have 5,200mAh batteries, 144Hz AMOLED displays and 45W wired charging.
Which chipset does the Tecno Spark 40 Pro+ use?
The MediaTek Helio G200 (6 nm) with a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. It was the first phone with this chipset.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 5,200mAh with 45W wired, 30W wireless and 5W reverse wireless charging.
Is the Spark 40 Pro+ waterproof?
It is rated IP64, which means dust tight and splash resistant. It is not designed to be submerged.
What cameras does it have?
A 50MP main camera with an auxiliary lens on the back and a 13MP front camera.
What RAM and storage options are there?
8GB of RAM with 128GB or 256GB of storage and no card slot.
How much does the Tecno Spark 40 Pro+ cost?
Reported launch pricing is around $214, with an estimate of about €180. In Kenya it is KES 21,999 for 8GB + 128GB. Prices vary by country, retailer and promotions.
How long does it get software updates?
Up to 3 major Android upgrades are listed.
