Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Huawei Mate 70 Pro is a flagship phone announced on November 26, 2024, with sales in China from December 4. It sits below the Mate 70 Pro+ and RS Ultimate Design, and above the standard Mate 70. It is aimed at buyers who want a large display, a variable-aperture camera and very fast charging.

Performance and Processor

The phone has 12GB of RAM and 256GB, 512GB or 1TB of storage. Independent sources report a HiSilicon Kirin 9020 chipset, although Huawei does not publish the chip name in its official specifications. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.9-inch OLED display has 1316 x 2832 pixels and a variable refresh rate of up to 120Hz. The Mate 70 Pro+ and RS use LTPO panels with the same resolution.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main with a variable f/1.4 to f/4 aperture and optical stabilisation, a 48MP periscope telephoto (f/2.1, 93 mm equivalent) and a 40MP ultrawide (f/2.2). The front camera is 13MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5500 mAh with 100W wired and 80W wireless charging, plus reverse wired (5W) and wireless (7.5W) charging. The standard Mate 70 has a 5300 mAh battery with slower 66W wired charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with HarmonyOS 4.3, Huawei's own operating system. It is rated IP68 and takes two Nano-SIM cards. Support for Google services depends on the market.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers in China who want a large screen, fast charging and a flexible camera from a Huawei flagship will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a titanium frame or the largest battery should look at the Mate 70 Pro+ or RS. Buyers outside China should check availability and software support in their region.

Editorial Verdict

The Mate 70 Pro offers very fast charging, a variable-aperture main camera and strong durability. The unpublished chipset name and regional availability are the main caveats.
