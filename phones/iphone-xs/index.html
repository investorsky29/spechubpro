Expert Review

The iPhone XS is Apple's premium flagship from 2018, featuring a compact 5.8-inch Super Retina OLED display and the A12 Bionic processor. Its dual 12MP rear cameras provide optical image stabilization and 2x optical zoom, while Face ID offers secure biometric authentication. The stainless-steel body, IP68 water resistance, wireless charging, and up to 512GB of storage give the XS a premium feature set for its generation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the iPhone XS?
The iPhone XS is priced at around $170 today as a used or refurbished device. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the iPhone XS use?
The iPhone XS is powered by Apple's A12 Bionic chip, a hexa-core processor built on a 7nm process, paired with 4GB of RAM.
What is the difference between the iPhone XS and iPhone X?
The iPhone XS upgrades to the faster A12 Bionic chip, adds IP68 water resistance instead of IP67, and supports dual-SIM through an added eSIM, while keeping the same 5.8-inch display size as the iPhone X.
When was the iPhone XS released?
The iPhone XS was released on September 21, 2018, alongside the iPhone XS Max.
Does the iPhone XS support 5G?
No, the iPhone XS does not support 5G connectivity. Apple did not introduce 5G to the iPhone lineup until the iPhone 12 series in 2020.
Is the iPhone XS still usable today?
The iPhone XS can still run modern apps reasonably well thanks to the A12 Bionic chip, though it lacks 5G, USB-C, and the latest iOS features, making it best suited as a low-cost secondary device today.
