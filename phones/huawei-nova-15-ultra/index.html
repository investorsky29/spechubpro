Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Huawei nova 15 Ultra is the top model of the nova 15 series, announced on December 22, 2025 and released in China on January 6, 2026. It adds wireless charging, three 50MP rear cameras and an IP68/IP69 rating to the nova 15 Pro's formula. It is aimed at buyers who want a stylish phone with flagship-level camera and durability features.

Performance and Processor

Independent sources report a HiSilicon Kirin 9010S chipset, shared with the nova 15 Pro, although Huawei does not publish the chip name officially. RAM and storage options were not verified for this page, so they are not listed. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.84-inch LTPO OLED display has 1320 x 2856 pixels (about 460 ppi) and a 1-120Hz refresh rate, matching the nova 15 Pro.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main with a variable f/1.4 to f/4 aperture and optical stabilisation, a 50MP periscope telephoto (f/2.2, 88 mm equivalent, 3.7x optical zoom) and a 50MP ultrawide. The front camera is 50MP. The nova 15 Pro uses a 12MP telephoto and a 13MP ultrawide instead. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6500 mAh with 100W wired and 50W wireless charging. The nova 15 Pro lacks wireless charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs HarmonyOS 6.0 and takes two Nano-SIM cards. It is rated IP68 and IP69 and weighs 209 g.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers in China who want strong cameras, wireless charging and a high durability rating in a stylish phone will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a lower price should look at the nova 15 or nova 15 Pro. Buyers outside China should check availability and software support.

Editorial Verdict

The nova 15 Ultra fills out the nova 15 series with three 50MP cameras, wireless charging and top-tier protection. Its regional availability is the main caveat.
