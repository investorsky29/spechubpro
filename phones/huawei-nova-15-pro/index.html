Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Huawei nova 15 Pro is the middle model of the nova 15 series, released in China on December 27, 2025 from ¥3,499. It has a 6.84-inch LTPO display, a 6500 mAh battery and a variable-aperture 50MP camera. It is aimed at buyers who want a camera-focused, long-lasting mid-premium phone.

Performance and Processor

Independent sources report a HiSilicon Kirin 9010S chipset, although Huawei does not publish the chip name officially. RAM was not specified at launch, so none is listed. Storage options are 256GB and 512GB. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.84-inch LTPO OLED display has 1320 x 2856 pixels (about 460 ppi) and a 1-120Hz refresh rate, with a peak brightness of up to 4,000 nits.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main with a variable f/1.4 to f/4 aperture and optical stabilisation, a 12MP periscope telephoto with 3x zoom (f/2.4) and a 13MP ultrawide. The front side has dual cameras, including a 50MP ultrawide. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6500 mAh (silicon-anode) with 100W wired charging and reverse wired charging. It does not support wireless charging, which the nova 15 Ultra adds. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs HarmonyOS 6.0 and takes two Nano-SIM cards. It has IP65 dust and water resistance and weighs 202 g.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers in China who want a large screen, a big battery and a strong main camera at a mid-premium price will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want wireless charging and IP68/IP69 protection should look at the nova 15 Ultra. Those on a tighter budget may prefer the nova 15.

Editorial Verdict

The nova 15 Pro offers a bright large display and a very large battery with a flexible camera. The missing wireless charging and unspecified RAM are its main caveats.
