Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 8 is the standard model of Google's 2023 line, released on October 12, 2023 at $699 in the US, $100 more than the Pixel 7. It is smaller than the Pixel 7, with a 6.2-inch screen and a weight of 187 g. It suits buyers who want a compact Pixel with a long update promise.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Google Tensor G3 (4 nm) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage. Google promises seven years of OS and security updates. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.2-inch OLED display has 1080 x 2400 pixels and a refresh rate of 60-120Hz, with 1,400 nits for HDR and up to 2,000 nits peak. That peak brightness is a step up from the 1,400 nits quoted for the Pixel 7. The Pixel 8 Pro has a larger, sharper display with a 1-120Hz range.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main with optical stabilisation and a 12MP ultrawide. The front camera is 10.5MP. There is no telephoto lens. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4575 mAh with 27W wired charging and 18W Qi wireless charging, plus Battery Share reverse wireless charging. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14. It supports Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.3 and a USB-C 3.2 port, and has face unlock and an in-screen fingerprint sensor. It is rated IP68.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a compact Pixel with a bright display and years of updates will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a telephoto lens, a larger screen or a larger battery should look at the Pixel 8 Pro. Those on a budget may prefer the Pixel 8a.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 8 is a compact, bright-screened phone with an unusually long update promise. Its main limits are the absence of a telephoto lens and a smaller battery than the Pro.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel 8 and the Pixel 8 Pro?
The Pixel 8 has a 6.2-inch 1080 x 2400 display, 8GB of RAM, a 4575 mAh battery and weighs 187 g. The Pixel 8 Pro has a 6.7-inch 1344 x 2992 display, 12GB of RAM, a 5050 mAh battery and weighs 213 g, and adds a telephoto camera.
How long does the Pixel 8 get updates?
Google promises seven years of OS and security updates.
How fast does the Pixel 8 charge?
Wired charging is 27W and wireless charging is 18W, with Battery Share reverse wireless charging.
Is the Pixel 8 water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68.
How much RAM does the Pixel 8 have?
It has 8GB of RAM, with 128GB or 256GB of storage.
How much did the Pixel 8 cost at launch?
In the US, the 128GB model launched at $699. Prices elsewhere differ, and current prices vary by retailer.
