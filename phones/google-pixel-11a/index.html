What We Know So Far

The Google Pixel 11a has not been officially announced. Reports and leaks suggest that Google's next affordable Pixel could use the Tensor G6 chipset, retain a 6.3-inch 1080p display, include 8GB of RAM, and receive a new front-facing camera. The phone is expected to launch around 2027, but Google has not confirmed its design, price, final specifications, or release date. All rumored specifications should therefore be treated as unconfirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Has the Google Pixel 11a been announced?
No. Google has not officially announced the Pixel 11a. It is expected sometime around 2027 based on Google's usual a-series release pattern, but nothing is confirmed.
What chipset will the Pixel 11a use?
Leaks suggest the Pixel 11a will use the same Google Tensor G6 chipset found in the Pixel 11 series, though this has not been officially confirmed.
How much will the Pixel 11a cost?
Google has not announced pricing for the Pixel 11a. Previous a-series Pixels have typically launched around $499, but this is not a confirmed figure for the 11a.
What display size is expected on the Pixel 11a?
Rumors point to a 6.3-inch 1080p OLED display running at 120Hz, consistent with recent Pixel a-series phones, though this has not been officially confirmed by Google.
How is the Pixel 11a different from the Pixel 11 Pro?
The Pixel 11 Pro has confirmed official specifications, including a $1,099 price and an August 20, 2026 release date. The Pixel 11a is expected to be a more affordable, less premium model that shares the same Tensor G6 chipset family, though Google has not confirmed details distinguishing the two.
