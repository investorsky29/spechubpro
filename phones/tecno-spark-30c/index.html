Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Spark 30C was announced on September 9, 2024 and released in October 2024. It is an entry-level 4G phone with a 6.67-inch 120Hz IPS LCD, a MediaTek Helio G81 chipset, a 50MP main camera and a 5,000mAh battery with 18W charging.

It is the cheapest model of the Spark 30 family, and an estimated price of about €150 has been published. It keeps several features that are disappearing from more expensive phones, including a microSD slot and a 3.5mm headphone jack.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G81 with two 2.0GHz Cortex-A75 and six 1.8GHz Cortex-A55 cores and a Mali-G52 MC2 GPU. It is offered with 4GB, 6GB or 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage, with a dedicated microSDXC slot for expansion. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.67-inch IPS LCD with 720 x 1600 pixels (about 263 pixels per inch) and a 120Hz refresh rate. Brightness and protection glass were not confirmed and are not listed. The phone has stereo speakers and a side-mounted fingerprint sensor.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 50MP main sensor (1/2.8-inch, PDAF) with an auxiliary lens, and the front camera is 8MP. Video recording is limited to 1080p at 30fps. There is no ultrawide or telephoto camera.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,000mAh with 18W wired charging. This is a stated figure and not a SpecHub test. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 14. It has IP54 dust and splash resistance, a USB Type-C port, an infrared port and no NFC. A software-update period was not confirmed and is not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a very low-cost phone with a big battery, expandable storage, a headphone jack and a smooth 120Hz screen will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a sharper display, faster charging, NFC, 5G or stronger performance should look at a more expensive Spark or another brand.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Spark 30C is a basic budget phone that keeps the practical extras (microSD, headphone jack, IP54) and a 120Hz screen, with an HD+ panel, slow charging and a modest chipset as the trade-offs. It suits first-time buyers and secondary phones.
