Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Galaxy M56 5G is a thin, light mid-range phone from Samsung's M series, released on April 17, 2025. At 7.2 mm and 180 g it is lighter than the Galaxy A36 5G (195 g), while keeping the same 5000 mAh battery and 45W charging. It suits buyers in markets where the M series is sold who want a slim phone with a big battery.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Samsung Exynos 1480 (4 nm) with an octa-core CPU of four Cortex-A78 cores at up to 2.75 GHz and four Cortex-A55 cores at up to 2.0 GHz, plus an Xclipse 530 GPU. RAM is 8GB and storage is 128GB or 256GB. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch Super AMOLED+ display has 1080 x 2340 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate, protected by Gorilla Glass Victus+ at the front. The panel size and resolution match the Galaxy A36 5G.

Camera Experience

A 50MP main camera is listed. Other rear and front cameras, apertures and video modes are omitted because we could not verify them. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh with 45W wired charging, the same figures as the Galaxy A36 5G. Wireless charging is not listed. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 15 with One UI 7, and Samsung lists six years of system updates. It does not have a water-resistance rating listed, unlike the IP67-rated Galaxy A36 5G.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People in markets where it is sold, who want a slim, lightweight phone with a 120Hz screen and a large battery, are the main audience.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want water resistance, a metal frame or the Galaxy A-series update promise of six OS upgrades should look at the Galaxy A36 5G or A56 5G.

Editorial Verdict

The Galaxy M56 5G puts a large battery and 120Hz screen into a thin 180 g body, at the cost of water resistance and a plastic build. It is a practical choice where the M series is available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Galaxy M56 5G sold?
Samsung's M series is sold in selected markets, such as India and Brazil. It is not a global model like the Galaxy A series.
How does the Galaxy M56 5G compare with the Galaxy A36 5G?
Both have a 6.7-inch 120Hz Super AMOLED display, a 5000 mAh battery and 45W charging. The M56 uses an Exynos 1480 and weighs 180 g, while the A36 uses a Snapdragon 6 Gen 3, weighs 195 g and has IP67 water resistance.
What chipset does the Galaxy M56 5G use?
It uses the Samsung Exynos 1480 with an Xclipse 530 GPU.
Is the Galaxy M56 5G water resistant?
No water-resistance rating is listed for this phone.
How fast does it charge?
Wired charging is listed at 45W with a 5000 mAh battery. Wireless charging is not listed.
How much does it cost?
Prices vary by country, retailer and configuration, and a launch price for every market could not be verified. Check local retailers.
