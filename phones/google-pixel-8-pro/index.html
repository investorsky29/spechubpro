Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 8 Pro is Google's 2023 flagship, released on October 12, 2023 at $999 in the US. It combines a 6.7-inch LTPO display, 12GB of RAM and a three-camera system including a 5x telephoto lens. It suits buyers who want the most capable Pixel of that generation.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Google Tensor G3 (4 nm) with 12GB of RAM and storage of 128GB to 1TB. Google promises seven years of OS and security updates. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch LTPO OLED display has 1344 x 2992 pixels and an adaptive 1-120Hz refresh rate, with 1,600 nits for HDR and up to 2,400 nits peak. That is brighter than the 2,000 nits peak of the standard Pixel 8, and an Always-On display is supported.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main with optical stabilisation, a 48MP ultrawide and a 48MP telephoto with 5x optical zoom. The front camera is 10.5MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5050 mAh with 30W wired charging and 23W Qi wireless charging, plus Battery Share reverse wireless charging. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and supports Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC, Ultra Wideband and a USB-C 3.2 port. It has face unlock and an in-screen fingerprint sensor, and is rated IP68.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a long zoom, a bright display and many years of updates in a Pixel will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a smaller phone should consider the Pixel 8. Those who want a newer Tensor chip should consider the Pixel 9 Pro or later.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 8 Pro offers a bright LTPO display, a 5x telephoto camera and long software support. Its size, weight and price are the trade-offs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel 8 Pro and the Pixel 8?
The Pixel 8 Pro has a 6.7-inch 1344 x 2992 display, 12GB of RAM, a 5050 mAh battery and a 5x telephoto camera. The Pixel 8 has a 6.2-inch 1080 x 2400 display, 8GB of RAM, a 4575 mAh battery and no telephoto lens.
How long does the Pixel 8 Pro get updates?
Google promises seven years of OS and security updates.
Does the Pixel 8 Pro have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 48MP telephoto camera with 5x optical zoom.
How fast does it charge?
Wired charging is 30W and wireless charging is 23W, with Battery Share reverse wireless charging.
Is the Pixel 8 Pro water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68.
How much did the Pixel 8 Pro cost at launch?
In the US, the 12GB + 128GB model launched at $999. Prices elsewhere differ, and current prices vary by retailer.
