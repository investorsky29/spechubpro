Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor 400 is the standard model of Honor's 2025 line, released on May 23, 2025 at €499 in Europe. It has a 200MP main camera, a 5300 mAh silicon-carbon battery and a slim 7.3 mm body. It suits buyers who want a long-lasting mid-range phone with a high-resolution camera.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 (4 nm) with 8GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. The Honor 400 Pro uses the faster Snapdragon 8 Gen 3. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.55-inch AMOLED display has 1264 x 2736 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate. It is a flat panel, and its size is slightly smaller than the Honor 200's 6.7 inches.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 200MP main (f/1.9, 1/1.4-inch sensor, optical stabilisation) and a 12MP ultrawide. The front camera is 50MP. There is no telephoto lens, which the Honor 400 Pro adds. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5300 mAh silicon-carbon with 66W wired charging and reverse wired charging. Wireless charging is not supported. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15 and MagicOS 9.0. It is rated IP65 for dust and splash resistance, and has dual Nano-SIM and an in-screen fingerprint sensor.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a large battery, a thin body and a high-resolution main camera at a mid-range price will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a telephoto lens, wireless charging or a flagship chip should look at the Honor 400 Pro.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor 400 pairs a 200MP camera and a big battery with a slim, light body at a mid-range price. It lacks a telephoto lens and wireless charging.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Honor 400 and the Honor 400 Pro?
The Honor 400 has a 6.55-inch display, a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3, 66W wired charging and no wireless charging. The Honor 400 Pro has a 6.7-inch display, a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, 100W wired and 50W wireless charging and a 3x telephoto camera.
How fast does the Honor 400 charge?
Wired charging is 66W with a 5300 mAh battery. Wireless charging is not supported.
Is the Honor 400 water resistant?
It is rated IP65 for dust and splashes.
How big is the Honor 400's main camera?
It has a 200MP main camera with optical stabilisation and a 12MP ultrawide camera.
Which software does the Honor 400 run?
It launched with Android 15 and MagicOS 9.0.
How much did the Honor 400 cost at launch?
In Europe, the 8GB + 256GB model launched at €499 and the 8GB + 512GB model at €549. Prices elsewhere differ.
