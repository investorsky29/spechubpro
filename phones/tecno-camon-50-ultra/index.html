Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Camon 50 Ultra 5G was shown at MWC 2026 on March 3, 2026 and went on sale in March 2026. It is the top model of the Camon 50 series, with a curved 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, the MediaTek Dimensity 7400 Ultimate chipset, a triple rear camera with a 3x telephoto and a 50MP autofocus selfie camera.

It shares its chipset and battery with the Camon 50 Pro 5G, and it differs from the 4G Camon 50 and Camon 50 Pro 4G in having 5G and a Dimensity chipset rather than the Helio G200. Its battery is listed as 6,500mAh by most sources, though one regional listing shows 6,150mAh.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 7400 Ultimate (4 nm), with four 2.6GHz Cortex-A78 and four 2.0GHz Cortex-A55 cores and a Mali-G615 MC2 GPU. It is sold with 8GB or 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. The memory and storage type were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a curved 6.78-inch AMOLED at 1208 x 2644 pixels (about 429 pixels per inch) with a 144Hz refresh rate, 2160Hz PWM dimming and HDR10 support. An in-display fingerprint scanner is built in. Protection glass differs between listings (Gorilla Glass Victus 2 in some and Gorilla Glass 7i in India), so it is not listed.

The 50MP selfie camera has autofocus and supports 4K video.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP Sony LYT-700C main camera (f/1.8, 1/1.56-inch sensor, optical stabilisation), a 50MP telephoto (70mm, f/2.4, 3x optical zoom) and an 8MP ultrawide (f/2.2, 112 degrees). The front camera is a 50MP sensor (f/2.4) with autofocus.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6,500mAh with 45W wired charging. Wireless charging has not been confirmed for this model. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 16 with HiOS 16 and Tecno's AI suite. It is rated IP68 and IP69K and meets MIL-STD-810H testing. A software-update period was not confirmed from more than one source and is not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a 5G mid-premium phone with a curved 144Hz screen, a 3x telephoto, a 50MP selfie camera and a large battery will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want faster charging, wireless charging or a flagship chipset should look at a more expensive phone. Buyers who want something similar for less could look at the Camon 50 Pro 5G, and buyers who do not need 5G could look at the Camon 50 or Camon 50 Pro 4G.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Camon 50 Ultra 5G is a camera-focused mid-premium phone with a good display, a useful telephoto and a big battery, held back by 45W charging and a mid-range chipset. It suits people who value cameras, screen and battery life at a moderate price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chipset does the Tecno Camon 50 Ultra 5G use?
The MediaTek Dimensity 7400 Ultimate (4 nm) with a Mali-G615 MC2 GPU.

A 50MP OIS main camera, a 50MP 3x telephoto and an 8MP ultrawide on the back, plus a 50MP autofocus selfie camera.
