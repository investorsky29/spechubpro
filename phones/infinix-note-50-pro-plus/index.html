Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Note 50 Pro+ is the top model of the Note 50 line, announced on March 20, 2025. It has a Dimensity 8350 Ultimate chip, a periscope telephoto lens and 100W wired charging with 50W wireless charging. It is aimed at buyers who want near-flagship features at a mid-range price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Dimensity 8350 Ultimate (4 nm) with 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB of storage with no card slot. It is a big step up from the Dimensity 7020 in the Note 40 Pro+. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.78-inch AMOLED display has 1080 x 2436 pixels (about 393 ppi) and a 144Hz refresh rate, with a peak brightness of up to 1,300 nits and instant touch sampling of up to 2,160Hz.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP Sony IMX896 main (f/1.9, optical stabilisation), a 50MP periscope telephoto with 3x optical zoom and an 8MP ultrawide (f/2.2). The front camera is 32MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5200 mAh (silicon-carbon) with 100W wired charging, which Infinix says reaches 100% in about 32 minutes, and 50W MagCharge wireless charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15 and XOS 15. It has JBL-tuned stereo speakers, NFC, an infrared blaster and an optical in-screen fingerprint sensor, and an IP64 rating.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a fast chip, a telephoto lens and very fast charging at a price below mainstream flagships will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a larger battery or a stronger ultrawide camera should look at rivals in the same price range. Those who need expandable storage should note that there is no card slot.

Editorial Verdict

The Note 50 Pro+ is a feature-rich mid-range phone with a fast chip, a telephoto lens and class-leading charging speed. Its modest battery capacity and ultrawide camera are the main trade-offs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What chipset does the Infinix Note 50 Pro+ use?
It uses the MediaTek Dimensity 8350 Ultimate (4 nm) with 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM.
How fast does the Note 50 Pro+ charge?
Wired charging is 100W (100% in about 32 minutes) and wireless MagCharge charging is 50W, with a 5200 mAh battery.
Does the Note 50 Pro+ have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 50MP periscope telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom.
What is the difference between the Note 50 Pro+ and the Note 40 Pro+?
The Note 50 Pro+ has a Dimensity 8350 Ultimate, a 5200 mAh battery, 50W wireless charging and a periscope telephoto. The Note 40 Pro+ has a Dimensity 7020, a 4600 mAh battery and 20W wireless charging.
How much did the Note 50 Pro+ cost at launch?
In the Philippines, the launch price was ₱17,999. Prices elsewhere differ.
Is the Note 50 Pro+ water resistant?
It is rated IP64, which means dust and splash resistant.
