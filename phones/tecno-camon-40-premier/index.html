Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Camon 40 Premier 5G was unveiled on March 3, 2025 at MWC 2025 as the top model of the Camon 40 series, and reached the Philippines in April 2025 at ₱21,999. It stands out from the rest of the series with a circular camera module, a triple 50MP camera setup and a faster 70W charger.

It has a 6.67-inch 1.5K AMOLED display, a MediaTek Dimensity 8350 Ultimate chipset, 12GB of RAM and a 5,100mAh silicon-carbon battery.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 8350 Ultimate (4 nm). It comes in a single 12GB + 256GB configuration. CPU and GPU details and the memory type were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.67-inch AMOLED at 1260 x 2800 pixels (about 460 pixels per inch) with a 144Hz refresh rate. One listing shows a lower 120Hz peak, so the refresh rate is not fully settled. An in-display fingerprint scanner is built in, and the 50MP front camera has autofocus.

Brightness and protection glass were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

Camera Experience

The rear system has three 50MP cameras: a main camera with optical stabilisation, an ultrawide, and a lens with 3x optical zoom. This is the only model in the Camon 40 line with a telephoto lens. The selfie camera is also 50MP with autofocus.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,100mAh using silicon-carbon cells, with 70W wired charging. This is faster than the 45W charging on the other Camon 40 models. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 15 with HiOS 15. It also adds a One-Tap multi-function button, Hi-Res audio and stereo speakers. A water-resistance rating and an update period were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want the strongest Camon 40 model, with a telephoto lens, a fast chipset and 70W charging, will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a larger battery or a lower price should look at the Camon 40 Pro 5G, and buyers who want a newer generation should look at the Camon 50 Ultra.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Camon 40 Premier 5G is a capable mid-premium phone with a triple 50MP camera including a 3x zoom, fast charging and a 144Hz display, with a slightly smaller battery than its cheaper siblings. It suits people who want the best camera set in the Camon 40 range.
