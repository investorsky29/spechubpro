Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy S9+ was Samsung's larger flagship model from 2018, featuring a 6.2-inch Super AMOLED display, premium glass-and-metal construction, and a dual-camera system with a dedicated telephoto lens. It is powered by the Snapdragon 845 or Exynos 9810 depending on the market. Features such as IP68 protection, wireless charging, expandable storage, stereo speakers, and a 3.5mm headphone jack give the S9+ a strong feature set, although its older hardware and 3500mAh battery limit it by modern standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy S9+?
The Samsung Galaxy S9+ is priced at around $60 to $100 today as a used or refurbished device. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the Samsung Galaxy S9+ use?
The Samsung Galaxy S9+ uses either a Qualcomm Snapdragon 845 or a Samsung Exynos 9810 chipset depending on the region, the same platform as the standard Galaxy S9.
What is different between the Galaxy S9 and Galaxy S9+?
The Galaxy S9+ has a larger 6.2-inch display, a bigger 3500mAh battery, and an additional telephoto camera lens compared to the standard Galaxy S9's single rear camera and 5.8-inch screen.
When was the Samsung Galaxy S9+ released?
The Samsung Galaxy S9+ was released on March 16, 2018, alongside the standard Galaxy S9.
Does the Galaxy S9+ support 5G?
No, the Samsung Galaxy S9+ does not support 5G connectivity, as 5G was not introduced to Samsung's Galaxy S lineup until 2019.
Is the Samsung Galaxy S9+ worth buying today?
As an older flagship, the Galaxy S9+ lacks 5G and modern performance, but its dual-camera system, premium build, and larger battery than the standard S9 still make it a capable low-cost option for buyers who want a bigger classic Galaxy phone.
