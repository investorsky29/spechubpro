Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The vivo X300 was announced in China on October 13, 2025 and released on October 17, 2025, with a global debut for selected markets on October 30, 2025 and an India launch on December 2, 2025. It is the compact model of vivo's X300 series, with a 6.31-inch LTPO AMOLED display, the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 chipset and a 200MP ZEISS main camera.

It launched alongside the larger vivo X300 Pro. The key camera difference is that the 200MP sensor is the X300's main camera, while on the X300 Pro it is a telephoto. Hardware varies by market: the Italian version has a 5,360mAh battery, while the UAE and India versions have 6,040mAh.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 (3 nm). It is available with up to 16GB of LPDDR5X RAM and up to 1TB of UFS 4.1 storage. CPU core layout and GPU details were not confirmed for this specific model on this page. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

Launch coverage describes a 6.31-inch 1.5K LTPO AMOLED with a 120Hz refresh rate and an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor. Exact resolution, brightness and protection figures were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed here.

The 50MP selfie camera is the same sensor as the ultrawide.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 200MP ZEISS main camera, a 50MP ZEISS APO telephoto with 3x optical zoom (LYT-602, 1/1.95-inch) and a 50MP ultrawide. Other aperture and video details were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The global X300 has a 6,040mAh battery, while the Italian version has 5,360mAh. Charging is 90W wired and 40W wireless. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

It has an IP68 and IP69 rating, an ultrasonic under-display fingerprint sensor and is 7.95 mm thick. Software version, update period, weight and connectivity versions were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a compact flagship with a high-resolution main camera, a 3x telephoto, wireless charging and strong water resistance will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a long-range telephoto should look at the vivo X300 Pro. Buyers in Italy and other EU markets should check the exact battery size.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the X300 is a compact camera flagship with a 200MP main camera, a 3x telephoto, a large battery for its size and fast charging, with regional battery differences as its main caveat. It suits people who want a smaller phone without giving up camera hardware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chipset does the vivo X300 use?
The MediaTek Dimensity 9500 (3 nm).
How big is the battery?
Global versions have 6,040mAh, while the Italian version has 5,360mAh. Charging is 90W wired and 40W wireless.
What is the difference between the X300 and X300 Pro?
The X300 has a 6.31-inch display, a 200MP main camera and a 50MP telephoto. The X300 Pro has a 6.78-inch display, a 50MP main camera and a 200MP periscope telephoto, with a larger battery.
What cameras does the X300 have?
A 200MP ZEISS main camera, a 50MP ZEISS APO telephoto with 3x optical zoom, a 50MP ultrawide and a 50MP selfie camera.
Is the vivo X300 waterproof?
It is rated IP68 and IP69. Water resistance is not permanent, so avoid unnecessary exposure.
How much did the vivo X300 cost at launch?
In China it started from ¥4,399 for 12GB + 256GB. A verified global price was not confirmed, and prices vary by country, retailer and storage size.
When was it released?
It was announced in China on October 13, 2025, released there on October 17, debuted in selected global markets on October 30, 2025 and launched in India on December 2, 2025.
Does it have wireless charging?
Yes. Launch coverage lists 40W wireless charging alongside 90W wired charging.
