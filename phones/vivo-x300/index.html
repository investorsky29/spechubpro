Expert Review

The vivo X300 is a compact flagship smartphone built around MediaTek's Dimensity 9500 processor and a 6.31-inch LTPO AMOLED display with a 120Hz adaptive refresh rate. Its camera system includes a 200MP ZEISS main camera, 50MP ZEISS APO telephoto camera and 50MP ultrawide camera. The phone also features a 6040mAh battery, 90W wired charging, 40W wireless charging, IP68/IP69 protection and fast UFS 4.1 storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the vivo X300?
The vivo X300 is priced at $649, depending on the RAM and storage configuration chosen.
What chipset does the vivo X300 use?
The vivo X300 is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 chipset, built on a 3nm process, paired with an Arm G1-Ultra GPU.
Does the vivo X300 support microSD storage expansion?
No, the vivo X300 does not have a microSD card slot. Storage is fixed at 256GB, 512GB, or 1TB depending on the model chosen.
What cameras does the vivo X300 have?
The vivo X300 has a 200MP ZEISS main camera with OIS, a 50MP ZEISS APO telephoto camera offering 3x optical zoom, and a 50MP ultrawide camera.
Does the vivo X300 have wireless charging?
Yes, the vivo X300 supports 40W FlashCharge wireless charging in addition to 90W wired charging.
Is the vivo X300 water resistant?
Yes, the vivo X300 carries both IP68 and IP69 ratings, protecting against dust, submersion, and high-pressure water jets.
