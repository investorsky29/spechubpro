Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Spark 40 Pro was announced on July 2, 2025 as the middle model of the Spark 40 series. It has a flat 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, a MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate chipset, a 50MP main camera, a 13MP selfie camera and a 5,200mAh battery with 45W charging.

It shares its display, battery and cameras with the Spark 40 Pro+ but uses a less powerful chipset and has no wireless charging. It is a 4G phone and carries an IP64 rating.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate (6 nm), with two 2.2GHz Cortex-A76 and six 2.0GHz Cortex-A55 cores and a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. It comes with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a flat 6.78-inch AMOLED at 1224 x 2720 pixels with a 144Hz refresh rate and Gorilla Glass protection. An in-display fingerprint scanner is included. Brightness figures vary between listings and are not given here.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 50MP wide main sensor with a dual LED flash and a dual-camera layout, and the front camera is 13MP. Rear video goes up to 1440p at 30fps and front video to 1080p at 30fps. The second rear camera was not confirmed and is not listed.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,200mAh with 45W wired charging. No wireless charging is available on the Spark 40 Pro. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 15 with HiOS 15 and has IP64 dust and splash resistance. It supports dual Nano-SIM but not 5G. The software-update period was not confirmed from more than one source for this model and is not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a bright 144Hz AMOLED screen, a decent main camera and a big battery at a low price, and who do not need wireless charging, will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want wireless charging and a faster chipset should look at the Spark 40 Pro+. Buyers who want a cheaper phone could look at the base Spark 40, and buyers who want 5G should look at a different Tecno series.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Spark 40 Pro is a strong budget phone for its display and battery, with 4G and a modest chipset as the main trade-offs. It suits people who prioritise screen quality and battery life.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Spark 40 and Spark 40 Pro?
The Spark 40 Pro has a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, a Helio G100 Ultimate, a 13MP selfie camera and IP64. The base Spark 40 has a 6.67-inch HD+ 120Hz IPS display, a Helio G81 and an 8MP selfie camera.
Which chipset does the Tecno Spark 40 Pro use?
The MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate (6 nm) with a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 5,200mAh with 45W wired charging. There is no wireless charging.
Is the Spark 40 Pro waterproof?
It is rated IP64, which means dust tight and splash resistant. It is not designed to be submerged.
What cameras does it have?
A 50MP main camera on the back and a 13MP front camera.
What RAM and storage options are there?
8GB of RAM with 128GB or 256GB of storage.
What colours does it come in?
Ink Black, Moon Titanium, Lake Blue and Bamboo Green.
How much does the Tecno Spark 40 Pro cost?
Reported launch pricing is around $189. In Nigeria it is ₦221,000 for 8GB + 128GB and in Ghana GHS 1,930. Prices vary by country, retailer and promotions.
