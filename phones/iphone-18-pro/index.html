What We Know So Far

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is expected to be Apple's next-generation Pro flagship for 2026. Current reports point to a 6.3-inch ProMotion LTPO OLED display, Apple's A20 Pro chip built on a 2nm process, a smaller Dynamic Island, and a new 48MP main camera with variable aperture. A next-generation C2 modem and improved battery life have also been reported. Apple has not officially announced the phone yet, so its final specifications, price, colors, and release date remain unconfirmed.
