Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 10 Pro is the compact Pro model of Google's 2025 line, announced on August 20, 2025 and released on August 28 at $999 in the US. It is the first Pixel with a Tensor chip made by TSMC rather than Samsung. It suits buyers who want a flagship Pixel in a small body.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Google Tensor G5 (3 nm) with the Titan M2 security chip, 16GB of RAM and storage from 128GB to 1TB. Google promises seven years of OS and security updates. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone, and reviewers have noted that the chip is not aimed at topping raw performance charts.

Display Experience

The 6.3-inch LTPO OLED display has 1280 x 2856 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate, with HDR10+ and Gorilla Glass Victus 2 protection. The size and resolution match the Pixel 9 Pro.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main (f/1.7, optical stabilisation), a 48MP telephoto (f/2.8, 5x optical zoom, optical stabilisation) and a 48MP ultrawide (f/1.7, 123 degrees). The front camera is 42MP. Video goes up to 8K at 30 fps. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4870 mAh with fast wired charging and Qi wireless charging. Wattage figures could not be verified, so they are not listed. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone ships with Android 16 and is rated IP68. It supports a Nano-SIM and an eSIM.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a compact flagship with a long zoom lens, a high-resolution selfie camera and years of updates will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a larger screen and battery should look at the Pixel 10 Pro XL. Those who want a foldable should consider the Pixel 10 Pro Fold.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 10 Pro offers a complete camera set and long software support in a compact body. Its modest battery is the main limit.
