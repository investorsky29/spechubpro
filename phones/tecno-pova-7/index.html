Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Pova 7 is a 4G budget phone built around a 7,000mAh battery. It uses the MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate chipset with 8GB of RAM, a 6.78-inch FHD+ 120Hz display and 45W charging, and it can charge other devices at 10W through reverse wired charging.

It is not the same phone as the Pova 7 5G or Pova 7 Pro 5G, which launched on July 4, 2025 with a Dimensity 7300 Ultimate chipset and 6,000mAh batteries. In the Philippines the Pova 7 has an SRP of ₱6,999 for 8GB + 128GB.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate, a 4G-only 6 nm chip at about 2.2GHz. It comes with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage. CPU and GPU details and the memory type were not confirmed and are not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is 6.78 inches at Full HD+ resolution with a 120Hz refresh rate and 1,000Hz instant touch sampling. The panel type, brightness and protection glass were not confirmed and are not listed. Stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos, Hi-Res Audio and Hi-Res Wireless support are included.

Camera Experience

The front camera is 8MP. Rear camera specifications were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 7,000mAh, which is larger than most phones at any price. Charging is 45W and is advertised as reaching 50% in 29 minutes and 100% in about 70 minutes, and a 45W adapter is included. Reverse wired charging at 10W lets it act as a power bank. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The operating system version, update period, water-resistance rating and connectivity versions were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. Check the manufacturer's regional page for those details before buying.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want the largest battery at the lowest price, and who do not need 5G or strong cameras, will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, a better selfie camera or a faster chipset should look at the Pova 7 5G or Pova 7 Pro 5G. Buyers who want a more established feature set could look at the Pova 6 Pro 5G.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Pova 7 is a battery-first budget phone with a 7,000mAh cell, 45W charging and a smooth 120Hz display, with 4G and a basic front camera as its trade-offs. It suits people who want long battery life and a very low price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Tecno Pova 7 a 5G phone?
No. The Pova 7 covered here is a 4G phone with a Helio G100 Ultimate chipset. The Pova 7 5G and Pova 7 Pro 5G have a Dimensity 7300 Ultimate chipset and 5G.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 7,000mAh with 45W wired charging, advertised as 50% in 29 minutes and 100% in about 70 minutes. It also supports 10W reverse wired charging.
Which chipset does the Tecno Pova 7 use?
The MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate, a 4G-only chip.
What is the display like?
A 6.78-inch FHD+ display with a 120Hz refresh rate and 1,000Hz instant touch sampling.
What RAM and storage options are there?
8GB of RAM with 128GB or 256GB of storage.
How much does the Tecno Pova 7 cost?
In the Philippines the SRP is ₱6,999 for 8GB + 128GB and ₱7,999 for 8GB + 256GB. Prices vary by country, retailer and promotions.
What is the front camera?
8MP.
Does it have stereo speakers?
Yes, stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos, Hi-Res Audio and Hi-Res Wireless support.
