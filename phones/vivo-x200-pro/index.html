Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The vivo X200 Pro was announced in China on October 14, 2024 and released there on October 19, then launched globally on November 19, 2024, starting in Malaysia. It is vivo's camera-focused flagship of that generation, with a MediaTek Dimensity 9400 chipset, a 6.78-inch LTPO AMOLED display and a 200MP ZEISS APO periscope telephoto.

The smaller X200 Pro mini stayed in China. The global model has a 6,000mAh battery and models sold in Austria, Hungary and Germany have 5,200mAh, so check the version before buying. It was followed by the vivo X300 Pro.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 9400 (3 nm) with an Immortalis-G925 GPU. It is available with 12GB or 16GB of RAM and 256GB, 512GB or 1TB of storage, with no card slot. The global launch version has 16GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 512GB of UFS 4.0 storage.

SpecHub has not run benchmarks or thermal tests, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch 1.5K LTPO AMOLED (1260 x 2800 pixels) with a 120Hz refresh rate. A peak brightness of up to 4,500 nits is listed for the global X200 Pro, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

Protection and other panel details were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

Camera Experience

The telephoto is the headline: a 200MP Samsung ISOCELL HP9 sensor (1/1.4-inch, f/2.7, 85mm equivalent) with about 3.7x optical zoom, optical stabilisation and a macro mode with a 2.7:1 ratio, co-engineered with ZEISS.

The phone has a triple rear camera, but the main and ultrawide specifications were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The global X200 Pro has a 6,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, while models sold in Austria, Hungary and Germany have 5,200mAh. Charging is 90W wired and 30W wireless. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15, and up to 4 major Android upgrades are listed. Connectivity versions, water-resistance rating and other features were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. Bands differ between International and China versions.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a long-range 200MP telephoto camera, flagship performance and fast charging in a camera-focused phone will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a newer chipset and software promise should look at the vivo X300 Pro. Buyers in Germany, Austria and Hungary should check the battery size, since 5,200mAh versions exist.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the X200 Pro is a camera-first flagship built around a 200MP periscope telephoto, with a large global battery and fast charging, and a smaller battery in some European markets. It suits people who prioritise zoom photography.
