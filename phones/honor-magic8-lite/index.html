Expert Review

The HONOR Magic8 Lite is a mid-range smartphone focused on battery life, durability and display quality. It features a 6.79-inch 120Hz AMOLED display with up to 6000 nits peak brightness, Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 performance and a large 7500mAh silicon-carbon battery with 66W wired charging. Its rear camera setup combines a 108MP OIS main camera with a 5MP ultrawide camera, while the 16MP front camera handles selfies and video calls. The phone also offers strong water and dust resistance with IP66, IP68, IP69 and IP69K ratings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the HONOR Magic8 Lite?
The HONOR Magic8 Lite is priced at $438, depending on the storage configuration chosen.
How big is the HONOR Magic8 Lite's battery?
The HONOR Magic8 Lite has a large 7500mAh silicon-carbon battery, supporting 66W wired fast charging.
What chipset does the HONOR Magic8 Lite use?
The HONOR Magic8 Lite is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 chipset built on a 4nm process, paired with an Adreno 810 GPU.
Is the HONOR Magic8 Lite water resistant?
Yes, the HONOR Magic8 Lite carries IP66, IP68, IP69 and IP69K ratings, offering strong protection against dust, submersion and high-pressure water jets.
Does the HONOR Magic8 Lite have a telephoto camera?
No, the HONOR Magic8 Lite does not have a dedicated telephoto camera. Its rear setup consists of a 108MP OIS main camera and a 5MP ultrawide camera.
What Android version does the HONOR Magic8 Lite ship with?
The HONOR Magic8 Lite ships with Android 15 and HONOR's MagicOS 9.0 out of the box.
