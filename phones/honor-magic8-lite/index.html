Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor Magic8 Lite is the affordable model of the Magic8 line, announced on December 8, 2025 and sold in Europe from January 2026. It follows the Magic7 Lite with a bigger battery and stronger water and dust ratings. It is aimed at buyers who want a long-lasting, tough mid-range phone.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 (4 nm) with 8GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage and no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.79-inch AMOLED display has 1200 x 2640 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate, with 3840Hz PWM dimming. An optical fingerprint sensor sits under the screen.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 108MP main (f/1.8, 1/1.67-inch sensor, optical stabilisation) and a 5MP ultrawide. The front camera is 16MP (f/2.5). SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 7500 mAh in Europe and 8300 mAh in Latin America, with 66W wired charging. Wireless charging is not supported. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15 and MagicOS 9, even though Android 16 was already available. It has stereo speakers, dual Nano-SIM and IP66, IP68 and IP69K ratings, with Honor claiming drop resistance from up to 2.5 metres.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a very large battery and strong water and dust protection at a mid-range price will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a faster chip, a telephoto lens or wireless charging should look at a higher-end Honor such as the Honor 400 Pro or the Magic8 Pro.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor Magic8 Lite is a durable, battery-focused mid-range phone. Its plastic body and older Android version are the main compromises.
