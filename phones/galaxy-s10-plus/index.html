Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Galaxy S10+ was Samsung's larger flagship of 2019, released on March 8, 2019. It combined a 6.4-inch Dynamic AMOLED display, a triple rear camera, a microSD slot and a 3.5mm headphone jack, all features that have since become rare on flagship phones. It is aimed today at buyers looking for an older phone with these traditional features.

Performance and Processor

The chipset depends on the region: the Exynos 9820 (8 nm) in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and the Snapdragon 855 (7 nm) in the US, Latin America and China. RAM is 6GB, 8GB or 12GB (12GB with the 1TB model) and storage is 128GB, 512GB or 1TB, expandable with a microSD card. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone, and as a 2019 chip it is much slower than current ones.

Display Experience

The 6.4-inch Dynamic AMOLED display has 1440 x 3040 pixels (about 522 ppi) with HDR10+ and an always-on mode. The refresh rate is 60Hz, so it lacks the smoother 120Hz found on newer phones. The front glass is Gorilla Glass 6.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 12MP main, a 12MP telephoto and a 16MP ultrawide. The front camera is a 10MP unit with an 8MP depth camera. Apertures and video modes are omitted because we could not verify them for this page. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4100 mAh. Wired charging is 15W, wireless charging is 15W and reverse wireless charging is 4.5W. We have no endurance tests, and an older battery may hold less charge after years of use.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 9 and can be upgraded to Android 12 with One UI 4.1, which was the last major update for the phone. Samsung has stopped regular software updates, so it no longer receives the latest security protections. It supports Bluetooth 5.0 and NFC, and has IP68 water resistance and Samsung DeX.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who specifically want a microSD slot, a headphone jack and a high-resolution AMOLED display in a secondhand phone may find it appealing.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Anyone who needs 5G, current security updates or faster charging should choose a newer phone, such as the Galaxy A56 5G or a recent S-series model. People who use banking and other sensitive apps should be aware that the phone is no longer updated.

Editorial Verdict

The Galaxy S10+ remains notable for its feature set but is held back by its age: 4G only, 15W charging and no further updates. It suits a niche audience that values its traditional features.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Galaxy S10+ support 5G?
No. The Galaxy S10+ is a 4G phone. Samsung made a separate Galaxy S10 5G model.
Does the Galaxy S10+ have a microSD slot and headphone jack?
Yes. It has a microSD slot, which is shared with the second SIM, and a 3.5mm headphone jack.
How fast does the Galaxy S10+ charge?
Wired charging is 15W and wireless charging is 15W. Reverse wireless charging is 4.5W.
Which chipset does the Galaxy S10+ use?
It depends on the market: the Exynos 9820 in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and the Snapdragon 855 in the US, Latin America and China.
Is the Galaxy S10+ still getting updates?
No. Its last major update was Android 12 with One UI 4.1, and regular updates have ended.
How much did the Galaxy S10+ cost at launch?
In the US, the 128GB model launched at about $999. Prices elsewhere differed, and it is no longer sold new by Samsung.
