Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy S20+ is the larger standard model in Samsung's 2020 Galaxy S20 lineup. It combines a 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with a 120Hz refresh rate, Snapdragon 865 or Exynos 990 performance, and 5G connectivity. Its camera system includes a 12MP main camera, 64MP telephoto camera with 3x hybrid zoom, and 12MP ultrawide camera, while the 4500mAh battery supports 25W wired charging and 15W wireless charging. Expandable storage, IP68 protection, and stereo speakers add to its appeal as an older flagship.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy S20+?
The Samsung Galaxy S20+ is priced at around $260 today as a used or refurbished device. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the Samsung Galaxy S20+ use?
The Samsung Galaxy S20+ uses either a Qualcomm Snapdragon 865 or a Samsung Exynos 990 chipset depending on the region, the same platform as the standard Galaxy S20.
What is different between the Galaxy S20 and Galaxy S20+?
The Galaxy S20+ has a larger 6.7-inch display compared to the standard S20's 6.2-inch screen, a bigger 4500mAh battery, and adds a ToF 3D depth camera to its rear camera array.
When was the Samsung Galaxy S20+ released?
The Samsung Galaxy S20+ was released on March 6, 2020, alongside the standard Galaxy S20 and Galaxy S20 Ultra.
Does the Galaxy S20+ support 5G?
Yes, the Samsung Galaxy S20+ supports 5G connectivity, one of the key upgrades Samsung introduced across the entire Galaxy S20 lineup.
Is the Samsung Galaxy S20+ still a good phone today?
The Galaxy S20+ still offers a sharp 120Hz display, a versatile telephoto camera, and a large 4500mAh battery, making it a solid budget option, though its charging speed and processor trail current flagships.
