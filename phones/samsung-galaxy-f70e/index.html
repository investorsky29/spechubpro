Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G is the first smartphone in Samsung's Galaxy F70 series, launched in February 2026. It features a 6.7-inch PLS LCD display with a smooth 120Hz refresh rate, a MediaTek Dimensity 6300 processor, and a large 6000mAh battery with 25W fast charging. The rear camera system consists of a 50MP main camera and 2MP depth camera, while an 8MP front camera handles selfies. The phone also supports 5G, microSD expansion up to 2TB, IP54 protection, and Samsung's One UI 8 based on Android 16. Samsung promises security updates through January 2032.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G?
The Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G is priced between ₹12,499 and ₹15,850 in India, depending on the RAM and storage configuration.
What chipset does the Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G use?
The Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G is powered by a MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset built on a 6nm process, paired with a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU.
Does the Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G have NFC?
No, the Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G does not include NFC, a common cost-cutting omission on budget phones in this price range.
When was the Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G released?
The Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G was released on February 9, 2026, as the first model in Samsung's Galaxy F70 series.
Does the Galaxy F70e support microSD storage expansion?
Yes, the Samsung Galaxy F70e 5G supports microSDXC cards up to 2TB, giving it more storage flexibility than many phones in its price range.
How long will the Galaxy F70e receive software updates?
Samsung has committed to security updates for the Galaxy F70e through January 2032, a notably long support window for a budget device.
