Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Camon 50 Pro 4G is a 4G mid-range phone announced on February 17, 2026 in several African markets and France, shown at MWC 2026 on March 3 and on sale from March 2026. It adds a curved display and a 3x telephoto camera to the base Camon 50, and it uses the MediaTek Helio G200 Ultimate chipset.

The 4G label distinguishes it from the Camon 50 Pro 5G, which uses a Dimensity 7400 Ultimate chipset, and from the Camon 50 Ultra 5G.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G200 Ultimate (6 nm), with two 2.2GHz Cortex-A76 and six 2.0GHz Cortex-A55 cores and a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU running at 1.1GHz. It comes with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a curved 6.78-inch AMOLED at 1208 x 2644 pixels (about 429 pixels per inch) with a 144Hz refresh rate and HDR support. Brightness and protection glass were not confirmed and are not listed. Stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos support complete the media setup.

Camera Experience

The rear system has three cameras: a 50MP main (f/1.8, 1/1.56-inch sensor, optical stabilisation), a 50MP telephoto (70mm, f/2.4, 3x optical zoom, optical stabilisation) and an 8MP ultrawide (f/2.2, 112 degrees). The selfie camera is 32MP (f/2.5). Video is listed at up to 1080p and 30fps on both the rear and front cameras.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6,150mAh in most listed markets and 6,500mAh in Pakistan, with 45W wired charging. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 16 with HiOS 16 and lists up to 3 major Android upgrades. It is rated IP68 and IP69K and meets MIL-STD-810 testing, supports dual Nano-SIM and includes a One-Tap AI key.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a large battery, a curved 144Hz AMOLED screen and a 3x telephoto camera in a durable mid-range 4G phone will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, 4K video or a more powerful chipset should look at the Camon 50 Pro 5G or Camon 50 Ultra 5G. Buyers who want a lower price and can do without the telephoto and curved screen could consider the base Camon 50.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, this is a well-equipped 4G mid-ranger with a telephoto camera, a curved 144Hz screen and a large battery, held back by a mid-range chipset and 1080p video. It suits people who prioritise battery, screen and zoom flexibility over speed.
