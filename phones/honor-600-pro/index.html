Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor 600 Pro is the higher-end model of Honor's 2026 pair, announced on April 22, 2026. It pairs the Snapdragon 8 Elite chip with a 200MP main camera, a telephoto lens and wireless charging. It suits buyers who want a camera and performance phone at a price below the major flagships.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite (3 nm), with two cores at up to 4.32 GHz and six at up to 3.53 GHz, an Adreno 830 GPU, 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. The standard Honor 600 uses a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.57-inch AMOLED display has 1264 x 2728 pixels (about 458 ppi) and a 120Hz refresh rate. Honor rates the peak brightness at up to 8,000 nits.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 200MP main (f/1.9, optical stabilisation), a 50MP telephoto with 3.5x optical zoom and a 12MP ultrawide. The front camera is 50MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 7000 mAh in international versions and 6400 mAh in Europe. Wired charging is 80W and wireless charging is 50W, with 27W wired reverse charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10. It supports Bluetooth 6.0, Wi-Fi 6, NFC, an infrared blaster and eSIM, and has an in-screen fingerprint sensor and face unlock. Sources disagree on its dust and water rating, so none is listed here.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want flagship-class performance, a telephoto camera and fast wired and wireless charging at a lower price than the top Galaxy and Pixel models will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a confirmed water-resistance rating or a lower price should look at the Honor 600 or the Honor 400 Pro. European buyers should note the smaller 6400 mAh battery.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor 600 Pro combines a Snapdragon 8 Elite, a 200MP camera and fast charging at a mid-premium price. The market-dependent battery and unclear durability rating are the main caveats.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Honor 600 Pro and the Honor 600?
The Honor 600 Pro uses a Snapdragon 8 Elite, has 12GB of RAM, 50W wireless charging and a 3.5x telephoto camera, and weighs 200 g. The Honor 600 uses a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, has 8GB of RAM, no wireless charging and weighs 185 g.
What is the Honor 600 Pro's battery capacity?
It is 7000 mAh in international versions and 6400 mAh in Europe, with 80W wired and 50W wireless charging.
Does the Honor 600 Pro have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 50MP telephoto camera with 3.5x optical zoom.
Which chip does the Honor 600 Pro use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite with an Adreno 830 GPU.
Which software does it run?
It runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10.
How much does the Honor 600 Pro cost?
In Europe, the 12GB + 256GB model is listed at €800. Prices elsewhere differ.
