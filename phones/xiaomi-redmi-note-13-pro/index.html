Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G was announced in China on September 21, 2023 and reached global markets in January 2024. It has a 6.67-inch 120Hz AMOLED screen, a Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset and a 200MP main camera.

The Redmi Note 13 range includes three Pro models, so naming is easy to mix up. This page covers the 5G Redmi Note 13 Pro. The Redmi Note 13 Pro 4G uses the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultra, and the Redmi Note 13 Pro+ uses the Dimensity 7200 Ultra with 120W charging and IP68.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 (SM7435-AB, 4 nm), with four 2.4GHz Cortex-A78 and four 1.95GHz Cortex-A55 cores and an Adreno 710 GPU. Xiaomi lists 8GB + 256GB and 12GB + 512GB versions with LPDDR4X RAM and UFS 2.2 storage, and some regions get 8GB + 128GB.

Most specifications list no memory card slot, but one source lists microSD support, so check the exact listing before relying on expandable storage. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.67-inch AMOLED at 1220 x 2712 pixels (about 446 pixels per inch) with a 120Hz refresh rate and 12-bit colour depth. A peak brightness of 1,800 nits is listed, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

Protection is Corning Gorilla Glass Victus. The fingerprint sensor sits under the screen and is optical.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 200MP main (f/1.65, 1/1.4-inch sensor, optical stabilisation) that bins 16 pixels into one 2.24µm pixel, an 8MP ultrawide (f/2.2) and a 2MP macro (f/2.4). Rear video goes up to 4K at 30fps, with 1080p at up to 60fps and 1080p slow motion at 120fps.

The front camera was not confirmed from the sources used here and is not listed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,100mAh (typical) with 67W turbo charging. Xiaomi lists a 67W charger in the box, with the note that the adapter is sold separately in some regions. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 13 and MIUI 14, and up to 3 major Android upgrades with HyperOS are listed. It has IP54 dust and splash resistance, Dolby Atmos stereo speakers, dual SIM (Nano-SIM plus Nano-SIM or eSIM) and a USB Type-C port. Xiaomi lists Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.2.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a mid-range 5G phone with a bright 120Hz AMOLED screen, a high-resolution main camera and quick charging will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want IP68 resistance, 120W charging or a curved display should look at the Redmi Note 13 Pro+. Buyers who want a newer chipset should look at a later Redmi Note.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G is a well-rounded mid-range phone: a good screen, a 200MP main camera and 67W charging, held back by older memory standards, IP54 and 4K video at only 30fps. It suits people who want strong value over top-tier build quality.

Which chipset does the Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 (SM7435-AB, 4 nm) with an Adreno 710 GPU.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 5,100mAh with 67W turbo charging. Xiaomi lists a 67W charger in the box, but the adapter is sold separately in some regions.

It is rated IP54, which means dust and splash resistant. It is not designed to be submerged.
What cameras does it have?
A 200MP main camera with optical stabilisation, an 8MP ultrawide and a 2MP macro camera.
What RAM and storage options are there?
Xiaomi lists 8GB + 256GB and 12GB + 512GB with LPDDR4X RAM and UFS 2.2 storage, and some regions get 8GB + 128GB.
Does it have a microSD slot?

Price varies by country, retailer, configuration, taxes and promotions. Reported launch prices include from $304 in the US and between about €250 and €400 in Europe depending on the market and version. Check a local seller.
