Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Pova 6 is the 4G model of the Pova 6 range. It shares the mecha design, 6.78-inch 120Hz AMOLED display, 6,000mAh battery and 70W charging with the Pova 6 Pro 5G, but uses a 4G-only MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate chipset and drops the Pro's third rear sensor.

It has a 108MP main camera with a depth sensor and a ring flash, a 32MP selfie camera, IP53 protection and stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate at about 2.2GHz, a 4G-only chip. It is offered with 8GB or 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. GPU details and the memory type were not confirmed and are not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch FHD+ AMOLED with a 120Hz refresh rate, 1,500Hz touch sampling, 100% DCI-P3 colour coverage and a 5,000,000:1 contrast ratio. Peak brightness is advertised at 1,300 nits, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 108MP main sensor with a depth sensor, and the front camera is 32MP. There is no ultrawide or telephoto camera.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6,000mAh with 70W wired charging and 10W reverse wired charging, which lets it charge other devices. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 14 with HiOS 14, has IP53 dust and splash resistance and stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos and Hi-Res audio, and supports dual SIM. A software-update period and connectivity versions were not confirmed and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a large battery with fast charging and a smooth AMOLED screen at a budget 4G price will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G should look at the Pova 6 Pro 5G. Buyers who want a newer model should look at the Pova 7 series.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Pova 6 is a no-frills 4G version of the Pova 6 Pro, with the same screen and battery but a weaker chipset. It suits people who do not need 5G and want the Pova look and battery for less.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pova 6 and Pova 6 Pro?
The Pova 6 uses a 4G-only Helio G99 Ultimate and has no third rear sensor. The Pova 6 Pro 5G uses a Dimensity 6080 chipset with 5G. The display, battery and charging are the same.
Which chipset does the Tecno Pova 6 use?
The MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate, which is 4G only.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 6,000mAh with 70W wired charging and 10W reverse wired charging.
What cameras does it have?
A 108MP main camera with a depth sensor on the back, plus a 32MP front camera.
Is the Pova 6 waterproof?
It is rated IP53, which means dust protected and splash resistant. It is not designed to be submerged.
What RAM and storage options are there?
8GB + 256GB and 12GB + 256GB.
What software does it run?
Android 14 with HiOS 14.
