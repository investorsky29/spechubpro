Expert Review

The Google Pixel 11 Pro XL is Google's largest premium flagship for 2026. It combines a 6.8-inch LTPO OLED display with the Tensor G6 processor, advanced Gemini-powered AI features, and an upgraded triple-camera system. The large model adds a 5115mAh battery and up to 120x Pro Res Zoom, making it ideal for users who want the biggest Pixel display and Google's most capable camera experience. Qi2 magnetic charging and seven years of software support further strengthen its flagship appeal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Google Pixel 11 Pro XL?
The Google Pixel 11 Pro XL is priced at $1,299. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the Google Pixel 11 Pro XL use?
The Google Pixel 11 Pro XL is powered by Google's own Tensor G6 chipset, available with up to 16GB of RAM on its highest configuration.
What is the battery capacity?
The Google Pixel 11 Pro XL has a 5115 mAh battery, the largest in the Pixel 11 lineup, with fast wired charging and Qi2 magnetic wireless charging.
When was the Google Pixel 11 Pro XL released?
The Google Pixel 11 Pro XL was released on August 20, 2026, alongside the rest of the Pixel 11 lineup.
How does the Pixel 11 Pro XL differ from the Pixel 11 Pro?
The Pixel 11 Pro XL uses a larger 6.8-inch display compared to the Pixel 11 Pro's 6.3-inch panel, has a bigger 5115mAh battery, and offers a 16GB RAM option, all at a $200 higher starting price.
What is the maximum zoom on the Pixel 11 Pro XL?
The Pixel 11 Pro XL supports up to 120x Pro Res Zoom, combining its 48MP periscope telephoto lens with Google's computational zoom processing.
