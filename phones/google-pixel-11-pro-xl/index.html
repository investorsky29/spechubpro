Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 11 Pro XL is the largest non-folding phone in Google's 2026 line, announced on August 12, 2026 and on sale from August 20. It has a 6.8-inch display and the largest reported battery of the four Pixel 11 models. It is aimed at buyers who want the biggest screen and battery.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Google Tensor G6 (2 nm, as reported) with the Titan M3 security chip. RAM is 12GB on the 256GB model and 16GB on higher-storage models. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.8-inch LTPO AMOLED display has 1344 x 2992 pixels. Refresh range and brightness could not be verified from the sources we checked, so they are not listed.

Camera Experience

Camera hardware could not be verified from the sources we checked, so no lens details are listed. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Pre-launch spec sheets report 5115 mAh typical, with a minimum rated capacity of 5000 mAh. Charging speeds could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone has the Titan M3 security chip and comes in Canyon, Olive, Fog and Obsidian. It weighs 226 g. The Android version and update policy were not verified for this page.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a large screen and the biggest battery in a Pixel will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who prefer a smaller, lighter phone should look at the Pixel 11 Pro or Pixel 11.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 11 Pro XL combines the Tensor G6 with the largest display and battery of the non-folding Pixel 11 models. Check Google's official sheet for camera and charging details before buying.
