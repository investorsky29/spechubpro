Expert Review

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 17 Pro Max is a 2026 Redmi smartphone focused heavily on battery life, display quality and fast charging. It features a 6.83-inch 1.5K AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and up to 3500 nits peak brightness, while the Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 provides mid-range performance. The rear camera system combines a 50MP OIS main camera with an 8MP ultrawide camera, while a 32MP front camera supports 4K video recording. Its standout feature is the large silicon-carbon battery, rated at 10,000mAh in some markets and 9,210mAh in others, with 100W HyperCharge and 27W wired reverse charging.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max?
The Xiaomi Redmi Note 17 Pro Max is priced at ₦812,500, depending on the RAM and storage configuration.
How big is the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max's battery?
The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max has a large silicon-carbon battery rated at 10,000mAh in most markets, though some regions ship a 9,210mAh variant. It supports 100W HyperCharge wired charging.
What chipset does the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max use?
The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 chipset built on a 4nm process, offering mid-range performance.
Does the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max have a telephoto camera?
No, the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max does not have a dedicated telephoto camera. Its rear setup consists of a 50MP OIS main camera and an 8MP ultrawide camera.
Does the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max support wireless charging?
No, the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max does not support wireless charging, but it does offer 27W wired reverse charging for topping up other devices.
Is the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max water resistant?
Yes, depending on market, the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max carries either an IP68 or IP69 rating for dust and water resistance.
