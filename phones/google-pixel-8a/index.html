Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 8a is the mid-range Pixel of 2024, released on May 14, 2024 at $499 in the US. It shares the Tensor G3 chip and a seven-year update promise with the Pixel 8 but uses a plastic back and a smaller feature set. It suits buyers who want Pixel software at a lower price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Google Tensor G3 (4 nm) with 8GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 128GB or 256GB of UFS 3.1 storage. Google promises seven years of OS and security updates. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.1-inch OLED display has 1080 x 2400 pixels and a refresh rate of 60-120Hz, with 1,400 nits for HDR and up to 2,000 nits peak. These figures match the Pixel 8, though the 8a is a bit smaller in screen area.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 64MP main with optical stabilisation and a 13MP ultrawide. The front camera is 13MP. There is no telephoto lens. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4492 mAh with fast wired charging and Qi wireless charging. Wattage figures are not listed because they could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14. It supports Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3 and NFC, and has an in-screen fingerprint sensor. It is rated IP67 and supports eSIM.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want modern Pixel features and long software support at a mid-range price will find it a strong match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a glass back, IP68 or a telephoto lens should look at the Pixel 8 or 8 Pro. Those who want Wi-Fi 7 should check the Pixel 8.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 8a gives mid-range buyers the Tensor G3 and a seven-year update promise. Its plastic back and Wi-Fi 6E are the main compromises.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel 8a and the Pixel 8?
Both use the Tensor G3 with 8GB of RAM. The Pixel 8 has a 6.2-inch display, a glass back, IP68, Wi-Fi 7 and a 4575 mAh battery. The Pixel 8a has a 6.1-inch display, a plastic back, IP67, Wi-Fi 6E and a 4492 mAh battery.
How long does the Pixel 8a get updates?
Google promises seven years of OS and security updates.
Does the Pixel 8a support wireless charging?
Yes. It supports Qi wireless charging.
Is the Pixel 8a water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP67.
How much RAM and storage does it have?
It has 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage.
How much did the Pixel 8a cost at launch?
In the US, the 128GB model launched at $499. Prices elsewhere differ, and current prices vary by retailer.
