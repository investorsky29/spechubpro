Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Camon 40 was unveiled on March 3, 2025 at MWC 2025 together with the Camon 40 Pro, Camon 40 Pro 5G and Camon 40 Premier 5G. It is the most basic member of the series, with a 6.78-inch Full HD+ 120Hz AMOLED display, a MediaTek Helio G100 chipset and a 5,200mAh battery with 45W charging.

It is a 4G phone. The Camon 40 Pro has the same hardware with a 50MP selfie camera, the Camon 40 Pro 5G swaps in a Dimensity 7300 chipset, and the Camon 40 Premier 5G is the top model.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G100, paired with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage. CPU and GPU details, the memory type and whether a memory card slot is included were not confirmed and are not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch Full HD+ AMOLED with a 120Hz refresh rate. Brightness, protection glass and pixel density were not confirmed and are not listed. The 32MP front camera is the main difference from the Camon 40 Pro, which uses a 50MP sensor with autofocus.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main camera with optical stabilisation, an 8MP ultrawide and a depth sensor, the same set shared by the Camon 40 Pro. There is no telephoto lens; the 3x optical zoom belongs to the Camon 40 Premier 5G.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,200mAh with 45W wired charging, the same as the Camon 40 Pro and Pro 5G. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The operating system version, update period, connectivity versions and water-resistance rating were not confirmed for this specific model and are not listed. Buyers should check the manufacturer's regional page for those details.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a large AMOLED screen, a big battery and an OIS main camera at the lower end of the Camon 40 range, and who do not need 5G, will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, a 50MP selfie camera or stronger performance should look at the Camon 40 Pro 5G, and buyers who want a telephoto camera should look at the Camon 40 Premier 5G.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Camon 40 is a straightforward 4G model: a good screen, a large battery and an OIS main camera, with a modest chipset and a lower-resolution selfie camera than its siblings. It suits people who want the Camon 40 look and battery at the lowest price point of the series.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Camon 40, Camon 40 Pro and Camon 40 Pro 5G?
The Camon 40 and Camon 40 Pro are 4G phones with the Helio G100. The Pro has a 50MP autofocus selfie camera, while the Camon 40 has a 32MP selfie camera. The Camon 40 Pro 5G uses a Dimensity 7300 chipset and a 144Hz display.
Which chipset does the Tecno Camon 40 use?
The MediaTek Helio G100.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 5,200mAh with 45W wired charging.
What cameras does it have?
A 50MP main camera with optical stabilisation, an 8MP ultrawide and a depth sensor on the back, plus a 32MP front camera.
Does it support 5G?
No. The Camon 40 is a 4G phone. The Camon 40 Pro 5G and Camon 40 Premier 5G support 5G.
What RAM and storage options are there?
8GB of RAM with 128GB or 256GB of storage.
