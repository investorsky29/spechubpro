Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Huawei Pura 70 Ultra is the top model of the Pura 70 line, announced in April 2024. It has a 16GB RAM base, a large 1-inch-type main camera sensor and a 50MP periscope telephoto lens. It is aimed at buyers who want Huawei's most camera-focused phone of that generation.

Performance and Processor

The phone has 16GB of RAM and 512GB or 1TB of storage. Independent sources report a HiSilicon Kirin 9010 chipset, although Huawei does not publish the chip name officially. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.8-inch LTPO OLED display supports a refresh rate of up to 120Hz and has an in-screen fingerprint sensor. Resolution and brightness were not verified for this page, so they are not listed.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main with a 1-inch-type sensor, variable aperture and optical stabilisation, a 50MP periscope telephoto with 3.5x optical zoom and a 40MP ultrawide. The front camera is 13MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5200 mAh with 100W wired and 80W wireless charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with HarmonyOS 4.2. It is rated IP68, supports satellite calls and messages and weighs 226 g.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Photography enthusiasts who want Huawei's biggest main sensor and a long zoom lens will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a lower price should look at the Pura 70 Pro. Buyers who want newer hardware can consider the Pura 80 Ultra.

Editorial Verdict

The Pura 70 Ultra is a camera-first flagship with a large main sensor and a generous memory base. Its unpublished chipset name and older software are the main caveats.
