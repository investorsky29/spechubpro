Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Hot 40 is a budget phone launched in December 2023 with a large 6.78-inch screen and a 5000 mAh battery. It sits below the Hot 40 Pro and above the Hot 40i. It is aimed at buyers who want a big screen and a long-lasting battery at a low price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Helio G88 chipset with 4GB or 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage, expandable by card. The Hot 40 Pro uses the faster Helio G99. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.78-inch IPS LCD has 1080 x 2460 pixels (about 396 ppi) and a 90Hz refresh rate. A software feature called Dynamic Bar uses the notch area to show alerts, similar in idea to the iPhone's Dynamic Island.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main and a 2MP macro, and the front camera is 32MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and apertures and video modes are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh with 33W wired charging. Wireless charging is not supported. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 13 and XOS 13.5, Infinix's own interface. It supports 4G only.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a big screen, a large battery and a good selfie camera at a very low price will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want smoother performance, an AMOLED screen or 5G should look at the Hot 40 Pro or a higher-tier Infinix phone.

Editorial Verdict

The Hot 40 is a straightforward budget phone with a big battery and screen. Its chipset and LCD display reflect its low price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Infinix Hot 40's battery capacity?
It has a 5000 mAh battery with 33W wired charging. Wireless charging is not supported.
Which chipset does the Infinix Hot 40 use?
It uses the MediaTek Helio G88.
What is the difference between the Hot 40 and the Hot 40 Pro?
The Hot 40 has a Helio G88, a 90Hz display and a 50MP main camera. The Hot 40 Pro has a Helio G99, a 120Hz display and a 108MP main camera.
Does the Hot 40 support 5G?
No. It supports 4G only.
Can the storage be expanded?
Yes. The storage is expandable with a memory card.
