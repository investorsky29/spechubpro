Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone Air is Apple's thinnest iPhone, at 5.64 mm and 165 g, with a titanium frame and a 6.5-inch display. It trades camera hardware and battery capacity for its slim body while using the same A19 Pro chip family as the Pro models. It is aimed at people who value weight and thinness over camera range.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Apple A19 Pro. Independent sources report 12GB of RAM, more than the 8GB reported for the iPhone 17. Storage options are 256GB, 512GB and 1TB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone, so sustained performance in such a thin body is untested here.

Display Experience

The 6.5-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display has 1260 x 2736 pixels and supports ProMotion up to 120Hz. Ceramic Shield 2 protects the front, and the Dynamic Island is retained.

Camera Experience

There is a single 48MP rear camera and an 18MP Center Stage front camera. No ultrawide or telephoto lens is listed, so the phone cannot take dedicated wide-angle or zoom shots. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Independent sources report a 3149 mAh battery, which Apple does not publish. This is smaller than the 3692 mAh reported for the iPhone 17. Charging speeds are not listed because they could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 26 and supports Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, NFC and USB-C. It is eSIM only, so there is no physical SIM tray, which matters when travelling or switching carriers. It has Face ID, the Action button and Camera Control.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want the lightest, thinnest iPhone with a large screen and modern chip, and who are comfortable with one rear camera, are the target.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who need a second rear lens, the largest possible battery or a physical SIM slot should look at the iPhone 17 or a Pro model.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone Air is a design-led phone: very thin and light with a fast chip, offset by a single camera, a smaller reported battery and eSIM-only connectivity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the iPhone Air the same as the iPhone 17 Air?
Apple sells this phone as the iPhone Air. It is a separate model from the iPhone 17.
How thin is the iPhone Air?
It is 5.64 mm thick and weighs 165 g.
Does the iPhone Air have a physical SIM slot?
No. It is eSIM only.
How many rear cameras does it have?
One. It has a single 48MP rear camera and an 18MP front camera.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone Air?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3149 mAh.
How much did the iPhone Air cost at launch?
In the US, launch prices were $999 for 256GB, $1,199 for 512GB and $1,399 for 1TB. Prices elsewhere and current retail prices differ.
