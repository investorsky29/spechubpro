Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Samsung Galaxy S25 was announced on January 22, 2025 and released on February 3, 2025. It is the compact model in the S25 family, with a 6.2-inch LTPO AMOLED 2X display, the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, 12GB of RAM and a triple rear camera.

This page lists the S25 family in general. Network bands and SIM arrangements differ between the International, USA and China versions (model numbers include SM-S931B, SM-S931U and SM-S9310), so check the exact model before you buy.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite (SM8750-AC, 3 nm), with two Oryon V2 Phoenix L cores at up to 4.47GHz and six Oryon V2 Phoenix M cores at up to 3.53GHz, plus an Adreno 830 GPU at 1200MHz.

Every version has 12GB of RAM, with 128GB, 256GB or 512GB of storage. The 128GB version uses UFS 3.1 and the larger versions use UFS 4.0. There is no card slot. SpecHub has not run benchmarks on this phone.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.2-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X panel at 1080 x 2340 pixels (about 416 pixels per inch) with a 120Hz refresh rate and HDR10+ support. GSMArena lists a peak brightness of 2,600 nits, which is a listed figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

The glass is Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2. The 12MP front camera (f/2.2) records 4K video at up to 60fps.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main (f/1.8, 1/1.56-inch sensor, optical stabilisation), a 10MP telephoto with 3x optical zoom and optical stabilisation, and a 12MP ultrawide (f/2.2, 120 degrees). There is no periscope telephoto.

Video goes up to 8K at 24 or 30fps, with 10-bit HDR and HDR10+ listed. These are listed capabilities; SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is a 4,000mAh Li-Ion cell, charged at up to 25W wired (PD3.0), 15W wireless (listed as Qi2 Ready) or 4.5W reverse wireless. GSMArena lists the wired charging as reaching 50% in 30 minutes, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub test.

SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15, and GSMArena lists up to 7 major OS updates. Connectivity includes 5G, Wi-Fi 7 (tri-band), Bluetooth 5.4, NFC, GNSS support for GPS, GLONASS, BDS, GALILEO and QZSS, and a USB Type-C 3.2 port with DisplayPort 1.2.

Other listed features are an ultrasonic under-display fingerprint sensor, Samsung DeX and Samsung Wireless DeX, stereo speakers and IP68 dust and water resistance. There is no 3.5mm jack and no FM radio.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a flagship-chip Android phone in a compact, 162 g body will find the most to like. It also suits buyers who want IP68 resistance, Wi-Fi 7 and a long update promise without a large screen.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a 200MP camera, a periscope telephoto, a stylus or a larger battery should look at the Galaxy S25 Ultra. People who want a headphone jack, a card slot or faster wired charging than 25W may prefer another phone.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the S25 is a compact flagship: it shares its chipset with the S25 Ultra but has a smaller screen, a smaller 4,000mAh battery, 25W charging and a simpler triple camera. It is best suited to people who value size and weight over camera reach and battery capacity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chipset does the Samsung Galaxy S25 use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite (SM8750-AC, 3 nm) with an Adreno 830 GPU, the same chipset GSMArena lists for the S25 Ultra.
How big is the screen and what is the refresh rate?
The display is a 6.2-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X panel at 1080 x 2340 pixels with a 120Hz refresh rate and HDR10+.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 4,000mAh. Charging is listed at 25W wired, 15W wireless (Qi2 Ready) and 4.5W reverse wireless.
Is the Galaxy S25 waterproof?
It is listed as IP68 dust tight and water resistant, immersible up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes. Water resistance is not permanent, so avoid exposing it to liquids unnecessarily.
What RAM and storage options are there?
GSMArena lists 128GB, 256GB and 512GB versions, all with 12GB of RAM. The 128GB version uses UFS 3.1 and the others use UFS 4.0. There is no memory card slot.
Does it have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 10MP telephoto with 3x optical zoom. It does not have the 5x periscope telephoto of the S25 Ultra.
How much does the Galaxy S25 cost?
Price varies by country, retailer, storage size, taxes and promotions, and SpecHub does not list a single price. Check a local seller or Samsung's regional site for the price in your region.
Does it have a headphone jack?
No. GSMArena lists no 3.5mm jack, and no FM radio either.
