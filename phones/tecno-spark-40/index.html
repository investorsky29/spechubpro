Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Spark 40 was announced on July 2, 2025 as the entry-level model of the Spark 40 series. It has a 6.67-inch HD+ 120Hz IPS display, a MediaTek Helio G81 chipset, an 8MP selfie camera and a 5,200mAh battery with 45W charging.

It costs less than the Spark 40 Pro and Pro+, which have AMOLED displays, faster chipsets and IP64 protection.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Helio G81. RAM and storage options were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.67-inch IPS LCD at HD+ resolution with a 120Hz refresh rate. Brightness and protection glass were not confirmed and are not listed. The Pro models use a larger 6.78-inch AMOLED with a 144Hz refresh rate.

Camera Experience

The front camera is 8MP. Rear camera specifications were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,200mAh with 45W wired charging, the same battery and charging speed as the Pro models. There is no wireless charging. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests.

Software and Features

The software version, water resistance and update period were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed. Colours are Ink Black, Titanium Grey, Veil White and Mirage Blue.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a low-cost phone with a big battery and fast wired charging, and who do not need an AMOLED screen, will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a sharper AMOLED display, a better selfie camera or splash resistance should look at the Spark 40 Pro or Pro+.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Spark 40 is the budget entry to the series, sharing the big battery and 45W charging of its siblings but with a basic HD+ screen and a modest chipset. It suits buyers who want the lowest price.

The Spark 40 has a 6.67-inch HD+ 120Hz IPS display, a Helio G81 and an 8MP selfie camera. The Spark 40 Pro has a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED, a Helio G100 Ultimate, a 13MP selfie camera and IP64.
