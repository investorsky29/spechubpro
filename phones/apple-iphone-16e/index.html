Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 16e is a lower-priced iPhone launched in February 2025, outside the main September cycle. It combines the A18 chip with a single rear camera and a 6.1-inch OLED display, and it keeps USB-C and IP68 resistance. It is aimed at buyers who want a current chip and a large battery at a lower price.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Apple A18 with 8GB of RAM reported by independent sources. Storage options are 128GB, 256GB and 512GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.1-inch OLED display has 1170 x 2532 pixels and a 60Hz refresh rate, with Ceramic Shield protection. It is slightly lower in resolution than the 1179 x 2556 panel of the iPhone 16. A notch is used rather than the Dynamic Island.

Camera Experience

There is a single 48MP rear camera and no ultrawide or telephoto lens. The front camera is 12MP. Because only one rear lens is listed, wide-angle shots are not available from a dedicated camera. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Independent sources report a 4005 mAh battery, the largest capacity among the standard-size iPhones covered in this group. Apple does not publish this figure. Charging speeds could not be verified, so they are not listed. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 18 and supports Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC and USB-C. It has Face ID. The 16e does not have the Camera Control button found on the iPhone 16.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who prioritise battery size and a lower entry price, and who are happy with a single rear camera, will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want an ultrawide camera, Wi-Fi 7 or the Camera Control button should look at the iPhone 16 or a newer model. The later iPhone 17e is a separate phone with its own specifications.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 16e trades camera hardware and some features for a lower price while keeping the A18 chip and a large reported battery. It is a value-focused choice rather than a full-featured one.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the iPhone 16e and the iPhone 16?
The 16e has one 48MP rear camera, a 1170 x 2532 display and a 4005 mAh reported battery. The iPhone 16 has a 48MP main plus 12MP ultrawide camera, a 1179 x 2556 display and a 3561 mAh reported battery.
Does the iPhone 16e have 5G?
Yes. Supported bands depend on the model number for your region.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 16e?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 4005 mAh.
Does the iPhone 16e use USB-C?
Yes. It has a USB-C port.
How much did the iPhone 16e cost at launch?
In the US, launch prices were $599 for 128GB, $699 for 256GB and $899 for 512GB. Prices elsewhere and current retail prices differ.
Is the iPhone 16e the same as the iPhone 17e?
No. The iPhone 17e is a later model released in March 2026 with its own specifications.
