Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 15 Pro is the smaller of the two Pro models from Apple's 2023 lineup. It moved the Pro range to a titanium frame, added the Action button in place of the mute switch, and was the first Pro iPhone with a USB-C port. It is aimed at buyers who want a compact phone with a telephoto lens and a high-refresh-rate screen.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A17 Pro is built on a 3 nm process and has a six-core CPU (two performance cores at up to 3.78GHz) and a six-core GPU. Apple does not publish RAM, but 8GB is widely reported, compared with 6GB on the standard iPhone 15. Storage ranges from 128GB to 1TB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone, so this page makes no performance or gaming claims.

Display Experience

The 6.1-inch LTPO OLED display has 1179 x 2556 pixels and an adaptive refresh rate from 1Hz to 120Hz. The low end of that range is what allows the Always-On display to run efficiently. Apple rates it for 1,000 nits typical, 1,600 nits peak in HDR and up to 2,000 nits outdoors. The Dynamic Island replaces the notch.

Camera Experience

The 48MP main camera has an f/1.78 lens and a 1/1.28-inch sensor with sensor-shift stabilization. It is joined by a 12MP ultrawide camera and a 12MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom, which the standard iPhone 15 lacks. The front camera is 12MP with autofocus. Video goes up to 4K at 60 fps with Dolby Vision, and ProRes recording is supported. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3274 mAh, which is smaller than the 3349 mAh reported for the standard iPhone 15. Apple states that the phone can reach about 50% in 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter, which is not included. Wireless charging supports MagSafe and Qi2 at up to 15W. We have no battery endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 17. It supports Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC and second-generation Ultra Wideband, and it has Face ID, a LiDAR scanner, Crash Detection and Emergency SOS via satellite. The USB-C port supports USB 3 speeds of up to 10 Gbps, which helps when moving large video files. SIM options depend on the market: international models take a Nano-SIM and an eSIM, US models use eSIM only, and China models take two Nano-SIMs.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a pocketable phone with a telephoto lens, a smooth screen and fast USB transfers will find it a good match. It also suits creators who record ProRes video and move files over USB-C.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who prioritise battery size or a longer zoom should look at the iPhone 15 Pro Max, which has a larger battery and a 5x telephoto lens. If you do not need a telephoto lens or a 120Hz display, the standard iPhone 15 covers most everyday needs at a lower price.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 15 Pro combines a titanium build, a ProMotion display, a telephoto lens and USB 3 speeds in a 187 g body. Its main limits are a modest reported battery capacity and the lack of a charger in the box. It is a strong choice for people who want a compact Pro iPhone rather than the largest screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the iPhone 15 Pro and the iPhone 15?
The iPhone 15 Pro has a titanium frame, a 1-120Hz ProMotion display with Always-On mode, the A17 Pro chip, a 3x telephoto camera, an Action button and USB 3 speeds. The standard iPhone 15 has an aluminum frame, a 60Hz display, the A16 Bionic chip and USB 2 speeds.
Does the iPhone 15 Pro have a 120Hz display?
Yes. It uses an adaptive ProMotion display that varies between 1Hz and 120Hz, and it supports the Always-On display.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 15 Pro?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3274 mAh.
How much RAM does the iPhone 15 Pro have?
Apple does not publish RAM. Independent sources report 8GB.
Does the iPhone 15 Pro have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 12MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom. The iPhone 15 Pro Max has a longer 5x telephoto lens.
Does it come with a charger?
No. Apple does not include a power adapter in the box. Check the retailer's listing for what is included in your market.
How much did the iPhone 15 Pro cost at launch?
In the US, launch prices were $999 for 128GB, $1,099 for 256GB, $1,299 for 512GB and $1,499 for 1TB. Prices in other countries and today's retail prices differ.
