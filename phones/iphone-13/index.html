Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 13 is the standard model of Apple's 2021 lineup, released on September 24, 2021 at $799 in the US. Compared with the iPhone 12 it has a newer chip, a larger reported battery, a smaller notch and 128GB of starting storage. It was aimed at buyers who wanted the main iPhone experience of that year. It is now an older phone.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A15 Bionic is a 5 nm chip with a six-core CPU and a four-core GPU. Independent sources report 4GB of RAM, which Apple does not publish. Storage is 128GB, 256GB or 512GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display has 1170 x 2532 pixels (about 460 ppi) and a 60Hz refresh rate. Apple rates it for 800 nits typical and up to 1,200 nits peak in HDR, which is brighter in typical use than the 625 nits of the iPhone 12. The notch is smaller than before.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 12MP main (f/1.6, sensor-shift stabilization) and a 12MP ultrawide (f/2.4, 120 degrees). There is no telephoto lens, which the Pro models add. The front camera is 12MP. Apple lists 4K video up to 60 fps with Dolby Vision and a Cinematic mode. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3227 mAh, compared with 2815 mAh reported for the iPhone 12. Apple states about 50% in 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter, which is not included. Wireless charging supports MagSafe at up to 15W and Qi at up to 7.5W. We have no endurance tests, and an older battery may hold less charge today.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 15 and supports 5G, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0, NFC and Ultra Wideband. It has Face ID, MagSafe and an IP68 rating up to 6 metres for 30 minutes. SIM options are a Nano-SIM plus an eSIM, or two Nano-SIMs in China.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a well-rounded older iPhone with 5G, OLED, MagSafe and a decent battery will find it a sensible choice at a secondhand price.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a 120Hz display, a telephoto lens or USB-C should look at a Pro model or a newer iPhone. Those who want a smaller body can consider the iPhone 13 mini.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 13 is a balanced older iPhone with good battery capacity for its generation. Its 60Hz screen and lack of a telephoto lens are the main limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the iPhone 13 and the iPhone 13 Pro?
The iPhone 13 has two rear cameras, a 60Hz display, 4GB of RAM (reported) and an aluminum frame. The iPhone 13 Pro has three rear cameras including a telephoto lens, a 10-120Hz ProMotion display, 6GB of RAM (reported) and a stainless steel frame.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 13?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3227 mAh.
Does the iPhone 13 support 5G?
Yes. It supports 5G on sub-6 GHz networks, and US models also support mmWave.
Is the iPhone 13 water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68 for up to 6 metres for 30 minutes.
Does it support MagSafe?
Yes. It supports MagSafe at up to 15W and Qi at up to 7.5W.
How much did the iPhone 13 cost at launch?
In the US, the launch price was $799 for 128GB. Prices elsewhere differ, and Apple no longer sells this model as a current product.
