Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 15 Plus is the large standard model of Apple's 2023 lineup, announced on September 12, 2023 and released on September 22 at $899 in the US. It brought the Dynamic Island, a 48MP main camera and USB-C to the big-screen non-Pro iPhone. It was aimed at buyers who wanted a large screen and long battery life without paying for a Pro Max. It is now an older phone.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A16 Bionic is a 4 nm chip with a six-core CPU (two cores at up to 3.46 GHz) and a five-core GPU. Independent sources report 6GB of RAM, which Apple does not publish. Storage is 128GB, 256GB or 512GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display has 1290 x 2796 pixels (about 460 ppi) and a 60Hz refresh rate. Apple rates it for 1,000 nits typical and up to 2,000 nits peak outdoors. The Dynamic Island replaces the notch. ProMotion and an always-on mode are limited to the Pro models.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 48MP main (f/1.6, sensor-shift stabilization) and a 12MP ultrawide (f/2.4, 120 degrees). There is no telephoto lens, and the 2x option crops the main sensor. The front camera is 12MP with autofocus. Apple lists 4K video up to 60 fps with Dolby Vision. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 4383 mAh, compared with 3349 mAh reported for the iPhone 15. Apple states about 50% in 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter, which is not included. Wireless charging supports MagSafe and Qi2 at up to 15W. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 17 and supports Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC and second-generation Ultra Wideband. It has Face ID, Crash Detection, Emergency SOS via satellite and an IP68 rating. The USB-C port supports USB 2 speeds and DisplayPort output.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a big OLED screen and a long reported battery in a mainstream iPhone, and who do not need ProMotion or a telephoto lens, will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a 120Hz display, a telephoto lens or fast USB transfers should look at the iPhone 15 Pro Max or a newer model. Those who prefer a smaller phone can consider the iPhone 15.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 15 Plus gives a large screen, USB-C and a strong reported battery at a lower price than the Pro Max. Its 60Hz display and lack of a telephoto lens are the main limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the iPhone 15 Plus and the iPhone 15?
Both use the A16 Bionic chip and the same cameras. The iPhone 15 Plus has a 6.7-inch display, a reported 4383 mAh battery and weighs 201 g. The iPhone 15 has a 6.1-inch display, a reported 3349 mAh battery and weighs 171 g.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 15 Plus?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 4383 mAh.
Does the iPhone 15 Plus use USB-C?
Yes. It has a USB-C port at USB 2 speeds, with DisplayPort output.
Does it have a 120Hz display?
No. It has a 60Hz OLED display. ProMotion is available on the iPhone 15 Pro models.
How much RAM does it have?
Apple does not publish RAM. Independent sources report 6GB.
How much did the iPhone 15 Plus cost at launch?
In the US, the launch price was $899 for 128GB. Prices elsewhere differ, and current retail prices vary.
