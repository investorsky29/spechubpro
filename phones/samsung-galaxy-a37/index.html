Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy A37 5G is a premium mid-range Galaxy A-series phone launched in 2026. It features a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate, an Exynos 1480 processor, and a 5000mAh battery with 45W charging. Its 50MP OIS main camera is paired with an 8MP ultrawide and 5MP macro camera, while IP68 protection provides strong resistance against dust and water. Running Android 16 with One UI 8.5, the Galaxy A37 is designed for users who want Samsung's long-term software support and everyday reliability without paying flagship prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy A37 5G?
The Samsung Galaxy A37 5G is priced at $449. Prices may vary by region and retailer.

The Samsung Galaxy A37 5G is powered by Samsung's own Exynos 1480 chipset with an octa-core CPU and Xclipse 530 GPU.
