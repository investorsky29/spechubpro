Expert Review

The Samsung Galaxy S10e is the compact member of Samsung's 2019 Galaxy S10 family. It delivers flagship performance in a smaller 5.8-inch body, using the same Exynos 9820 or Snapdragon 855 platform found in the larger S10 models. Its dual-camera system includes a 12MP OIS main sensor and a 16MP ultrawide camera, while IP68 protection, wireless charging, expandable storage, and a headphone jack give it a strong feature set for a compact phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Samsung Galaxy S10e?
The Samsung Galaxy S10e is priced at around $109 today as a used or refurbished device. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the Samsung Galaxy S10e use?
The Samsung Galaxy S10e uses either a Samsung Exynos 9820 or a Qualcomm Snapdragon 855 chipset depending on the region, the same platform used in the larger Galaxy S10 and S10+.
Does the Galaxy S10e have a telephoto camera?
No, the Galaxy S10e uses a dual-camera system with a 12MP main sensor and a 16MP ultrawide lens, but it does not include the telephoto lens found on the standard Galaxy S10 and S10+.
When was the Samsung Galaxy S10e released?
The Samsung Galaxy S10e was released on March 8, 2019, alongside the Galaxy S10 and Galaxy S10+.
Does the Galaxy S10e support wireless charging?
Yes, the Galaxy S10e supports 15W wireless charging as well as Wireless PowerShare, letting it charge other Qi-compatible devices.
Is the Galaxy S10e a good compact phone today?
For buyers who specifically want a smaller phone, the Galaxy S10e still offers a sharp display and solid build quality, though its lack of 5G, smaller 3100mAh battery, and aging processor are worth weighing against newer compact options.
