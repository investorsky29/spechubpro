Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Tecno Pova 6 Pro 5G was unveiled at MWC 2024 in February, launched in India on March 29, 2024 and went on sale on April 4. It is a gaming-oriented mid-ranger with a 6.78-inch 120Hz AMOLED display, a MediaTek Dimensity 6080 chipset, a 108MP main camera, a 6,000mAh battery and 70W charging.

Its signature feature is the Arc Interface, a ring of 210 individually controlled mini LEDs on the back. The Tecno Pova 6 (4G) uses the same design with a Helio G99 Ultimate chipset instead.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 6080 (6 nm), with two 2.4GHz Cortex-A76 and six 2.0GHz Cortex-A55 cores and a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. RAM is 8GB or 12GB with 256GB of storage, and up to 12GB of virtual RAM can be added. A 27,342 mm² cooling area and a 4D Vibration Sense motor are listed.

SpecHub has not run benchmarks or thermal tests, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.78-inch FHD+ AMOLED (1080 x 2436) with a 120Hz refresh rate and 2160Hz dimming, rated at 1,300 nits peak, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement. It has TÜV Rheinland Low Blue Light certification and an in-display fingerprint scanner.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 108MP main camera (f/1.89) with a 2MP sensor and an AI lens. There is no ultrawide or telephoto camera. The front camera is 32MP.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6,000mAh with 70W charging, advertised as reaching 50% in 20 minutes and 100% in about 50 minutes, and a 70W charger is included in the box. It also supports 10W reverse charging. Tecno lists safety features such as Bypass Charge and Charging Water Detection. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests, and SpecHub has not run battery-life tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and HiOS 14. Connectivity includes 5G (SA and NSA), dual 4G VoLTE, 802.11ac Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.1, NFC and a USB Type-C port. A software-update period and a water-resistance rating were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a big battery with fast charging, a smooth AMOLED screen and a distinctive gaming look at a budget 5G price will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a stronger chipset, an ultrawide camera, newer Wi-Fi or a newer phone should look at the Pova 7 5G series or another model.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the Pova 6 Pro 5G offers an excellent battery and charging package and a good display for its tier, with an entry-level chipset and a basic camera set. It suits people who prioritise battery life and charging speed over performance and camera range.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chipset does the Tecno Pova 6 Pro 5G use?
The MediaTek Dimensity 6080 (6 nm) with a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 6,000mAh with 70W charging, and a 70W charger is included. It also supports 10W reverse charging.
What is the difference between the Pova 6 and Pova 6 Pro?
The Pova 6 Pro 5G has a Dimensity 6080 chipset with 5G and a third rear sensor, while the Pova 6 uses a 4G-only Helio G99 Ultimate. Their displays, batteries and charging are the same.
What cameras does it have?
A 108MP main camera, a 2MP sensor and an AI lens on the back, plus a 32MP front camera.
What is the Arc Interface?
It is a set of 210 individually controlled mini LEDs on the back of the phone that create light effects for notifications, charging and gaming.
What RAM and storage options are there?
8GB + 256GB and 12GB + 256GB, with up to 12GB of virtual RAM.
How much did the Tecno Pova 6 Pro 5G cost at launch?
In India it launched at ₹19,999 for 8GB + 256GB and ₹21,999 for 12GB + 256GB. Prices vary by country, retailer and promotions.
What software does it run?
Android 14 with HiOS 14.
