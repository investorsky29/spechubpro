Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Note 40 Pro+ 5G is the top model of the Note 40 family, announced on March 19, 2024. It combines 5G, 100W wired charging, wireless charging and a curved AMOLED display. It is aimed at buyers who want the fastest charging and the full feature set of the Note 40 line.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Dimensity 7020 (6 nm) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. The 4G Note 40 models use the Helio G99 Ultimate instead. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.78-inch curved AMOLED display has a 120Hz refresh rate. It matches the Note 40 Pro's screen size.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 108MP main sensor with optical stabilisation, and the front camera is 32MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and secondary lenses and video modes are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4600 mAh, smaller than the 5000 mAh on the other Note 40 models, but supports 100W wired and 20W MagCharge wireless charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and XOS 14, supports 5G and weighs 196 g.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want 5G, very fast wired charging and wireless charging in a low-priced phone will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want the largest battery should look at the other Note 40 models. Buyers who want a faster chip should consider the later Note 50 Pro+.

Editorial Verdict

The Note 40 Pro+ 5G is the most complete Note 40, with 5G and the fastest charging. Its smaller battery and mid-range chip are the main trade-offs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast does the Infinix Note 40 Pro+ charge?
It supports 100W wired charging and 20W MagCharge wireless charging with a 4600 mAh battery.
Does the Note 40 Pro+ support 5G?
Yes. It uses a MediaTek Dimensity 7020 chipset with 5G.
What is the Note 40 Pro+'s battery capacity?
It has a 4600 mAh battery, smaller than the 5000 mAh of the other Note 40 models.
How much RAM does it have?
It has 12GB of RAM, with 256GB or 512GB of storage.
How much did the Note 40 Pro+ cost at launch?
Launch pricing was $309 in US dollars. Prices in other countries differ.
What is the difference between the Note 40 Pro+ and the Note 50 Pro+?
The Note 50 Pro+ has a Dimensity 8350 Ultimate, a 5200 mAh battery and a periscope telephoto camera. The Note 40 Pro+ has a Dimensity 7020 and a 4600 mAh battery.
