Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Smart 9 is an entry-level phone announced on September 23, 2024 and released in October 2024. It has a 6.7-inch 120Hz LCD, a Helio G81 chip, a 5000 mAh battery and IP54 splash resistance, which is unusual at this price. It is aimed at buyers who want the basics at the lowest price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Helio G81 with an octa-core CPU and a Mali-G52 MC2 GPU. RAM is 3GB, 4GB or 6GB and storage is 64GB or 128GB, expandable by microSDXC. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch IPS LCD has 720 x 1600 pixels (about 262 ppi) and a 120Hz refresh rate, with a peak brightness of 500 nits. The low resolution keeps the price down but makes text less sharp than on full-HD screens.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a single 13MP (f/1.9) unit, and the front camera is 8MP (f/2.0). Video goes up to 1080p at 30 fps. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh with 10W wired charging, so a full charge is slow. Wireless charging is not supported. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 (Go edition) and XOS 14. It has stereo speakers, a 3.5mm headphone jack, dual-band Wi-Fi and dual Nano-SIM, but no NFC.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want an inexpensive phone with a smooth screen, a headphone jack and a long-lasting battery will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want faster charging, NFC, a stronger camera or 5G should look at the Hot 40 or Hot 50 series.

Editorial Verdict

The Smart 9 is a basic phone with a few useful extras for its price, such as 120Hz and IP54. Its charging speed and screen resolution are the main limits.
