Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 11 is the standard model of Google's 2026 line, announced on August 12, 2026 alongside the Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. It weighs 197 g and uses the new Tensor G6 chip. The phone goes on sale from August 20, 2026.

Performance and Processor

All four Pixel 11 models use Google's Tensor G6, paired with the Titan M3 security chip. Reported figures describe it as built on a 2 nm process. Starting storage is 256GB. RAM for the standard Pixel 11 could not be verified, so it is not listed. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The display is listed at 6.3 inches, the same size as the Pixel 11 Pro. Resolution, refresh rate and brightness could not be verified for the standard model, so they are not listed here.

Camera Experience

Camera hardware for the standard Pixel 11 could not be verified from the sources we checked, so no lens details are listed. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Pre-launch spec sheets report a 4985 mAh typical battery with a minimum rated capacity of 4840 mAh. Charging speeds could not be verified, so none are listed. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The Titan M3 security chip is part of the Pixel 11 line. The Android version and update policy were not verified for this page.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want the newest Pixel in a lighter body and the lowest entry point of the 2026 line will find it a fit, once its full specifications are confirmed.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a larger screen or the highest-end camera features should look at the Pixel 11 Pro or Pro XL. Those who want a foldable should consider the Pixel 11 Pro Fold.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 11 brings the Tensor G6 to Google's standard model. Check Google's official specification sheet for display, camera and charging details before buying.
