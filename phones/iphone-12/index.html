Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 12 is the standard model of Apple's 2020 lineup, released on October 23, 2020 at $799 in the US. It was the first mainstream iPhone with 5G, an OLED display across the range and MagSafe wireless charging. It was aimed at buyers who wanted the main iPhone experience of that year. It is now an older phone.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A14 Bionic is a 5 nm chip with a six-core CPU and a four-core GPU. Independent sources report 4GB of RAM, which Apple does not publish. Storage is 64GB, 128GB or 256GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display has 1170 x 2532 pixels (about 460 ppi) and a 60Hz refresh rate. Apple rates it for 625 nits typical and up to 1,200 nits peak in HDR, with Ceramic Shield protection. The iPhone 12 Pro has the same size but a higher typical brightness of 800 nits.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 12MP main (f/1.6, optical stabilisation) and a 12MP ultrawide (f/2.4, 120 degrees). There is no telephoto lens, which the iPhone 12 Pro adds. The front camera is 12MP. Video goes up to 4K at 60 fps with Dolby Vision. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 2815 mAh. Apple states about 50% in 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter, which is not included. Wireless charging supports MagSafe at up to 15W and Qi at up to 7.5W. We have no endurance tests, and an older battery may hold less charge today.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 14 and supports 5G, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0, NFC and Ultra Wideband. It has Face ID, MagSafe and an IP68 rating up to 6 metres for 30 minutes. SIM options are a Nano-SIM plus an eSIM, or two Nano-SIMs in China.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want an affordable older iPhone with 5G, an OLED screen and MagSafe accessory support will find it a reasonable fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a telephoto lens, a 120Hz display or longer battery life should look at the iPhone 12 Pro or a newer model. Those who want a smaller body can consider the iPhone 12 mini.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 12 is a capable older iPhone with 5G, OLED and MagSafe. Its 60Hz screen, modest reported battery and lack of a telephoto lens are the main limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the iPhone 12 support 5G?
Yes. It supports 5G on sub-6 GHz networks, and US models also support mmWave.
What is the difference between the iPhone 12 and the iPhone 12 Pro?
Both have a 6.1-inch display. The iPhone 12 has two rear cameras, 4GB of RAM reported and 625 nits typical brightness. The iPhone 12 Pro has three rear cameras including a telephoto lens, 6GB of RAM reported, 800 nits typical brightness and a stainless steel frame.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 12?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 2815 mAh.
Does the iPhone 12 support MagSafe?
Yes. It supports MagSafe wireless charging at up to 15W and Qi charging at up to 7.5W.
Is the iPhone 12 water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68 for up to 6 metres for 30 minutes.
How much did the iPhone 12 cost at launch?
In the US, the launch price was $799 for 64GB. Prices elsewhere differ, and Apple no longer sells this model as a current product.
