Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Hot 40 Pro is the higher-spec model of the Hot 40 family, launched in December 2023 with the Helio G99 chip, a 120Hz display and a 108MP main camera. It is aimed at buyers who want a budget phone that handles games and social media more smoothly than the Hot 40.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Helio G99 (6 nm) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of UFS 2.2 storage. The Hot 40 uses the slower Helio G88. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.78-inch IPS LCD has 1080 x 2460 pixels (about 396 ppi) and a 120Hz refresh rate, higher than the 90Hz of the Hot 40. The Dynamic Bar feature uses the notch area to show alerts.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 108MP main and a 2MP macro, and the front camera is 32MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and apertures and video modes are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh with 33W wired charging, which Infinix says takes the phone from 20% to 75% in about 35 minutes. Wireless charging is not supported. We have no charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 13 and XOS 13.5. It supports 4G only.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers on a budget who want a smooth 120Hz screen and a capable chip will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want 5G, an AMOLED display or the latest Android version should look at a newer Infinix such as the Hot 50 Pro.

Editorial Verdict

The Hot 40 Pro raises performance and screen smoothness above the Hot 40 at a small extra cost. Its LCD display and 4G-only connectivity show its budget positioning.
