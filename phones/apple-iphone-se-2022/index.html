Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone SE (2022), also called the third-generation iPhone SE, kept the compact body and Home button of earlier SE and iPhone 8 models while moving to the A15 Bionic chip and adding 5G. It launched at $429 in the US. It was aimed at buyers who wanted a small, familiar iPhone with Touch ID and a current chip at a low price.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A15 Bionic is a 5 nm chip with a six-core CPU and a four-core GPU. Independent sources report 4GB of RAM, which Apple does not publish. Storage options are 64GB, 128GB and 256GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 4.7-inch Retina HD LCD has 750 x 1334 pixels (about 326 ppi) at 60Hz and is rated for 625 nits typical brightness. It has large bezels above and below the screen, and it is far smaller than the 6.1-inch displays on recent standard iPhones.

Camera Experience

There is a single 12MP rear camera (f/1.8) and a 7MP front camera (f/2.2). No ultrawide or telephoto lens is listed. Video goes up to 4K at 60 fps, and 1080p slow motion goes up to 240 fps. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Independent sources report a 2018 mAh battery, which Apple does not publish. Apple states about 50% in 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter, which is not included. Wireless charging uses Qi at up to 7.5W; MagSafe is not supported. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 15 and supports Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0 and NFC. Security is handled by Touch ID in the Home button. SIM options are a Nano-SIM plus eSIM. It uses a Lightning port rather than USB-C.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a small iPhone with a physical Home button and Touch ID, and who value a low price over screen size and camera range, may still find it fits if they can find one at a good price.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a larger, sharper screen, longer battery life, a second camera, USB-C or MagSafe should consider a newer iPhone such as the iPhone 16e or iPhone 15.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone SE (2022) offered a fast chip in a compact, familiar body, but its display, battery and camera reflect an older design. It is now a niche choice for fans of the small-iPhone form factor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the iPhone SE (2022) the same as the iPhone SE 3?
Yes. It is also called the iPhone SE (3rd generation).
Does the iPhone SE (2022) support 5G?
Yes, on sub-6 GHz networks. It does not support mmWave.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone SE (2022)?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 2018 mAh.
Does it have Face ID or Touch ID?
It has Touch ID in the Home button and no Face ID.
Does it support wireless charging?
Yes, with Qi chargers at up to 7.5W. MagSafe is not supported.
How much did the iPhone SE (2022) cost at launch?
In the US, the launch price was $429 for the 64GB model. Prices elsewhere differ, and Apple no longer sells this model.
