Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Galaxy Z Flip7 is Samsung's 2025 clamshell foldable. It opens to a tall 6.9-inch display and closes into a compact square with a 4.1-inch cover screen. It is released alongside the cheaper Galaxy Z Flip7 FE, which uses different hardware. It suits buyers who want a pocketable phone and are willing to pay a premium for the form factor.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Samsung Exynos 2500 with a ten-core CPU at up to 3.3 GHz and an Xclipse 950 GPU. RAM is 12GB and storage is 256GB or 512GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The main display is a 6.9-inch foldable AMOLED at 1080 x 2520 pixels with a refresh rate of up to 120Hz. The cover display is 4.1 inches (948 x 1048 pixels) and can show notifications, widgets and camera previews without opening the phone.

Camera Experience

A 50MP main camera (f/1.8, optical stabilisation) is listed. Other rear and front cameras are omitted because we could not verify them. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4300 mAh with 25W wired charging, 15W wireless charging and 4.5W reverse wireless charging. Compared with non-folding flagships such as the Galaxy S25+ (4900 mAh), the battery is smaller. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 16. Samsung promises seven years of OS and security updates. The IP48 rating protects against water but only limited dust, which is typical for foldables with a hinge.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a small, pocketable phone that opens into a full-size screen, and who are comfortable with a premium price, will find it appealing.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want the longest battery life, fast charging or a dust-resistance rating should consider a non-folding phone such as the Galaxy S25+. Those who want a tablet-size screen should consider the Galaxy Z Fold7.

Editorial Verdict

The Galaxy Z Flip7 delivers a very compact foldable with a large cover display and strong update support. Its limits are the smaller battery, 25W charging and IP48 rating.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Galaxy Z Flip7 and the Galaxy Z Flip7 FE?
The Z Flip7 has an Exynos 2500, 12GB of RAM, a 4300 mAh battery and a 4.1-inch cover display. The Z Flip7 FE uses an Exynos 2400, 8GB of RAM and a 4000 mAh battery.
What is the Galaxy Z Flip7's water resistance?
It is rated IP48. That protects against water but only limited dust.
How big are the Galaxy Z Flip7's screens?
The main foldable display is 6.9 inches and the cover display is 4.1 inches.
How long does the Galaxy Z Flip7 get updates?
Samsung promises seven years of OS and security updates.
How fast does it charge?
Wired charging is listed at 25W, wireless charging at 15W and reverse wireless charging at 4.5W.
How much did the Galaxy Z Flip7 cost at launch?
In the US, the 12GB + 256GB model launched at $1,099. Prices vary by country, configuration and promotions.
