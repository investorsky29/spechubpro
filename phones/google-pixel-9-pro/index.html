Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 9 Pro is the compact flagship of Google's 2024 line. It has the same 6.3-inch screen size as the Pixel 9 but adds a higher-resolution LTPO panel, 16GB of RAM, a telephoto camera and a 42MP front camera. It was released on September 4, 2024 at $999 in the US.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Google Tensor G4 with the Titan M2 security chip, 16GB of RAM and storage of up to 1TB. Google promises seven years of OS and security updates. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.3-inch Super Actua OLED display has 1280 x 2856 pixels and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, with a peak brightness of up to 3,000 nits. That is sharper and brighter than the 1080 x 2424 panel of the standard Pixel 9.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main, a 48MP ultrawide and a 48MP telephoto with 5x optical zoom. The front camera is 42MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4700 mAh with 27W wired charging and 21W wireless charging, plus Battery Share. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and has Gorilla Glass Victus 2 and IP68 resistance.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a compact phone with a long zoom lens and a high-resolution display will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a larger screen and battery should look at the Pixel 9 Pro XL. Those who do not need a telephoto lens may prefer the Pixel 9.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 9 Pro offers a flagship camera set and display in a compact body. Its main trade-offs are the price and the smaller battery compared with the XL.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel 9 Pro and the Pixel 9 Pro XL?
The Pixel 9 Pro has a 6.3-inch 1280 x 2856 display, a 4700 mAh battery, 27W wired and 21W wireless charging, and weighs 199 g. The Pixel 9 Pro XL has a 6.8-inch 1344 x 2992 display, a 5060 mAh battery, 37W wired and 23W wireless charging, and weighs 221 g.
Does the Pixel 9 Pro have a telephoto camera?
Yes. It has a 48MP telephoto camera with 5x optical zoom.
How much RAM does the Pixel 9 Pro have?
It has 16GB of RAM, with storage of up to 1TB.
How long does it get updates?
Google promises seven years of OS and security updates.
Is the Pixel 9 Pro water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68.
How much did the Pixel 9 Pro cost at launch?
In the US, the 128GB model launched at $999. Prices elsewhere differ, and current prices vary by retailer.
