Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone XR was the lower-priced model of Apple's 2018 lineup, announced on September 12, 2018 and released on October 26 at $749 in the US. It combined the A12 Bionic chip and Face ID with an LCD display and a single rear camera, and it came in six colours. It is now an older phone.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A12 Bionic is a 7 nm chip with a six-core CPU and a four-core GPU. Independent sources report 3GB of RAM, compared with 4GB for the iPhone XS; Apple does not publish this figure. Storage is 64GB, 128GB or 256GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.1-inch Liquid Retina display is an IPS LCD with 828 x 1792 pixels (about 326 ppi) and a 60Hz refresh rate, rated for 625 nits typical brightness. It uses True Tone and a wide color gamut. It is less sharp and lower in contrast than the OLED displays of the iPhone XS models.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a single 12MP main (f/1.8, optical stabilisation), and the front camera is 7MP (f/2.2). There is no telephoto lens, which the iPhone XS adds. Apple lists 4K video up to 60 fps. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity. Regulatory filings reported 2942 mAh, larger than the 2658 mAh reported for the iPhone XS. Apple states about 50% in 30 minutes with an 18W or higher adapter, although a 5W adapter was in the box. Wireless charging uses Qi at up to 7.5W. We have no endurance tests, and an old battery may hold less charge today.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 12 and supports Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.0 and NFC. It has Face ID and an IP67 rating (up to 1 metre for 30 minutes). It uses a Lightning port and does not support 5G. SIM options are a Nano-SIM plus an eSIM, or two Nano-SIMs in China.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want an inexpensive older iPhone with Face ID, a colourful body and a decent reported battery capacity may still find it a fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Anyone who needs 5G, an OLED screen, a telephoto lens or current software support should look at a newer iPhone.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone XR offered most of the iPhone XS experience for less money. Its LCD, single camera and age are the main limits today.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the iPhone XR support 5G?
No. The iPhone XR supports LTE only.
What is the difference between the iPhone XR and the iPhone XS?
The iPhone XR has a 6.1-inch LCD, one rear camera and 3GB of reported RAM. The iPhone XS has a 5.8-inch OLED, two rear cameras, 4GB of reported RAM and IP68 resistance.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone XR?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Regulatory filings reported 2942 mAh.
What colours did the iPhone XR come in?
Black, White, Yellow, Coral, Blue and (PRODUCT)RED.
Does it support wireless charging?
Yes. It supports Qi wireless charging at up to 7.5W.
How much did the iPhone XR cost at launch?
In the US, launch prices were $749 for 64GB, $799 for 128GB and $899 for 256GB. Prices elsewhere differ, and Apple no longer sells this model.
