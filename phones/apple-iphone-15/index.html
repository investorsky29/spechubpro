Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 15 is the standard model of Apple's 2023 lineup and the first iPhone with a USB-C port. It brought the Dynamic Island and a 48MP main camera down from the Pro models of the previous year, while keeping an aluminum frame and a 60Hz display. It suits people who want a current-feeling iPhone without paying Pro prices.

Performance and Processor

The iPhone 15 uses the Apple A16 Bionic, the same chip that powered the iPhone 14 Pro, with a six-core CPU and a five-core GPU. Apple does not publish RAM, but teardowns and developer tools report 6GB. That is the figure listed here, marked as reported. Storage starts at 128GB and there is no card slot, so choose the capacity you need when you buy. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone and makes no gaming claims.

Display Experience

The 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display has 1179 x 2556 pixels and runs at a fixed 60Hz. Apple rates it for 1,000 nits typical brightness and up to 2,000 nits peak outdoors. The Dynamic Island replaces the notch and shows alerts and live activities. There is no ProMotion refresh rate and no always-on display, which are limited to the Pro models in this generation.

Camera Experience

The main camera is a 48MP unit with an f/1.6 lens and sensor-shift stabilization, paired with a 12MP ultrawide camera. There is no dedicated telephoto lens, so the 2x option is a crop of the main sensor. The front camera is a 12MP unit with autofocus. Apple lists 4K video up to 60 fps with Dolby Vision HDR and slow-motion video at 1080p. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity. Regulatory filings and teardowns report 3349 mAh, shown here as reported. Apple states that the phone can reach about 50% in 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter, which is not included in the box. Wireless charging works with MagSafe and Qi2 chargers at up to 15W. We have no endurance tests for this phone.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 17 and is updated directly by Apple. It supports Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC and second-generation Ultra Wideband, and it has Face ID, Crash Detection and Emergency SOS via satellite. SIM options depend on the market: international models take a Nano-SIM plus an eSIM, US models use eSIM only, and China models take two Nano-SIMs.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People moving from an older iPhone or an Android phone who want USB-C, a good main camera and long Apple software support will find it a sensible fit. It also suits anyone who wants MagSafe accessories without paying for a Pro model.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a smoother 120Hz display, an always-on screen or an optical telephoto lens should look at a Pro model or a newer iPhone. People who need fast USB data transfers should note that this model's USB-C port runs at USB 2 speeds.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 15 is a well-rounded standard iPhone with a flagship-class main camera, USB-C and the Dynamic Island. Its limits are the 60Hz display, the lack of a telephoto lens and the slow USB speed. It is a practical choice for everyday use rather than for display or camera enthusiasts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the iPhone 15 support 5G?
Yes. All models support 5G on sub-6 GHz networks, and US models also support mmWave. Supported bands depend on the model number.
Does the iPhone 15 use USB-C?
Yes. It has a USB-C port, but it runs at USB 2 speeds, and it supports DisplayPort output for external displays.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 15?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3349 mAh. Apple states that the phone can reach about 50% charge in 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter.
Does the iPhone 15 come with a charger?
No. Apple does not include a power adapter in the box. Check the retailer's listing for what is included in your market.
Is the iPhone 15 water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68 (up to 6 metres for 30 minutes). Apple notes that water resistance is not permanent and can decrease with wear.
How much RAM does the iPhone 15 have?
Apple does not publish RAM. Independent sources report 6GB.
How much did the iPhone 15 cost at launch?
In the US, launch prices were $799 for 128GB, $899 for 256GB and $1,099 for 512GB. Prices in other countries and today's retail prices differ.
