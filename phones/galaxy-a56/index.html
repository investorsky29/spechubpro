Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Galaxy A56 5G is the higher-priced of Samsung's two main 2025 A-series phones. Compared with the A36, it has an aluminum frame, an Exynos 1580 chip and a higher-resolution ultrawide camera. It suits buyers who want a better-built mid-range Samsung with the same long update plan.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Samsung Exynos 1580 with an octa-core CPU that runs at up to 2.9 GHz. RAM is 8GB or 12GB and storage is 128GB or 256GB, with no memory card slot. The Galaxy A36 5G uses a Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 instead. SpecHub has not benchmarked either phone, so no performance claims are made.

Display Experience

The 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display has 1080 x 2340 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate. It matches the A36 on paper, so the display is not the main reason to choose one over the other. Dual stereo speakers are listed, which helps for video.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main camera, a 12MP ultrawide and a 5MP macro. No telephoto lens is listed. The ultrawide is a step up from the 8MP unit on the A36. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and apertures, stabilisation and front-camera details are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh with 45W wired charging, the same as the A36. Wireless charging is not listed. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 15 with One UI 7. Samsung lists six generations of OS upgrades and up to six years of security updates. The USB port is USB-C.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a metal frame, stereo speakers and a better ultrawide camera in a mid-range Samsung, and who plan to keep the phone for years, are the target.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People on a tighter budget may prefer the Galaxy A36 5G, which has the same display and battery. Those who want wireless charging or a telephoto lens should look at more expensive Galaxy models.

Editorial Verdict

The Galaxy A56 5G refines the A36 formula with a better frame and ultrawide camera while keeping the same display, battery and update promise. The extra cost is a matter of how much those changes matter to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Galaxy A56 5G and the Galaxy A36 5G?
Both have a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display, a 5000 mAh battery and 45W charging. The A56 has an aluminum frame, an Exynos 1580 chip and a 12MP ultrawide camera. The A36 has a Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 and an 8MP ultrawide camera.
How long will the Galaxy A56 5G get updates?
Samsung lists six generations of OS upgrades and up to six years of security updates.
What chipset does the Galaxy A56 5G use?
It uses the Samsung Exynos 1580.
How fast does it charge?
Wired charging is listed at 45W with a 5000 mAh battery. Wireless charging is not listed.
Does it have a memory card slot?
No. Storage options are 128GB and 256GB, depending on the region.
How much did the Galaxy A56 5G cost at launch?
In the US, the 8GB + 128GB model launched at $499. Prices and RAM options differ by country.
