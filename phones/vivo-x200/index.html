Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The vivo X200 was announced in China on October 14, 2024 and released there on October 19. It reached global markets from November 19, 2024, starting in Malaysia, and launched in India in December 2024. It is the more compact of vivo's X200 flagships, with a 6.67-inch OLED display, the MediaTek Dimensity 9400 chipset and a triple 50MP ZEISS camera.

The global model has a 5,800mAh battery, and a 5,220mAh version is sold only in Austria. The larger vivo X200 Pro has a 200MP periscope telephoto and a 6.78-inch screen, and the vivo X300 is its successor.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the MediaTek Dimensity 9400 (3 nm) with one 3.626GHz Cortex-X925 core, three 3.3GHz Cortex-X4 cores and four 2.4GHz Cortex-A720 cores, plus an Immortalis-G925 GPU. vivo lists LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 4.0 storage, with 12GB + 256GB and 16GB + 512GB versions and no expandable storage.

SpecHub has not run benchmarks or thermal tests, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.67-inch 1.5K OLED (1260 x 2800 pixels) with a 120Hz refresh rate and a quad-curved design, down in size from the 6.78-inch screen of the previous generation. vivo lists a local peak brightness of 4,500 nits, which is a listed figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

The 32MP front camera and an optical in-display fingerprint sensor complete the front hardware.

Camera Experience

The rear system has three 50MP cameras co-engineered with ZEISS: a main (1/1.56-inch, optical stabilisation), a 3x telephoto (1/1.95-inch, f/2.57, optical stabilisation, telemacro) and an ultrawide. Sensor model names differ between sources and are not listed.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality, so no image-quality claim is made.

Battery and Charging

The global X200 has a 5,800mAh battery and vivo lists 90W wired charging. An Austria-only model has a 5,220mAh battery. No wireless charging is listed. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 15 and Funtouch OS 15. The listed update promise is 4 years of OS updates and 5 years of security updates. It is rated IP68 and IP69 (the IP69 rating depends on the market) and is 7.99 mm thick.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a compact, light camera flagship with a flagship chipset, 90W charging and strong water resistance will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a periscope telephoto and wireless charging should look at the vivo X200 Pro, and buyers who want a newer model should look at the vivo X300. Buyers in Austria should check the battery size.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the X200 is a compact, lightweight flagship with a large battery, fast wired charging, a 3x telephoto and a high water-resistance rating, without wireless charging. It suits people who want a smaller camera phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chipset does the vivo X200 use?
It uses the MediaTek Dimensity 9400 (3 nm) with an Immortalis-G925 GPU.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The global model has a 5,800mAh battery with 90W wired charging. A 5,220mAh version is sold only in Austria.
Is the vivo X200 waterproof?
It is rated IP68 and IP69 (IP69 is market dependent), immersible up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes. Water resistance is not permanent.
What cameras does it have?
A 50MP main, a 50MP ultrawide and a 50MP 3x telephoto, plus a 32MP front camera, co-engineered with ZEISS.
Does it have wireless charging?
No wireless charging is listed.
What RAM and storage options are there?
12GB + 256GB and 16GB + 512GB, with LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 4.0 storage. Expandable storage is not supported.
How much did the vivo X200 cost at launch?
It started at ₹65,999 in India, RM 3,599 in Malaysia and BDT 139,999 + VAT for 16GB + 512GB in Bangladesh. Prices vary by country, retailer and storage size, so check a local seller.
What is the difference between the X200 and X200 Pro?
The X200 Pro has a 6.78-inch display, a 6,000mAh global battery, 30W wireless charging and a 200MP periscope telephoto. The X200 has a 6.67-inch display, a 5,800mAh battery and a 50MP 3x telephoto.
