Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Note 60 Ultra is the top model of the Note 60 line, announced on March 4, 2026. It combines a Dimensity 8400 Ultimate chip, a 200MP main camera, a 7000 mAh battery and 100W charging. It is aimed at buyers who want a performance and camera phone with a very large battery at a mid-premium price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Dimensity 8400 Ultimate (4 nm). RAM and storage options were not verified for this page, so they are not listed. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The AMOLED display runs at 144Hz and is rated for up to 4,500 nits of peak brightness. The screen size and resolution could not be verified for this page, so they are not listed.

Camera Experience

The rear system includes a 200MP main camera and a periscope telephoto lens. Lens details and the front camera could not be verified, so they are not listed. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 7000 mAh (silicon-carbon) with 100W wired charging, which Infinix says reaches 50% in about 18 minutes and 100% in about 48 minutes. Wireless charging is supported, but its wattage was not verified. We have no charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone supports 5G and weighs 220 g. The Android version and other software details were not verified for this page.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a large battery, fast charging and a high-resolution camera in a mid-premium phone will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a lighter phone or a lower price should look at the Note 60 or Note 60 Pro.

Editorial Verdict

The Note 60 Ultra packs a big battery, very fast charging and a strong chip. Check Infinix's official specifications for the display size, memory and software before buying.
