Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Note 40 Pro (4G) is the middle model of the Note 40 family, announced on March 19, 2024. It adds a curved screen, 12GB of RAM and much faster wired charging over the Note 40. It is aimed at buyers who want quick charging and a premium-feeling display at a low price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate (6 nm) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. The separate Note 40 Pro 5G uses a Dimensity 7020. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.78-inch curved AMOLED display has a 120Hz refresh rate. Curved edges give a more premium look but can make the screen more prone to accidental touches.

Camera Experience

The rear camera is a 108MP main sensor with optical stabilisation, and the front camera is 32MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and secondary lenses and video modes are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5000 mAh with 70W wired charging and 20W MagCharge wireless charging, compared with 45W wired on the Note 40. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and XOS 14 and supports 4G only in this version. It weighs 190 g and is 7.8 mm thick.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want fast charging, a curved AMOLED screen and plenty of RAM on a budget will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want 5G should look at the Note 40 Pro 5G or Note 40 Pro+. Those who want the lowest price may prefer the Note 40.

Editorial Verdict

The Note 40 Pro (4G) pairs very fast wired charging with a premium-feeling screen at a modest price. It lacks 5G and uses a mid-range chip.
