Expert Review

The Redmi Note 15 Pro is a 4G mid-range smartphone focused on display quality, camera resolution, durability and battery life. It features a 6.77-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and up to 3200 nits peak brightness. The Helio G200-Ultra powers the device, while its camera setup combines a 200MP OIS main camera with an 8MP ultrawide camera and a 32MP selfie camera. Its 6500mAh silicon-carbon battery supports 45W Turbo Charging and up to 18W wired reverse charging. The phone also supports microSD expansion up to 2TB.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Redmi Note 15 Pro?
The Redmi Note 15 Pro is priced at $299, depending on the RAM and storage configuration chosen.
Does the Redmi Note 15 Pro support 5G?
No, the Redmi Note 15 Pro is a 4G-only device and does not support 5G connectivity.
What chipset does the Redmi Note 15 Pro use?
The Redmi Note 15 Pro is powered by a MediaTek Helio G200-Ultra chipset built on a 6nm process, paired with a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU.
Does the Redmi Note 15 Pro have a telephoto camera?
No, the Redmi Note 15 Pro does not have a dedicated telephoto camera. Its rear setup consists of a 200MP OIS main camera and an 8MP ultrawide camera.
Does the Redmi Note 15 Pro support microSD storage expansion?
Yes, the Redmi Note 15 Pro supports microSD card expansion up to 2TB via its hybrid SIM slot.
Does the Redmi Note 15 Pro support wireless charging?
No, the Redmi Note 15 Pro does not support wireless charging, but it does offer up to 18W wired reverse charging.
