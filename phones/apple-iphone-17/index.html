Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 17 is the standard model of Apple's 2025 lineup. It moves up to a 6.3-inch display with a faster refresh rate, a second 48MP rear camera and an 18MP front camera, and it starts at 256GB of storage. It is aimed at buyers who want the main iPhone experience with fewer compromises than the 16.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Apple A19 with 8GB of RAM reported by independent sources. Storage options are 256GB and 512GB with no card slot. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone, and the CPU and GPU core counts are not listed because they were not verified.

Display Experience

The 6.3-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display has 1206 x 2622 pixels and supports ProMotion up to 120Hz, which the standard iPhone 16 does not offer. It is protected by Ceramic Shield 2 and keeps the Dynamic Island.

Camera Experience

Both rear cameras are 48MP: a main camera and an ultrawide. There is no telephoto lens. The front camera is an 18MP Center Stage unit. SpecHub has not tested image quality, and lens details beyond resolution are omitted because they were not verified.

Battery and Charging

Independent sources report a 3692 mAh battery, compared with 3561 mAh for the iPhone 16. Apple does not publish this figure. Charging speeds are not listed because they could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with iOS 26 and supports Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, NFC and USB-C. It has Face ID, the Action button and Camera Control.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People upgrading from an iPhone 15 or older who want a smoother screen, more storage and a better front camera without paying Pro prices will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a telephoto lens should consider an iPhone 17 Pro. Those who want a lower price may prefer the iPhone 17e or an older model.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 17 gives the standard iPhone a 120Hz display, two 48MP rear cameras and 256GB of starting storage. Its limits are the lack of a telephoto lens and unpublished battery and RAM figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the iPhone 17 have a 120Hz display?
Yes. It supports ProMotion up to 120Hz, which is new for the standard iPhone.
How much storage does the iPhone 17 start with?
Storage starts at 256GB, with a 512GB option.
What is the battery capacity of the iPhone 17?
Apple does not publish battery capacity. Independent sources report 3692 mAh.
How much RAM does the iPhone 17 have?
Apple does not publish RAM. Independent sources report 8GB.
Does the iPhone 17 have a telephoto camera?
No. It has a 48MP main and a 48MP ultrawide camera. A telephoto lens is available on the Pro models.
How much did the iPhone 17 cost at launch?
In the US, launch prices were $799 for 256GB and $999 for 512GB. Prices elsewhere and current retail prices differ.
