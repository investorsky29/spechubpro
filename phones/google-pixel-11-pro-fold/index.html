Expert Review

The Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold is Google's most advanced foldable smartphone for 2026. It features a thinner and lighter design than its predecessor, a large 8.0-inch inner OLED display, a 6.5-inch cover screen, and both displays reaching up to 3600 nits of peak brightness. Powered by the Tensor G6 chipset and 16GB of RAM, it is designed for Gemini Intelligence, advanced multitasking, and AI-powered photography. The phone also introduces a new 48MP main camera, up to 30x Super Zoom, IP68 protection, faster 30W wired charging, and 25W Qi2.2 wireless charging. With Android 17 and seven years of software support, it is one of the most capable foldable phones in Google's lineup.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold?
The Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold is priced at $1,899. Prices may vary by region and retailer.
What chipset does the Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold use?
The Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold is powered by the Google Tensor G6 chipset, paired with 16GB of RAM, the most RAM of any Pixel in the 2026 lineup.
How big is the Pixel 11 Pro Fold's battery?
The Pixel 11 Pro Fold has a 4750 mAh battery, which is smaller than the previous generation's Pixel 10 Pro Fold, though it charges faster at 30W wired and 25W wireless.
When was the Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold released?
The Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold was released on August 20, 2026, alongside the rest of the Pixel 11 lineup.
Is the Pixel 11 Pro Fold water and dust resistant?
Yes, the Pixel 11 Pro Fold carries an IP68 rating, a meaningful durability upgrade that gives it dust and water resistance comparable to a standard, non-folding flagship phone.
How big are the Pixel 11 Pro Fold's displays?
The Pixel 11 Pro Fold has an 8.0-inch inner display for use when unfolded, and a 6.5-inch cover display for quick tasks without opening the phone, both reaching up to 3600 nits of peak brightness.
