Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold is Google's third book-style foldable, announced on August 12, 2026. It has an 8-inch inner display and a 6.4-inch cover display, uses the Tensor G6 chip and weighs 239 g, lighter than the 257 g Pixel 9 Pro Fold. It is aimed at buyers who want a tablet-size screen that folds into a phone.

Performance and Processor

It uses the Google Tensor G6 (2 nm, as reported) with the Titan M3 security chip and 16GB of RAM. The configuration sold in India is 512GB of storage, and other markets may differ. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The inner display is listed at 8 inches and the cover display at 6.4 inches. Resolution, refresh range and brightness could not be verified from the sources we checked, so they are not listed.

Camera Experience

Camera hardware could not be verified from the sources we checked, so no lens details are listed. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Pre-launch spec sheets report 4806 mAh typical, with a minimum rated capacity of 4658 mAh. Charging speeds could not be verified. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone has the Titan M3 security chip and comes in Obsidian and Olive. The Android version, water-resistance rating and update policy were not verified for this page.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a lighter large-screen foldable from Google will find it a fit once its full specifications are confirmed.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a lower price or a non-folding phone should look at the Pixel 11 Pro or Pro XL.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold brings the Tensor G6 and a lighter body to Google's foldable line. Check Google's official sheet for display, camera, charging and durability details before buying.
