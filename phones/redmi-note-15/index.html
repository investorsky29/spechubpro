Expert Review

The Redmi Note 15 is a 4G mid-range smartphone focused on display quality, battery life and everyday performance. It features a 6.77-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and up to 3200 nits peak brightness. The Helio G100-Ultra chipset powers the phone, while its camera system combines a 108MP main camera with a 2MP depth camera and a 20MP selfie camera. A 6000mAh battery supports 33W Turbo Charging, while the hybrid SIM slot allows microSD expansion up to 2TB.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the Redmi Note 15?
The Redmi Note 15 is priced at ₦279,900, depending on the RAM and storage configuration chosen.
Does the Redmi Note 15 support 5G?
No, the Redmi Note 15 is a 4G-only device and does not support 5G connectivity.
What chipset does the Redmi Note 15 use?
The Redmi Note 15 is powered by a MediaTek Helio G100-Ultra chipset built on a 6nm process.
Does the Redmi Note 15 have an ultrawide camera?
No, the Redmi Note 15 does not have an ultrawide camera. Its rear setup consists of a 108MP main camera and a 2MP depth camera.
Does the Redmi Note 15 support microSD storage expansion?
Yes, the Redmi Note 15 supports microSD card expansion up to 2TB via its hybrid SIM slot.
Is the Redmi Note 15 water resistant?
The Redmi Note 15 carries an IP64 rating, offering basic protection against dust and light splashes of water.
