Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The vivo X200 Ultra was announced in China on April 21, 2025 and released on April 29, 2025. It is vivo's top camera phone of the X200 generation, with a Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, a 6.82-inch 2K LTPO AMOLED display and a 200MP periscope telephoto, and it launched together with the X200s.

Unlike the standard X200 and X200 Pro, the X200 Ultra was not part of the global launch and is expected to stay exclusive to China. International buyers who want vivo's camera flagship from this generation should look at the X200 Pro, and the later X300 Pro.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite (SM8750-AB, 3 nm), unlike the Dimensity chips in the X200 and X200 Pro. Memory is 12GB or 16GB of LPDDR5X RAM with 256GB, 512GB or 1TB of storage and no card slot. The storage standard was not confirmed and is not listed.

Two imaging chips support the camera pipeline. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.82-inch LTPO AMOLED at 1440 x 3168 pixels (2K) with a 120Hz refresh rate, Dolby Vision and HDR Vivid. A peak brightness of 4,500 nits is advertised, which is a stated figure and not a SpecHub measurement.

The front glass is described as armor glass, and the phone is rated IP68 and IP69, so it is dust tight and can be immersed up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes. Water resistance is not permanent.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main with a 35mm-equivalent lens (f/1.7, 1/1.28-inch sensor, gimbal-style stabilisation), a 50MP 14mm ultrawide and a 200MP periscope telephoto (85mm, 1/1.4-inch, 3.7x optical zoom, macro 3.4:1), all co-engineered with ZEISS.

The 35mm main lens is a longer focal length than most phones' main cameras. Sources disagree on the telephoto aperture, so it is not listed, and the selfie camera was not confirmed. SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6,000mAh with 90W wired and 40W wireless charging. These are stated figures and not SpecHub tests. SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone runs Android 15 with OriginOS 5, the China version of vivo's software. It has dual Nano-SIM slots. The fingerprint sensor type and port specification were not confirmed and are not listed. Bands are listed for China and nearby markets, so check compatibility before importing it. A software-update period was not confirmed and is not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers in China, or importers comfortable with a China-market phone, who want a large-screen camera flagship with a 200MP telephoto and a big battery will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers outside China who want a local warranty, global software and clear update support should look at the vivo X200 Pro or X300 Pro. Buyers who want a smaller phone should look at the vivo X200 or X300.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the X200 Ultra is a camera-led flagship with a Snapdragon chip, a 2K display, a 200MP telephoto and a large battery, limited mainly by being a China-only model. It suits users who prioritise camera hardware and can accept the import trade-offs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the vivo X200 Ultra sold globally?
No. It launched in China and is expected to stay exclusive to China. The standard X200 and X200 Pro were the models that launched globally.
Which chipset does the vivo X200 Ultra use?
The Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite (SM8750-AB, 3 nm). The X200 and X200 Pro use MediaTek Dimensity 9400 chips.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 6,000mAh. Charging is 90W wired and 40W wireless.
What cameras does it have?
A 50MP 35mm main camera, a 50MP 14mm ultrawide and a 200MP periscope telephoto with 3.7x optical zoom, co-engineered with ZEISS.
Is the vivo X200 Ultra waterproof?
It is rated IP68 and IP69. Water resistance is not permanent, so avoid unnecessary exposure.
How much does the vivo X200 Ultra cost?
In China launch pricing was reported from about ¥6,499, and an import estimate of about €780 has been published. Prices vary by seller and region, and there is no official global price.
What RAM and storage options are there?
12GB or 16GB of RAM with 256GB, 512GB or 1TB of storage. There is no memory card slot.
What software does it run?
Android 15 with OriginOS 5, the China version of vivo's software.
