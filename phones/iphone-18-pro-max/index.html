Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The iPhone 18 Pro Max is the largest model of Apple's 2026 Pro line, announced on September 9, 2026 alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and released on September 18. Apple's announcement highlights the A20 Pro chip on a 2 nm process, a 48MP Fusion camera with variable aperture and vapor chamber cooling. This page lists only items confirmed by Apple, so it is shorter than the pages for older iPhones.

Performance and Processor

The Apple A20 Pro is built on a 2 nm process, and Apple's announcement mentions a vapor chamber for cooling. Apple does not publish RAM, and the storage options were not confirmed in the sources we checked. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

Apple's launch announcement did not state the display size or resolution for the iPhone 18 Pro Max, so no display figures are listed here.

Camera Experience

Apple's announcement lists a 48MP Fusion camera with variable aperture, which lets the lens change its opening. Other lenses and front-camera details were not confirmed in the sources we checked, so they are omitted. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

Apple does not publish battery capacity or charging speeds in the announcement, and figures reported elsewhere were inconsistent, so none are listed. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone runs iOS 27. Water resistance, connectivity versions and colours were not verified for this page.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want the newest large Pro iPhone and the A20 Pro chip will find it a fit once the missing specifications are published.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a smaller phone should look at the iPhone 18 Pro. Those who want a lower price may prefer an older Pro model.

Editorial Verdict

The iPhone 18 Pro Max brings a 2 nm chip, a variable-aperture main camera and vapor cooling to the largest Pro. Check Apple's official specifications for display, battery and weight before buying.
