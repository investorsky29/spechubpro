What We Know So Far

The iPhone 18 Pro Max has not been announced by Apple, so there is no hands-on review to give you yet. Based on current supply chain reports and analyst notes, it is expected to launch in September 2026 alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and Apple's rumored first foldable iPhone, while the standard iPhone 18 reportedly waits until spring 2027. The most consistently repeated leak is a new A20 Pro chip built on a 2nm process, expected to bring a meaningful efficiency jump over the current A19 Pro. Camera rumors point to a variable aperture main lens, a first for any iPhone, though a competing claim about a 200MP Samsung-made sensor is less well corroborated. Battery capacity is rumored to grow modestly past 5,000mAh, and the phone is expected to ship with iOS 27, with a sharper focus on Apple Intelligence and a more capable Siri. None of this is official. We'll rewrite this page with confirmed specifications, real pricing, and our own hands-on review once Apple unveils the device.
