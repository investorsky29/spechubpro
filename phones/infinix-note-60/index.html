Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Infinix Note 60 is a mid-range 5G phone released in February 2026. It pairs a large 6500 mAh battery with wireless charging in a 7.5 mm body. It is aimed at buyers who want long battery life and modern charging features at a mid-range price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the MediaTek Dimensity 7400 with 8GB of RAM. Storage options and the Android version were not verified for this page, so they are not listed. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The display resolution is listed as 1208 x 2644 pixels, with an in-screen fingerprint sensor. Screen size, panel type and refresh rate could not be verified, so they are not listed.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main (f/1.6) and an 8MP ultrawide (f/2.2), and the front camera is 13MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 6500 mAh with 45W wired charging, 30W wireless charging and reverse charging. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone supports 5G. Software details were not verified for this page.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a long-lasting phone with wireless charging in a slim body will find it a good fit.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want a faster chip, a larger battery or a telephoto camera should look at the Note 60 Ultra.

Editorial Verdict

The Note 60 offers a large battery, wireless charging and a slim body at a mid-range price. Check Infinix's official specifications for display and software details before buying.
