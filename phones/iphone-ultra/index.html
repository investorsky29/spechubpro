What We Know So Far

The Apple iPhone Ultra is the rumored name for Apple's first foldable iPhone, which is expected to be introduced alongside the iPhone 18 Pro lineup. Reports suggest a book-style folding design with a 5.5-inch outer display and a 7.8-inch inner OLED display supporting up to 120Hz ProMotion. Other rumored features include an A20 Pro chip, 12GB of RAM, up to 2TB of storage, Touch ID, and dual 48MP rear cameras. Apple has not officially confirmed the device, its final name, specifications, price, or release date.

This page will be updated with confirmed specs once Apple officially announces a foldable iPhone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Has Apple announced a foldable iPhone?
No. Apple has not officially announced a foldable iPhone. "iPhone Ultra" is a rumored name based on leaks and supply-chain reports, and nothing about the device is confirmed.
What chip will the iPhone Ultra use?
Leaks suggest the iPhone Ultra will use a next-generation Apple A20 Pro chip, though this has not been officially confirmed by Apple.
How much will the iPhone Ultra cost?
Apple has not announced pricing for a foldable iPhone. Given the premium materials and folding mechanism involved, reports expect a very high price, though no specific figure has been confirmed.
How big will the iPhone Ultra's displays be?
Rumors point to a 5.5-inch outer display and a 7.8-inch inner foldable OLED display supporting up to 120Hz ProMotion, though Apple has not confirmed any display specifications.
Will the iPhone Ultra use Face ID or Touch ID?
Reports suggest Apple may use Touch ID rather than Face ID on its first foldable iPhone, likely due to the folding hinge design, though this has not been officially confirmed.
When might the iPhone Ultra be released?
Reports suggest a foldable iPhone could launch alongside the iPhone 18 Pro lineup, but Apple has not confirmed a release date, and timing could still change.
