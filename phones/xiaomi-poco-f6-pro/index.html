Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Xiaomi POCO F6 Pro was announced and released on May 23, 2024. It is the higher-end model in POCO's F6 line, pairing a 6.67-inch 1440 x 3200 AMOLED display with the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset, a 5,000mAh battery and 120W charging.

It stays true to POCO's value-for-money approach and is positioned as the "flagship" of the lineup. It has a triple rear camera led by a 50MP main sensor.

Performance and Processor

The chipset is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 (4 nm) with an Adreno 740 GPU. RAM is 12GB or 16GB (LPDDR5X per two sources), with 256GB, 512GB or 1TB of storage and no card slot. Sources disagree on the exact chip model code, so none is shown. SpecHub has not run benchmarks, so no performance claim is made here.

Display Experience

The display is a 6.67-inch AMOLED at 1440 x 3200 pixels (about 526 pixels per inch) with a 120Hz refresh rate, 3840Hz PWM dimming, Dolby Vision and HDR10+. Brightness is listed at 700 nits typical, 1,200 nits in high-brightness mode and 4,000 nits peak, which are stated figures and not SpecHub measurements.

Protection is Corning Gorilla Glass 5. The front camera is 16MP.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main (f/1.6, optical stabilisation), an 8MP ultrawide and a 2MP macro. There is no telephoto lens. Other camera details, such as video modes, were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

SpecHub has not tested photo or video quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5,000mAh with 120W wired charging (PD3.0, QC3+), which is listed as reaching 100% in 19 minutes. That is a stated figure and not a SpecHub test. No wireless charging is listed.

SpecHub has not run battery-life tests, so no endurance claim is made here.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and HyperOS, and up to 3 major Android upgrades are listed. It has IP54 dust and water-splash resistance, an under-display fingerprint sensor and an infrared port. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth versions were not confirmed from more than one source and are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a very fast charging phone with a bright high-resolution AMOLED screen and a flagship-era chipset from a value brand will find the most to like.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a newer chipset, wireless charging, full water resistance or a telephoto camera should look at a later POCO model or a more expensive flagship. Buyers outside POCO's global markets should check availability first.

Editorial Verdict

On paper, the POCO F6 Pro offers a high-end screen, 120W charging and a capable chipset in a value-focused package, with an older chipset generation and splash-only protection as its main trade-offs. It suits buyers who prioritise screen and charging speed over camera versatility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chipset does the POCO F6 Pro use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 (4 nm) with an Adreno 740 GPU.
How big is the battery and how fast does it charge?
The battery is 5,000mAh and wired charging is 120W, which is listed as 100% in 19 minutes. No wireless charging is listed.
Is the POCO F6 Pro waterproof?
It is rated IP54, which means dust protected and splash resistant. It is not designed to be submerged.

It comes with 12GB RAM and 256GB or 512GB, or 16GB RAM and 1TB of storage. There is no memory card slot.

Yes. Two sources list an infrared port.
