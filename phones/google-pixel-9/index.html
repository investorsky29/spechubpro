Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Pixel 9 is the standard model of Google's 2024 line, announced on August 13, 2024 and released on August 22 at $799 in the US, $100 more than the Pixel 8. It has a redesigned body, a 6.3-inch display and 12GB of RAM. It suits buyers who want a current Pixel without paying for a Pro.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Google Tensor G4 with the Titan M2 security chip, 12GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage. Google promises seven years of OS and security updates. SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.3-inch Actua OLED display has 1080 x 2424 pixels and a refresh rate of 60-120Hz, with a peak brightness of up to 2,700 nits. The Pixel 9 Pro has the same screen size but a higher-resolution LTPO panel with a peak of 3,000 nits.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 50MP main and a 48MP ultrawide, and the front camera is 10.5MP. There is no telephoto lens, which the Pro models add. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 4700 mAh with 27W wired charging and 15W wireless charging, plus Battery Share. We have no endurance tests.

Software and Features

The phone launched with Android 14 and has Gorilla Glass Victus 2 and IP68 resistance.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

People who want a current, compact Pixel with long update support and a bright display will find it a good match.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

Buyers who want a telephoto camera, 16GB of RAM or faster wireless charging should look at the Pixel 9 Pro. Those who want a lower price may prefer the Pixel 9a.

Editorial Verdict

The Pixel 9 is a well-specified standard Pixel with a bright display and long support. Its base storage and lack of a telephoto lens are its main limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Pixel 9 and the Pixel 9 Pro?
Both have a 6.3-inch display and a 4700 mAh battery. The Pixel 9 has 12GB of RAM, a 1080 x 2424 display with 2,700 nits peak and 15W wireless charging. The Pixel 9 Pro has 16GB of RAM, a 1280 x 2856 display with 3,000 nits peak, 21W wireless charging and a telephoto camera.
How long does the Pixel 9 get updates?
Google promises seven years of OS and security updates.
How fast does the Pixel 9 charge?
Wired charging is 27W and wireless charging is 15W, with Battery Share reverse wireless charging.
Is the Pixel 9 water resistant?
Yes. It is rated IP68.
How much RAM and storage does it have?
It has 12GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage.
How much did the Pixel 9 cost at launch?
In the US, the 128GB model launched at $799. Prices elsewhere differ, and current prices vary by retailer.
