Editorial Overview

This is an editorial analysis of the specifications listed on this page. It is not based on SpecHub lab testing, so it makes no claims about benchmarks, battery life or camera results. Manufacturer lab figures are not reproduced here.

Phone Overview

The Honor X9b is a mid-range phone launched in February 2024, with an India launch on February 16. Its Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chip carried over to the X9c. It suits buyers who want a curved AMOLED screen and a big battery at a modest price.

Performance and Processor

The phone uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 (4 nm) with an octa-core CPU at up to 2.2 GHz. RAM is 8GB plus 8GB of extended RAM, and storage is 256GB (UFS 3.1). SpecHub has not benchmarked the phone.

Display Experience

The 6.78-inch curved AMOLED display has 1220 x 2652 pixels (about 431 ppi) and a 120Hz refresh rate, with a peak brightness of 1,200 nits. The Honor X9c raised the peak brightness rating to 4,000 nits.

Camera Experience

The rear cameras are a 108MP main, a 5MP ultrawide and a 2MP macro camera. The front camera is 16MP. SpecHub has not tested image quality.

Battery and Charging

The battery is 5800 mAh with 35W wired charging. The X9c raised these figures to 6600 mAh and 66W. Wireless charging is not supported. We have no endurance or charge-time tests.

Software and Features

The phone has a single speaker and dual Nano-SIM support. The operating system version and manufacturer interface at launch could not be verified for this page, so they are not listed.

Who Should Consider This Phone?

Buyers who want a large curved AMOLED screen and a big battery at a low price will find it appealing.

Who May Prefer Another Phone?

People who want faster charging, a brighter display or stereo speakers should look at the Honor X9c.

Editorial Verdict

The Honor X9b is a solid mid-range phone for battery and display, and it has since been surpassed by the X9c on charging speed, brightness and speakers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Honor X9b and the Honor X9c?
The X9b has a 5800 mAh battery, 35W charging, a 1,200 nits display and a single speaker. The X9c has a 6600 mAh battery, 66W charging, a 4,000 nits display and stereo speakers.
What is the Honor X9b's battery capacity?
It has a 5800 mAh battery with 35W wired charging.
Which chip does the Honor X9b use?
It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 1.
Does it support wireless charging?
No. Wireless charging is not supported.
How many cameras does it have on the back?
It has three: a 108MP main, a 5MP ultrawide and a 2MP macro camera.
How much did the Honor X9b cost at launch?
Prices vary by country. In the Philippines it launched at ₱16,999.
